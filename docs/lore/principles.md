# Established Worldbuilding Principles

These principles govern future lore development.

1. **Wonder Kingdom is genuinely united.** Its different communities are not secretly hostile toward one another.
2. **The four original peoples were historically divided.** Their conflict ended through cooperation against the Balrog and the subsequent founding treaty.
3. **Cultural differences remain important.** Unification did not erase the distinct traditions or identities of Veligor's communities.
4. **Urbanograd was founded 100 years ago.** Its inhabitants are societal rebels in a cultural sense, not political insurgents.
5. **Bain Harbour and Goa Harbour are independent towns.** Their resemblance to other settlements is cultural and atmospheric, not an indication of administrative dependence.
6. **Bain Harbour and Goa Harbour are on opposite sides of Veligor.** They have different identities without being rivals.
7. **Selsior is the City of Everything.** It is a joyful, bustling, cosmopolitan metropolis with classic medieval fantasy architecture.
8. **Kaldemor is the Town of Joyful Craftsmanship.** It is a cheerful, prosperous, pastoral community inspired by Henesys.
9. **Midbarium is a rugged highland settlement.** Its identity is associated with strength, resilience, independence, and ancestral traditions.
10. **Fairy Forest is a magical woodland settlement.** It is associated with the Magicians and became the new home of the Children of the Tree.
11. **Cavelor was established by ancient guardians.** Its inhabitants preserve the responsibility of watching over the Balrog's prison.
12. **The Great World Tree Expedition occurred 50 years ago.** It discovered the Children of the Tree and Cavelor but accidentally damaged the Balrog's seal.
13. **The Balrog's weakening seal makes the wilderness increasingly dangerous.** This is the central lore explanation for the aggressive monsters encountered throughout Veligor.
14. **The kingdom remains peaceful despite the monster threat.** The danger affects the land and wilderness rather than causing widespread hostility between its peoples.
15. **The player belongs to Wonder Kingdom from the beginning.** The player character was born and raised within its society.
16. **The Balrog is the overarching ancient threat.** Its exact nature, intentions, and eventual fate have not yet been fully established.
17. **The world is predominantly bright, colorful, prosperous, and adventurous.** Dark environments and dangerous creatures exist, but they do not define the everyday atmosphere of the entire kingdom.
18. **Not every local story must concern the Balrog.** The world contains ordinary lives, personal adventures, regional traditions, and independent mysteries.
19. **Veligor is the initial setting, not necessarily the entire world.** Other islands and kingdoms may be introduced in the future.
20. **Undecided details must remain undecided.** Future lore development should distinguish established facts from proposals rather than silently introducing new canon.
