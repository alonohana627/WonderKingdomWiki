# Worldbuilding Principles

These principles guide all future lore. New pages and stories should stay true to them.

1. **Wonder Kingdom is genuinely united.** Its communities are not secretly hostile toward one another.
2. **The four original peoples were once divided.** Their conflict ended through cooperation against the Balrog and the founding treaty that followed.
3. **Cultural differences still matter.** Unity did not erase the traditions or identities of Veligor's communities.
4. **Urbanograd was founded 100 years ago.** Its people are rebels in a cultural sense, not political insurgents.
5. **Bain Harbour and Goa Harbour are independent towns.** Their resemblance to other towns is cultural and atmospheric, not a sign that anyone governs them.
6. **Bain Harbour and Goa Harbour lie on opposite sides of Veligor.** They have different identities without being rivals.
7. **Selsior is the City of Everything.** It is a joyful, bustling, cosmopolitan metropolis with classic medieval fantasy architecture.
8. **Kaldemor is the Town of Joyful Craftsmanship.** It is a cheerful, prosperous, pastoral community inspired by Henesys.
9. **Midbarium is a rugged highland town.** It stands for strength, resilience, independence and ancestral tradition.
10. **Fairy Forest is a magical woodland town.** It is the Magicians' historic home and the new home of the Children of the Tree.
11. **Cavelor was founded by ancient guardians.** Its people still carry the duty of watching over the Balrog's prison.
12. **The Great World Tree Expedition took place 50 years ago.** It found the Children of the Tree and Cavelor, and accidentally damaged the Balrog's seal.
13. **The weakening seal makes the wilderness ever more dangerous.** This is the core explanation for the aggressive monsters across Veligor.
14. **The kingdom stays peaceful despite the monsters.** The danger is in the land and the wilds, not between its peoples.
15. **The player belongs to Wonder Kingdom from the start.** The player character was born and raised here.
16. **The Balrog is the overarching ancient threat.** Its true nature, intentions and fate are not yet fully decided.
17. **The world is mostly bright, colorful, prosperous and adventurous.** Dark places and dangerous creatures exist, but they do not define everyday life in the kingdom.
18. **Not every story is about the Balrog.** The world has ordinary lives, personal adventures, local traditions and mysteries of its own.
19. **Veligor is the starting point, not necessarily the whole world.** Other islands and kingdoms may come later.
20. **Undecided details stay undecided.** New lore should be clear about what is established and what is only a proposal, rather than quietly adding new canon. On these pages, open questions are kept in **Design notes** boxes.
