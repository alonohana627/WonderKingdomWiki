# Lore

Wonder Kingdom is a bright, prosperous and genuinely united kingdom on the island of **Veligor**. Its people come from different cultures and keep different traditions, yet they share a real sense of belonging and respect for one another. Their towns are lively, their markets are full, and their differences are celebrated rather than feared.

It was not always so. Before the kingdom, Veligor was split between four peoples who had fought each other for generations. Only the coming of **the Balrog**, an ancient being too strong for any one of them, drove them to stand together. They beat it, sealed it beneath the roots of the great World Tree, and founded Wonder Kingdom on the cooperation that won that war.

Fifty years ago, an expedition into the World Tree opened a rift deep beneath its roots and damaged the seal. The Balrog has not escaped, but its influence has been seeping out ever since. Wild creatures have grown aggressive, and the wilderness between the towns has filled with monsters.

The kingdom is still whole and happy. The land around it is turning hostile. That contrast is the heart of the world.

!!! quote "Core premise"
    Wonder Kingdom is a genuinely happy and united civilization, born from the cooperation of four formerly divided peoples. Generations later, an ancient threat imprisoned beneath the World Tree is slowly returning, and a new generation must protect the peaceful world its ancestors created.
