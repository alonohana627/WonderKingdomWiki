# Historical Timeline

| Period | Historical event |
|---|---|
| Ancient era | The four peoples develop distinct civilizations throughout Veligor. |
| Age of Division | The Savages and Magicians fight against the Commons and Aristocrats. |
| The Balrog War | The Balrog attacks Veligor, forcing the four peoples to unite. |
| Founding of Wonder Kingdom | The Balrog is defeated and sealed beneath the World Tree. The founding treaty establishes Wonder Kingdom. |
| Age of Unity | The kingdom prospers, settlements expand, and the old divisions disappear. |
| 100 years ago | Urbanograd is founded and develops its distinctive countercultural identity. |
| 50 years ago | The Great World Tree Expedition discovers the Children of the Tree and Cavelor. |
| 50 years ago | The expedition accidentally opens a rift that damages the Balrog's ancient seal. |
| Following decades | The Balrog's influence gradually spreads, making the wilderness increasingly dangerous. |
| Present day | Wonder Kingdom remains united and prosperous while aggressive monsters occupy much of Veligor's wilderness. The Balrog's prison continues weakening. |
