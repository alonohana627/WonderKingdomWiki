# The Balrog

The Balrog is the greatest threat Wonder Kingdom has ever faced. It is an ancient being of immense power and destruction, which once nearly destroyed every people on Veligor, and which now lies imprisoned beneath the [World Tree](world-tree.md), held by a seal that is slowly failing.

To most people today, it is a legend: a monster from history books and bedtime stories. To the guardians of [Cavelor](towns/cavelor.md), it is the reason their city exists.

## What It Is

No one alive has seen the Balrog, and the old accounts disagree about almost everything. They agree on a few things:

- It is **enormous**, larger than any creature on Veligor, and it moved through the land like a storm.
- It **burned**. Settlements it passed were left as ash, and the ground where it stood stayed scorched for years.
- It **could not be killed**. The four peoples' greatest warriors wounded it again and again, and every wound closed.
- Its **influence spreads**. Even before it reached a place, the creatures there grew wild and vicious, as if something in them had answered it.

Beyond that, every people tells it differently. Savage songs describe a horned giant wreathed in flame. Magician records describe it as something less like an animal and more like a wound in the world, a hunger given shape. Commons tales call it simply "the Burning". Aristocrat chronicles, which care about numbers, record the armies and towns it destroyed, and say little about what it was.

## Where It Came From

The oldest question about the Balrog has never been answered. The four peoples each have a story:

- **The Savages** say it came out of the deep earth, woken from a sleep older than the island by the noise of their endless wars.
- **The Magicians** believe it came from somewhere else entirely, through a tear in the world, drawn by the World Tree's magic the way moths are drawn to a lantern.
- **The Commons** say it was always there, under the island, and that the island's warmth and fertility are what is left over from its fire.
- **The Aristocrats** recorded only that it appeared in the far wilds and moved toward the center of Veligor, toward the World Tree, from the very first day.

No one knows which, if any, is true. Cavelor's scholars say that each story might hold a piece of the truth.

## The Balrog War

### The First Burning

The Balrog first appeared in the wilds at the edge of Veligor, while the Age of Division still raged. Its first victims were frontier villages of both alliances. Each side blamed the other at first, and wasted precious time accusing each other of some terrible new weapon.

By the time they understood, it was moving inland. Armies sent against it were scattered. Strongholds that had held for generations against human enemies burned in a night. Refugees from all four peoples fled toward the center of the island, and the Balrog followed, always moving toward the World Tree.

### The Alliance

With the Balrog closing on the heart of the island, the four peoples finally met under truce. It was the first time in generations that their leaders had sat at one table. The talks were bitter and nearly failed more than once, but the refugee camps filling the plains made the choice for them. They united.

Each people gave what it did best. The Savages sent their strongest warriors. The Magicians brought their knowledge of ancient magic. The Commons supplied, armed and fed the united army. The Aristocrats organized it and commanded it as one.

### The Last Stand at the Tree

The final battle was fought at the foot of the World Tree. The united army could not kill the Balrog, but it could hold it and wear it down, and that was the plan: while the warriors fought, the Magicians worked a seal powerful enough to bind it forever.

The battle lasted for days. The Savage front line held the Balrog in place, at terrible cost. When the creature finally weakened enough, the Magicians drove it down into the earth beneath the Tree and closed the seal over it, binding it deep within the roots.

The war was over. The four peoples who had fought together founded [Wonder Kingdom](history.md#the-founding-of-wonder-kingdom), and a company of warriors stayed below to guard the seal. Their descendants are the people of Cavelor.

## The Seal

The seal is a binding of powerful magic, made by the Magicians and strengthened by the combined efforts of the whole alliance. It holds the Balrog in a prison deep below the World Tree, beneath Cavelor and the oldest roots.

For the whole history of the kingdom, it held. Then, fifty years ago, the [Great World Tree Expedition](expedition.md) accidentally opened a rift in the deep passages near the prison and damaged it. The seal did not break, but it began to weaken, and it has been weakening ever since.

## The Balrog Today

Through the damaged seal, the Balrog's influence has been seeping out across Veligor. This is the cause of the [Age of Disturbance](age-of-disturbance.md): creatures grown aggressive, monsters filling the wilds, and danger on every road between the towns. It is exactly what the old accounts described happening before the Balrog arrived, and that is what worries Cavelor's guardians most.

What the Balrog wants, whether it is aware of the world above, and what will happen if the seal fails completely, nobody knows.
