# Wonder Kingdom Today

Fifty years have passed since the Great World Tree Expedition, and Wonder Kingdom is still peaceful, prosperous and wonderfully diverse.

- [Selsior](towns/selsior.md) flourishes as the bustling capital.
- [Kaldemor](towns/kaldemor.md) keeps its cheerful country traditions and its love of craft.
- [Midbarium](towns/midbarium.md) holds fast to its resilient highland ways.
- [Fairy Forest](towns/fairy-forest.md) remains the heart of magic and harmony with nature.
- [Urbanograd](towns/urbanograd.md), now a century old, still goes its own colorful way.
- [Bain Harbour](towns/bain-harbour.md) and [Goa Harbour](towns/goa-harbour.md) thrive as independent ports with very different characters.
- [Cavelor](towns/cavelor.md) keeps its ancient watch beneath the World Tree.

## Everyday Life

For all the danger beyond their walls, the people of Wonder Kingdom live ordinary, happy lives. They trade, farm, craft, learn, play, explore and celebrate. Travel, trade, friendship and shared culture tie the island's communities together, and people are proud to call the kingdom home.

Their happiness is real. It is not a pretty face hiding a broken society.

## The Dangerous Wilderness

Outside the towns, it is another story. Monsters roam much of Veligor's wilderness, and forests, mountains, plains, caves and ancient ruins have all grown more dangerous. Travelers and adventurers must face aggressive creatures and treacherous ground.

Peaceful towns and a dangerous wild: that contrast is the defining feature of the world.

## The Forgotten Threat

To most people, the Balrog is a legend, a figure out of history books. It was defeated and locked away so long ago that hardly anyone thinks of it as a danger today.

The people of Cavelor know better. They remember the first war and understand the seal, and they know that its slow failure is a serious threat.

And deep beneath the World Tree, the Balrog's influence keeps spreading.
