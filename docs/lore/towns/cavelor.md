# Cavelor

*The Underground City of Guardians*

Cavelor is a small and very old city hidden deep beneath the [World Tree](../world-tree.md). It was founded right after the first war against [the Balrog](../balrog.md), when some of the greatest warriors and guardians of that age chose to stay below and keep watch over the seal.

## History

### The Oath Below

When the seal was finished and the four armies began to march home, a company of warriors stayed behind in the roots. They came from all four peoples: Savage warriors, Magicians who understood the seal, Commons who knew how to dig and build, and Aristocrat officers to lead them. They swore an oath, the **Oath Below**, to watch the Balrog's prison for as long as it held, and to send word to the surface if it ever began to fail.

Then they were nearly forgotten. The surface celebrated, the kingdom grew, and within a few generations, the guardians beneath the Tree had faded into legend. Kragvar songs in Midbarium still claimed that some of their warriors stayed below, but few took the songs as anything more than stories.

### A City in the Roots

Below, the guardians built a home. They carved halls out of the rock between the great roots, grew pale mushrooms and root-fruit in the dark, drew water from deep springs and lit their streets with lanterns fed by the faint glow of the Tree's magic. Their children were born below and learned the Oath before they learned to read. Over the centuries, the camp became Cavelor: small, orderly, and completely devoted to its task.

The guardians never stopped watching. Cavelor's **Wardens of the Seal** walk the deep passages around the prison in shifts, and have done so without a single gap for the whole of the kingdom's history. Its scholars keep careful records of every tremor and every change in the seal's glow, written in great ledgers that go back to the founding.

### Rediscovery

For generations, Cavelor had almost no contact with the rest of the kingdom. Then, fifty years ago, the [Great World Tree Expedition](../expedition.md) reached its halls. The explorers were astonished to find the legend alive; the guardians were just as astonished to learn how completely the surface had forgotten them.

It was a joyful meeting, and a terrible one. Within days of that first contact, the expedition's explorers, pushing deeper than the guardians advised, opened the rift that damaged the seal. Cavelor's ledgers recorded the change at once. For fifty years since, the guardians have watched the seal grow weaker, and they have tried, with growing urgency, to make the surface understand what that means.

## The Swordmaster's Oath

Since Queen Aveline's alliance with Cavelor fifteen years ago, the Royal Guard's Swordmasters come down the Upper Stair to swear their oath in the Oath Hall, before the Keeper of the Oath. They swear to stand with the guardians if the seal ever fails, and to carry Cavelor's warnings to the surface. For a city that was forgotten for generations, it is a promise that someone above is listening, and Cavelor takes it as seriously as its own Oath Below.

## Cultural Identity

Cavelor's people live by responsibility, vigilance, discipline and endurance. They keep the traditions of the first guardians and guard the records of the Balrog, the ancient battle and the magic that holds the prison shut. Every adult in Cavelor serves a turn as a Warden.

Their duty is a solemn one, but Cavelor is not a gloomy or oppressive place. Its people have built a real community with a life of its own: they are close-knit and fiercely loyal, quick to laugh among themselves, and deeply curious about the bright world above, which many of them have only recently seen for the first time.

Cavelor is still very much its own place, but since its rediscovery it has been part of the same united kingdom, and its people are as proud of Wonder Kingdom as anyone on the surface. They just know better than most what it might cost to keep it.

## Leadership Today

[Marrun](../people/cavelor/keeper_marrun.md) has been Keeper of the Oath for twenty-two years, since old Keeper Ardith died, and leads Cavelor and the Wardens of the Seal from the Oath Hall. He has walked the Deep Watch for sixty-five years and was there the night the expedition came down. Fifteen years ago he swore Cavelor's alliance with Queen Aveline, and four years ago he climbed to the surface for the first and only time.
