# Urbanograd — The Town of Societal Rebels

Urbanograd is a distinctive urban settlement founded **100 years before the game's present**.

Unlike the original four peoples, Urbanograd's identity was not established during the ancient conflicts preceding Wonder Kingdom.

Instead, it emerged within the already united kingdom.

Its founders were people who preferred unconventional lifestyles, greater personal expression, and independence from traditional social expectations.

Over the following century, Urbanograd developed a distinctive counterculture.

Its general atmosphere is inspired by MapleStory's Kerning City.

## Cultural Identity

Urbanograd is known as the home of **societal rebels**.

Its inhabitants value individuality, creativity, unconventional thinking, freedom of expression, and the ability to live differently from established social norms.

The town attracts people who enjoy alternative lifestyles and unusual forms of art, culture, and personal expression.

However, its inhabitants are not political insurgents.

They do not seek to destabilize Wonder Kingdom, overthrow its institutions, or create conflict with neighboring settlements.

Their rebellious identity is primarily cultural and personal.

## Relationship with Wonder Kingdom

Urbanograd is fully integrated into Wonder Kingdom.

Its residents respect the kingdom and maintain positive relationships with other communities.

The rest of Veligor accepts Urbanograd's unconventional character.

Its people are understood rather than distrusted.

Urbanograd demonstrates that the kingdom's founding principles extend beyond coexistence between the original four peoples.

Wonder Kingdom also accepts communities that develop entirely new cultural identities.
