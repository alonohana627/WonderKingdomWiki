# Fairy Forest

*The Magical Woodland*

Fairy Forest is a settlement hidden among ancient trees, where enchanted plants grow, strange creatures wander and the air hums with magic. It is the historic home of the Magicians, one of the four founding peoples.

## History

### The Listening Woods

The Magicians did not choose Fairy Forest so much as find it. Long before the Age of Division, the first of them came to these woods because magic gathered here more thickly than anywhere else on Veligor, like water collecting in a valley. They believed, and still believe, that the forest's roots reach all the way to the World Tree's, and that the Tree's magic flows outward through the soil and comes up again here.

They learned magic by listening: to the trees, to the wind, to the creatures that lived among them. Their first homes were built into living trunks and branches, shaped by coaxing the trees to grow rather than by cutting them, and the oldest of those homes are still lived in today.

### Keepers of the Tree

Through the Age of Division, the Magicians guarded the forest and, above all, the World Tree. Any attempt to cut, claim or exploit a place of power was met by Magician resistance, and that was the root of their long alliance with the Savages, who had no love for outside rulers either.

In the Balrog War, it was the Magicians of Fairy Forest who understood what the Balrog was enough to know it could not be destroyed, and who designed the great seal that holds it beneath the World Tree. The knowledge of how that seal was made has been one of the forest's most closely guarded secrets ever since. Since the expedition damaged it, the forest's scholars have been studying old records day and night, trying to understand how it might be strengthened.

### The Children Arrive

Fifty years ago, the [Great World Tree Expedition](../expedition.md) found a mysterious people of magical beings living high in the upper reaches of the World Tree. They came to be known as **the Children of the Tree**.

After that first meeting, the Children slowly came down from the Tree and settled in Fairy Forest, perhaps because its magic felt most like home. The forest welcomed them as family. They have since become part of its community and made friends across the kingdom, and their presence gives the forest much of its unique magic.

## Environment and Culture

The people of Fairy Forest live close to both magic and nature. Magical learning, care for the living forest and the study of supernatural wonders are at the heart of who they are. The settlement does not stand apart from the woods; its homes and gathering places are woven into the forest itself, linked by branch-bridges, lantern-lit paths and stairways that spiral around the trunks.

The forest's schools teach magic to anyone with the gift, from any town in the kingdom. Its people take only what the forest can spare, and they will gently but firmly stop any visitor who tries to take more.

The forest has its own friends in the wider kingdom, too. The Brenna of Midbarium have been welcome here since the days when the Savages and the Magicians were allies, and the friendship has outlasted the war that made it.
