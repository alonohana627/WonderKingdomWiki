# Fairy Forest

*The Magical Woodland*

Fairy Forest is a settlement hidden among ancient trees, where enchanted plants grow, strange creatures wander and the air hums with magic. It is the historic home of the Magicians, one of the four founding peoples.

## History

### The Listening Woods

The Magicians did not choose Fairy Forest so much as find it. Long before the Age of Division, the first of them came to these woods because magic gathered here more thickly than anywhere else on Veligor, like water collecting in a valley. They believed, and still believe, that the forest's roots reach all the way to the World Tree's, and that the Tree's magic flows outward through the soil and comes up again here.

They learned magic by listening: to the trees, to the wind, to the creatures that lived among them. Their first homes were built into living trunks and branches, shaped by coaxing the trees to grow rather than by cutting them, and the oldest of those homes are still lived in today.

### Keepers of the Tree

Through the Age of Division, the Magicians guarded the forest and, above all, the World Tree. Any attempt to cut, claim or exploit a place of power was met by Magician resistance, and that was the root of their long alliance with the Savages, who had no love for outside rulers either.

In the Balrog War, it was the Magicians of Fairy Forest who decided it could not be destroyed, and who designed the great seal that holds it beneath the World Tree. The knowledge of how that seal was made has been one of the forest's most closely guarded secrets ever since. Since the expedition damaged it, the forest's scholars have been studying old records day and night, trying to understand how it might be strengthened.

### The Children Arrive

Fifty years ago, the [Great World Tree Expedition](../expedition.md) found a mysterious people of magical beings living high in the upper reaches of the World Tree. They came to be known as **the Children of the Tree**.

After that first meeting, the Children slowly came down from the Tree and settled in Fairy Forest, perhaps because its magic felt most like home. The forest welcomed them as family. They have since become part of its community and made friends across the kingdom, and their presence gives the forest much of its unique magic.

## Environment and Culture

The people of Fairy Forest live close to both magic and nature. Magical learning, care for the living forest and the study of supernatural wonders are at the heart of who they are. The settlement does not stand apart from the woods; its homes and gathering places are woven into the forest itself, linked by branch-bridges, lantern-lit paths and stairways that spiral around the trunks.

The forest's schools teach magic to anyone with the gift, from any town in the kingdom. Its people take only what the forest can spare, and they will gently but firmly stop any visitor who tries to take more.

The forest has its own friends in the wider kingdom, too. The Brenna of Midbarium have been welcome here since the days when the Savages and the Magicians were allies, and the friendship has outlasted the war that made it.

## The Lands Around Fairy Forest

Inside the lantern light, Fairy Forest is as safe and bright as it has ever been. Beyond the last lantern the woods are wild, and in the Age of Disturbance they have grown wilder every year. Three roads lead in: the royal road from Selsior, the Eastern Forest Road from Kaldemor, and the Northern Woodland Trail, which comes in from the west out of [Midbarium](midbarium.md)'s highlands. A chain of ever deeper and darker places runs west from the forest toward the World Tree, whose crown stands far off above the canopy.

### The Eastern Forest Road

The old woodland road runs north from [Kaldemor](kaldemor.md)'s fields to the forest. At **the Forest's Hem**, where the last of the farmland gives way to birch and fern, carters stop at a stile to rest their horses before the trees close in. The road grows greener and older as it goes, until it becomes **the Glimmer Path**, a sun-dappled way under glowing moss and the first of the outer lanterns, which brings travellers in at the Forest Edge. Its creatures are small ones, bubbling things and slimes, mushrooms and walking stumps, but there are far more of them than there used to be, and the carters who bring Kaldemor's goods north now buy their travelling lights in the forest before they turn for home.

### The Royal Road to Selsior

The royal road leaves the forest on its southwest side and runs through **the Bloomwood**, a wide flowering woodland of bluebell glades, wild roses and tall, light trees, before the land opens onto the central plain on its way to [Selsior](selsior.md). It is one of the loveliest roads on Veligor, and lately one of the more troublesome: bright winged creatures the forest calls glimmerwings flit through the glades in swarms, and the largest of them, the crowned glimmerwings, will drive a traveller off the road. From the Bloomwood an old path turns west, toward the World Tree, and leads down to Shimmerwater.

### Shimmerwater and the Night Diggers

**Shimmerwater** is a quiet lake west of the Bloomwood that glows faintly at dusk, reached down three winding lakeside paths. Its slimes, sprites and wisps were once a pretty sight for courting couples. This summer the lake has soured.

It begins, as far as the forest knows, with [Merrow](../people/fairy-forest/merrow_gardener.md)'s seedling beds in the Herb Gardens, dug up night after night by something nobody saw. A traveller who sits up with [Ailo](../people/fairy-forest/ailo_child.md) to watch finds the culprits: young slimes from the lake, seeping into the freshly watered beds and slipping away at the swing of a lantern. [Hesper](../people/fairy-forest/hesper_herbalist.md) tastes the churned soil and finds it bitter. The lake's young are fleeing poison.

At the head of the lake, squatting over the spring that feeds it, sits **the Blightstone**: a Disturbance-touched hulk of mossy stone and rotten wood, from which a bitter sap seeps into the water. With it gone, Shimmerwater can begin to clear, and Merrow may at last walk out to the last lantern for the first time in years, carrying a basket of his wife's salves for the edge watch.

### The Hollow Oak and the Sharpened

Beyond the head of the lake, the shore climbs steeply into **the High Shore**, and above it stands **the Hollow Oak**, an immense, ancient hollow tree so tall that it is climbed in stages, from the wind-tossed lower boughs to where only birds go, and above them to the sky. A ravine runs below it, where the lake's outflow falls away.

This is the country [Juniper](../people/fairy-forest/juniper_adventurer.md) and [Kestrel](../people/fairy-forest/kestrel_adventurer.md) know best, and it is where their worst fears show most clearly. The creatures here are sharpened: larger, bolder and angrier than in any old drawing, and kinds turn up on the oak that never lived there. Lizard-like eyes have multiplied in the wet ground at its foot, and the monkeys in its branches throw more than fruit. The edge watch's spears snap on hides like these, and [Seren](../people/fairy-forest/seren_warden.md) wants them refitted with ironbark from the oak's own fallen limbs, the only wood the forest will spare.

The cause sits in the crown. **Old Grizzlemane**, a vast, grizzled, Disturbance-touched ape, has made the top of the Hollow Oak its own, tearing the crown apart and driving everything beneath it down and out. In its nest lies Juniper's lost notebook, dropped the autumn before as she climbed out of the ravine with something large below her. Given that missing season, Hesper's list of what has attacked her patients, and Juniper's forty notebooks spread across his floor, [Caelum](../people/fairy-forest/caelum_scholar.md) builds his thirty-second theory. For the first time he does not file it as wrong. He starts a new drawer for it, labelled "not wrong yet", and will not say what it shows until he has checked it twice.

### The Gloaming and the Homes That Will Not Wake

West of the Hollow Oak lies **the Gloaming**, a deep, old wood where it is always dusk. In **the Deepening**, the forest's great roots break the surface, the roots that the Magicians have always believed run all the way to the World Tree's. Further on, **the Twilit Wood** is all brambles and glowing springs, where wisps drift, howlets watch from the branches and glimmerfoxes slip between the trees. The creatures of the Gloaming are the most strongly touched anywhere near the forest, and the worst of them are deeply touched.

The trouble shows first at home. A handful of the very oldest living homes around the Heart Tree, the ones the first Magicians shaped, woke late this spring, with thin sap and cold rooms. [Oriel](../people/fairy-forest/oriel_treewright.md) has kept it quiet. Soil and water from the great roots in the Deepening come back cold and thin. On the western side of the forest [Sorrel](../people/fairy-forest/sorrel_teacher.md)'s strange note in the wind is loudest, and there [Ondine](../people/fairy-forest/ondine_lanterns.md)'s outer lanterns keep dimming. If Ondine's book of nineteen years of lantern brightnesses ever reaches the top of the Heart Tree stair, the Grove Council, with [Thessaly](../people/fairy-forest/thessaly_councillor.md) reading out the oldest unkept promise in her ledger, may at last give Seren the guards she has been asking for.

At **the Hidden Spring**, deep in the Twilit Wood, the oldest root rises through the water, and around it lies **the Knotbriar**: a vast, ancient, deeply touched bramble coiled round the root and slowly tightening, with a heart of dark thorn in its coils. Cut it away, and the sap rises again in the oldest homes, late but green, and Sorrel's note in the wind falls silent. Even then, Oriel says, the coldest room of the oldest home stays colder than he remembers, and he is not done listening.

### The Rootbound Wing

Behind the Grove School stands a bramble-grown door that students dare each other to touch. It leads to **the Rootbound Wing**, the oldest wing of the school, where the first Magicians taught, sealed so long ago that nobody's teacher's teacher remembers it open. Living trees have grown through and around it until wing and grove are one, and the old books inside have come alive in the Disturbance: they fly, snap and swarm through **the Hushed Grove**, **the Stilled Grove** and **the Shadowed Grove** to **the Old Reading Room** at the wing's roofless heart, where the great teaching-book of the wing, **the Unbound**, turns in a storm of its own pages.

The way in begins with [Wynne](../people/fairy-forest/wynne_copyist.md). Copying an old seal record, she has found a gap in its numbering, leaves removed long ago by someone who knew how. A shelf list older than the one in use, which [Ilwen](../people/fairy-forest/ilwen_steward.md) can find by page and shelf without asking why, carries a note beside that record in a hand nobody knows: certain leaves "kept apart, in the old wing". [Faelan](../people/fairy-forest/faelan_apprentice.md) makes the lantern to light the way, and Corisande, whose mother and grandmother kept the wing's key before her without anyone remembering why, opens the door for the first time in her life.

From the wing come fragments: scraps of old leaves, mostly faded or torn, a few with numbers that fall inside Wynne's gap. Whether the rest were torn up, taken elsewhere or never there, nobody can say. Wynne takes them to her grandmother, [Isaura](../people/fairy-forest/isaura_records.md), who reads them in silence, says only "thank you", and carries them to the Eldest that night. What they say, she tells nobody else.

The wing never stays quiet for long. Corisande lets anyone with the nerve back in, and the books are always waiting.

## Leadership Today

[Calyra](../people/fairy-forest/eldest_calyra.md) has been Eldest of the Grove for nine years and leads the Grove Council from the Heart Tree. She taught at the Grove School for forty years. When old Eldest Ferris died, the forest's teachers and elders gathered to choose one of their own, and Calyra was chosen over [Thessaly](../people/fairy-forest/thessaly_councillor.md) by a handful of voices.
