# Midbarium

*The Highland Settlement*

Midbarium is a rugged, rural mountain town and the ancestral home of the Savages' culture. It sits high in Veligor's dry uplands, among red-rock peaks, sheer cliffs, deep canyons and steep highland trails, and its people grew strong learning to live with them.

## The Land

The highlands around Midbarium are a hard, beautiful country. Rain is scarce, the sun is fierce, and the wind carries red dust down the canyons for days at a time. Above the town rise wind-carved mesas and bare stone spires; below it, dry riverbeds wind through rocky valleys dotted with caves, hardy scrub and the odd twisted tree. Nights are cold and clear, and the stars over the plateau are famous across the kingdom.

Life here follows the land. There are no grand fields like Kaldemor's. Instead, families farm small stone terraces on the slopes, watered by springs and carefully kept cisterns, and they drive herds of goats and other hardy beasts between the high pastures and the valley floors as the seasons turn. Many Midbarians live not in the town itself but in scattered homesteads and camps across the highlands, coming in to trade, to settle disputes and to celebrate.

## Architecture

Midbarium is built into the mountainside. Its houses are thick-walled and earthen-red, made of stone and sun-dried clay that keep out the heat of the day and hold warmth through the night. Heavy timber beams carry the roofs of the great halls, and terraces, carved stairways and swaying rope-and-plank bridges join the levels of the town across the cliffs.

Out in the highlands, herding families live in sturdy hide tents and stone shelters that can be moved or rebuilt with the seasons. Painted standards and carved totems mark the camps of each family.

## The Four Families

Midbarium is not governed like Selsior. Its people belong to four great **families**, clans bound by blood, oath and shared history, and each family follows a **chieftain**.

### The Kragvar

*The stone-cutters.* The Kragvar built Midbarium. Their ancestors cut the first terraces, raised the great hall and carved the stairways that climb the cliffs, and Kragvar masons still keep every wall and bridge in the town standing. In the Age of Division they were the hardest of the Savage warriors, and in the Balrog War they stood in the front line. Kragvar songs claim that some of their greatest fighters never came home from that war, but stayed beneath the World Tree to keep watch. The family keeps the council fire in the great hall burning, and counts that as its proudest duty.

### The Tolmach

*The herders.* The Tolmach are the wanderers of the highlands. Most of the family still lives in hide tents and moves with its herds between the high pastures and the valley floors, as it has for longer than anyone remembers. They know every old path through the canyons and every spring that runs in a dry year, and that knowledge kept the Savages alive through the long wars. After the founding, it was Tolmach guides who first led traders from Kaldemor and Selsior up into the mountains, and they still bring most of the town's wool, hides and cheese down to market.

### The Uskari

*The hunters.* The Uskari live along the canyon rims, and they are the best trackers, climbers and scouts in Veligor. They once hunted the great beasts of the plateau, and in the old wars they were the eyes of the Savage armies. Since the Age of Disturbance began, the Uskari have taken on a new duty: they patrol the highland trails, drive off the monsters that come up from the canyons, and see travelers safely from one camp to the next. Many young Uskari leave home to become adventurers.

### The Brenna

*The keepers.* The Brenna are Midbarium's healers, storytellers and drummers. They know the highland herbs and how to set a broken bone, and they keep the memory of the families: the long songs that name every chieftain and every Old One back to the Age of Division, recited at every gathering. When the Savages and Magicians were allies, it was Brenna envoys who walked to the forests to talk with them, and the family still has old friends in Fairy Forest. The Brenna host every choosing, and their drums call the families together.

## The Chieftains and the Old One

A chieftain is not simply born to the role. When a chieftain dies, their family gathers around its fire and chooses a new one from among its own people, for strength, wisdom and fairness. A chieftain is expected to lead from the front: to walk the hardest trail first, to judge disputes fairly, to protect the herds and to look after the weakest members of the family.

Above the four chieftains stands one leader for all of Midbarium, whom everyone calls **the Old One**. The name is a title of respect, not a description: an Old One can be any age. The Old One is chosen the same way. When the Old One dies, the four families come together at the great hall, around the fire that is never allowed to go out, and choose the next. By custom, no family may hold the title twice in a row, so each Old One owes the role to all four families, not to their own.

The chieftains meet in council with the Old One to settle matters that touch the whole town. Debates are long and loud, but once the Old One closes the council, every family stands by its decision. Within Wonder Kingdom, the Old One speaks for Midbarium, and the families are as loyal to the kingdom as any lord in the capital.

## Culture and Atmosphere

Midbarium stands for strength, resilience, independence and respect for the ancestors. Its people admire anyone who overcomes hardship, and they guard the knowledge handed down by earlier generations: the old paths through the canyons, where water can be found in a dry year, and the stories of the four families.

Hospitality is sacred in the highlands, where a traveler turned away might not survive the night. Anyone who comes to a Midbarian fire in peace is given water, food and a place to sleep, no questions asked. Feasts, storytelling, contests of strength and long nights of drumming and song mark the families' gatherings.

For all its rugged looks, Midbarium is not hostile, primitive or grim, and it holds nothing against outsiders. Today it is a peaceful, welcoming town whose people are proud of both their ancestral traditions and their place in Wonder Kingdom.

!!! abstract "Design notes"
    The geography and look are inspired by MapleStory's Perion. Midbarium is rural and mountainous, ruled by the chieftains of four families and the Old One, with a dry highland and desert feel. It should draw on highland, canyon and steppe cultures, not on Middle Eastern or Arabian desert cultures. Still open: who the current chieftains and Old One are, and how a family decides between rivals when it chooses a chieftain.
