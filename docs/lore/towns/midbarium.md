# Midbarium — The Highland Settlement

Midbarium is a rugged mountainous settlement associated with the ancestral culture of the Savages.

Its geography and general visual atmosphere are inspired by MapleStory's Perion.

The settlement is surrounded by rocky mountains, cliffs, valleys, highland pathways, and difficult terrain.

Its inhabitants historically developed a culture shaped by the challenges of living in this environment.

## Architecture and Environment

Midbarium's buildings are adapted to the mountainous landscape.

Stone foundations, heavy timber structures, terraces, carved pathways, stairways, and wooden bridges are characteristic of its proposed architectural style.

Its surrounding wilderness contains rocky valleys, caves, highland trails, and mountain regions.

## Culture and Atmosphere

Midbarium is associated with strength, resilience, independence, and ancestral traditions.

Its inhabitants value the ability to overcome difficulties and preserve the knowledge of earlier generations.

However, Midbarium is not a hostile, primitive, or miserable settlement.

Its rugged appearance does not reflect aggression toward outsiders.

Modern Midbarium is a peaceful and welcoming community whose inhabitants are proud of both their ancestral traditions and their membership in Wonder Kingdom.

The exact balance between warrior traditions, ancestral culture, and wilderness-oriented lifestyles remains open for further development.
