# Midbarium

*The Highland Settlement*

Midbarium is a rugged, rural mountain town and the ancestral home of the Savages' culture. It sits high in Veligor's dry uplands, among red-rock peaks, sheer cliffs, deep canyons and steep highland trails, and its people grew strong learning to live with them.

## The Land

The highlands around Midbarium are a hard, beautiful country. Rain is scarce, the sun is fierce, and the wind carries red dust down the canyons for days at a time. Above the town rise wind-carved mesas and bare stone spires; below it, dry riverbeds wind through rocky valleys dotted with caves, hardy scrub and the odd twisted tree. Nights are cold and clear, and the stars over the plateau are famous across the kingdom.

Life here follows the land. There are no grand fields like Kaldemor's. Instead, families farm small stone terraces on the slopes, watered by springs and carefully kept cisterns, and they drive herds of goats and other hardy beasts between the high pastures and the valley floors as the seasons turn. Many Midbarians live not in the town itself but in scattered homesteads and camps across the highlands, coming in to trade, to settle disputes and to celebrate.

## Architecture

Midbarium is built into the mountainside. Its houses are thick-walled and earthen-red, made of stone and sun-dried clay that keep out the heat of the day and hold warmth through the night. Heavy timber beams carry the roofs of the great halls, and terraces, carved stairways and swaying rope-and-plank bridges join the levels of the town across the cliffs.

Out in the highlands, herding families live in sturdy hide tents and stone shelters that can be moved or rebuilt with the seasons. Painted standards and carved totems mark the camps of each clan.

## The Clans and Their Chieftains

Midbarium is not governed like Selsior. Its people belong to **clans**, large families bound by blood, oath and shared history, and each clan follows a **chieftain**.

A chieftain is not simply born to the role. They are chosen for their strength, wisdom and fairness, and they are expected to lead from the front: to walk the hardest trail first, to judge disputes fairly, to protect the herds and to look after the weakest members of the clan. A chieftain who forgets this does not stay chieftain for long.

When matters affect the whole town, the chieftains meet in council at the great hall, around a fire that, by tradition, is never allowed to go out. Decisions are argued long and loudly, but once the council agrees, every clan stands by it. Within Wonder Kingdom, the council speaks for Midbarium, and the chieftains are as loyal to the kingdom as any lord in the capital.

## Culture and Atmosphere

Midbarium stands for strength, resilience, independence and respect for the ancestors. Its people admire anyone who overcomes hardship, and they guard the knowledge handed down by earlier generations: the old paths through the canyons, where water can be found in a dry year, and the stories of the clans.

Hospitality is sacred in the highlands, where a traveler turned away might not survive the night. Anyone who comes to a Midbarian fire in peace is given water, food and a place to sleep, no questions asked. Feasts, storytelling, contests of strength and long nights of drumming and song mark the clans' gatherings.

For all its rugged looks, Midbarium is not hostile, primitive or grim, and it holds nothing against outsiders. Today it is a peaceful, welcoming town whose people are proud of both their ancestral traditions and their place in Wonder Kingdom.

!!! abstract "Design notes"
    The geography and look are inspired by MapleStory's Perion. Midbarium is rural and mountainous, ruled by clan chieftains, with a dry highland and desert feel. It should draw on highland, canyon and steppe cultures, not on Middle Eastern or Arabian desert cultures. The names of the clans, the number of chieftains and how the council chooses its speaker are still open.
