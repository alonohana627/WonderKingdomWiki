# Midbarium

*The Highland Settlement*

Midbarium is a rugged mountain town and the ancestral home of the Savages' culture. It is ringed by rocky peaks, sheer cliffs, deep valleys and steep highland trails, and its people grew strong learning to live with them.

## Architecture and Environment

Midbarium is built into the mountainside: stone foundations, heavy timber halls, terraces, paths carved into the rock, long stairways and swaying wooden bridges. Around it lie rocky valleys, caves, highland trails and open mountain country.

## Culture and Atmosphere

Midbarium stands for strength, resilience, independence and respect for the ancestors. Its people admire anyone who overcomes hardship, and they guard the knowledge handed down by earlier generations.

For all its rugged looks, Midbarium is not hostile, primitive or grim, and it holds nothing against outsiders. Today it is a peaceful, welcoming town whose people are proud of both their ancestral traditions and their place in Wonder Kingdom.

!!! abstract "Design notes"
    The geography and look are inspired by MapleStory's Perion. The architecture described here is proposed. The balance between warrior traditions, ancestral culture and wilderness life is still open.
