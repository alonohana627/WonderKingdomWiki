# Bain Harbour

*The Coastal Town*

Bain Harbour is a prosperous port on one coast of Veligor. It feels like a little of Selsior and a little of Kaldemor, with town life, cheerful craftsmanship and busy trade all meeting the sea.

## History

### The Fishermen's Cove

Bain Harbour began as a cove of Commons fishing families, who found that the deep, sheltered bay was the one place on that coast where boats could ride out a storm. Through the Age of Division, the cove stayed small and kept its head down, selling fish to whichever side came to buy and trying hard to stay out of the wars.

### The Shipwrights' Century

After the founding, peace changed everything. With the island united, goods needed to move from one end of Veligor to the other, and the sea was often the fastest road. The fishing families took up shipbuilding, and the cove's sheltered water became the island's busiest shipyard. Merchants followed the ships, craftsmen followed the merchants, and the cove grew into a town.

The town takes its name from **Old Bain**, a fisherwoman remembered as the first to build a ship big enough to sail around the island. Her boat-hook still hangs above the door of the harbour hall.

### A Town of Its Own

As it grew, both Selsior and Kaldemor did a great deal of business through Bain Harbour, and more than once someone in the capital suggested that the port ought to answer to the crown's own officials. The people of Bain Harbour politely refused every time. The harbour is run by its own **harbour council**, made up of its captains, shipwrights and merchants, and the town is proud that no one else has ever had a say in its affairs.

## Environment and Identity

Bain Harbour is a town of docks and waterfront houses, markets and shipwrights' workshops, warehouses and lighthouses, and everything travellers and traders need. Its look and spirit blend city and countryside: Selsior's busy streets and stone, Kaldemor's colorful wood and cheer. It is welcoming, prosperous and always sociable, and it smells of tar, salt and fresh bread.

Since the Age of Disturbance began, the sea around Veligor has grown rougher and stranger, and Bain Harbour's sailors tell of creatures in the deep water that were never there before. The harbour's ships still sail, but its captains now hire adventurers to sail with them.

## Independence

Bain Harbour is **not Selsior's port, nor Kaldemor's**. It is a town in its own right, with its own history, people and identity. What it shares with those towns is a feeling, not a chain of command, and it belongs to Wonder Kingdom on equal terms with every other settlement.

It lies on the opposite side of the island from [Goa Harbour](goa-harbour.md). The two ports are very different, but they are friends, not rivals, and they hold a friendly boat race between them every summer that goes all the way around Veligor.
