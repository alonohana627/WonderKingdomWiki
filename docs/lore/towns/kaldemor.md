# Kaldemor

*The Town of Joyful Craftsmanship*

Kaldemor is a cheerful pastoral town set among fertile fields, rolling hills and green countryside. Its heart is **joyful craftsmanship**: its people love making things with their own hands, growing their own food and living well together.

## Architecture and Environment

Kaldemor is a town of colorful wooden houses and cozy cottages, workshops and gardens, barns and welcoming village greens. Beyond it stretch meadows, farmland, orchards, gentle hills and patches of woodland. It is a sunny, colorful place that seems to invite visitors in.

## Culture and Atmosphere

The people of Kaldemor value creativity, practical know-how, friendliness, cooperation and the quiet satisfaction of work done well. Traditional crafts are part of everyday life: woodworking, carpentry, pottery and farming all shape who the town is.

Kaldemor is no poor backwater. It is a prosperous, happy town whose people love the life they have made. Where Selsior stands for the excitement and variety of the great city, Kaldemor stands for the joy of creating, growing and belonging to a close-knit community.
