# Kaldemor

*The Town of Joyful Craftsmanship*

Kaldemor is a cheerful pastoral town set among fertile fields, rolling hills and green countryside, "the greenest town this side of the old forest", as its mayor likes to say. Its heart is **joyful craftsmanship**: its people love making things with their own hands, growing their own food and living well together. Just under three hundred people live there, not counting the snails.

## History

### One Seed

Every child in Kaldemor knows how the town began. Long ago, a traveller crossing the eastern meadows planted a single seed and slept beside it for the night. In the morning, the seed had become a tree taller than a tower.

People came from all over to see it, and some of them never left. They built their homes around the great tree, and then in it: the **great treehouse** behind the town square is the oldest building in Kaldemor. Nobody remembers the traveller's name, but the town still lives by the saying the story left behind: *every great town starts with one seed.*

Whether the seed came from the World Tree, as the Magicians of Fairy Forest suspect, nobody can say. But the soil around Kaldemor has been strangely generous ever since.

### Mushroom Houses and Windmills

That generous soil grows mushrooms big enough to live in, and Kaldemor's settlers did exactly that. The town's famous **mushroom houses** are real mushrooms, hollowed out, fitted with doors and windows, and painted in bright colors. They grow a little every spring, and their owners simply add another room.

The early settlers were mostly Commons: farmers, carpenters, potters and bakers. They planted the fields and orchards, raised a windmill farm in the park, and built workshops wherever there was room. When the Royal Road was laid from Selsior to the east, Kaldemor became the last stop before the eastern countryside, and its markets grew busy with travellers.

### The Clock Tower

The town's other landmark is the **clock tower** in the park. It was raised by the whole town together, a gift from every family and every workshop, and its bell has kept Kaldemor's time ever since. The terrace oven in the market was built the same year, by Rosa the baker's grandmother, and it has not gone cold since.

The clock stopped once, not long ago, when a Shroom from the woods carried off its great gear. The whole town held its breath until the gear came back and the bell rang again.

## The Town Today

Kaldemor is led by its **mayor**, chosen by the townspeople. Mayor Tobin has held the post for thirty years and knows every family by name. The town square sits in the middle, with the Market to the east for potions, weapons and bread, and the Park to the west with its clock tower and windmills.

Beyond the town lie the fields, hills and woods where young warriors learn their trade, and lately there has been plenty to learn on. Snails eat the gardens, Shrooms raid the woods, and something huge has settled in a hollow past the woods: a giant snail the townsfolk call **the Snail King**. The town's park ranger and drillmaster keep a close watch on the wilds, and they are always glad of help.

## Architecture and Environment

Kaldemor is a town of colorful wooden houses and cozy cottages, mushroom houses and treehouses, workshops and gardens, barns and welcoming village greens. Beyond it stretch meadows, farmland, orchards, gentle hills and patches of woodland. It is a sunny, colorful place that seems to invite visitors in.

## Culture and Atmosphere

The people of Kaldemor value creativity, practical know-how, friendliness, cooperation and the quiet satisfaction of work done well. Traditional crafts are part of everyday life: woodworking, carpentry, pottery, baking and farming all shape who the town is.

Kaldemor is no poor backwater. It is a prosperous, happy town whose people love the life they have made. Where Selsior stands for the excitement and variety of the great city, Kaldemor stands for the joy of creating, growing and belonging to a close-knit community.
