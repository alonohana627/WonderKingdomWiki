# Goa Harbour

*The Wilderness Harbour*

Goa Harbour is a port on the west coast of Veligor, on the far side of the island from [Bain Harbour](bain-harbour.md). It feels like a little of Midbarium and a little of Fairy Forest, with highland ruggedness and woodland magic meeting the sea.

## History

### Where the Mountains Meet the Sea

The coast around Goa Harbour is wild: cliffs that drop straight into the water, forests that run down to the shore, and a single deep inlet cut between them. During the Age of Division, both Savages and Magicians came to that inlet. Savage hunters came down from the hills for the fishing, and Magicians came for the sea-herbs and the strange tides, which they believed were moved by the same magic that flowed through the forests. Being allies, they shared it, and a mixed camp grew up on the shore.

### The Sea Wardens

The camp gave the town its oldest tradition. The tides in the inlet are fast and treacherous, and the first families learned to read them the way Magicians read the forest. They called themselves **wardens of the sea**, and to this day, Goa Harbour's pilots guide every ship through the inlet, and no captain sails in without one.

The name Goa is said to come from an old Savage word for "the gap", the narrow gap between the cliffs where the inlet opens to the sea.

### After the Founding

When the kingdom was founded, Goa Harbour opened to the whole island. Timber from its forests and furs and wool from the highlands went out by sea, and travellers began to come for the wild coast and the strange, half-magical town on it. It never grew as large or as busy as Bain Harbour, and its people prefer it that way.

## Environment and Identity

Goa Harbour is rougher and wilder than Bain Harbour, shaped by the mountains, forests and rocky shores around it. Its houses are built of timber and stone and climb the cliffs above the water, joined by rope bridges and carved stairways much like Midbarium's, while lanterns of the forest's making light its quays at night.

Its people keep proud seafaring traditions, along with the independent spirit of the highlands and a touch of the forests' magic. They are adventurous and welcoming, and the town is unlike any other on the island. It is also a natural home for adventurers: since the Age of Disturbance began, the wild coast and forests around it have filled with monsters, and Goa Harbour's people have never been the sort to wait for help.

## Independence

Goa Harbour is **not Midbarium's port, nor Fairy Forest's**. It has its own people, traditions and history, and its resemblance to those towns is cultural, not political. It is led by its **First Warden**, the most respected of its pilots, chosen by the others.

Bain Harbour and Goa Harbour are not rivals. Both are peaceful, prosperous parts of Wonder Kingdom, and every summer their sailors race each other all the way around Veligor.

## Leadership Today

[Iska](../people/goa-harbour/first_warden_iska.md) has been First Warden for seven years. She learned the tides from [Varro](../people/goa-harbour/warden_varro.md) from the age of seven, and seven years ago the other wardens chose her to lead them and the town from the Wardens' House. [Nessa](../people/goa-harbour/warden_nessa.md) was the other name spoken that night, and backed Iska herself in the end. Iska has skippered Goa Harbour's race boat for twelve summers.
