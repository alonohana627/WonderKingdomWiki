# The Great World Tree Expedition

Fifty years ago, Wonder Kingdom sent an expedition into the World Tree. The Tree had been a landmark for centuries, but most of its interior had never been explored, and the journey became one of the greatest feats of exploration in the kingdom's history.

## The Upper Chambers

The explorers first climbed up through the Tree's vast inner passages. They found astonishing magical places: strange plants, extraordinary creatures and natural structures of dizzying complexity.

There they met the [Children of the Tree](towns/fairy-forest.md#the-children-arrive), a magical people who had lived cut off from the rest of the world. After the expedition, the Children gradually moved to Fairy Forest and became part of the wider kingdom.

## The Discovery of Cavelor

Having explored the heights, the expedition turned downward, into the depths beneath the Tree. There they found [Cavelor](towns/cavelor.md).

They learned that the descendants of ancient guardians had lived in the hidden city for generations, keeping the memory of the Balrog alive and guarding its prison. The legendary war against the Balrog, it turned out, had left behind a living order sworn to stop its return.

## The Accident

Exploring the passages deeper still, the explorers accidentally opened a rift, and the rift damaged the magic that held the Balrog.

The seal did not break. It began, very slowly, to weaken. At first nobody realized what had happened. But over the following decades, the failing seal began to reach the world above, and the [Age of Disturbance](age-of-disturbance.md) began.
