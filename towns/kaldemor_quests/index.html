    Quests of Kaldemor¶

    Kaldemor has 54 quests, grouped below into quest lines in the order they are played. A line is a chain of quests that each need the one before.

    Story line, 12 quests, from level 1. Starts with Lia.

    # Quest Level Giver Objectives Rewards
    1 Welcome Home 1 Lia - 15 EXP, +10 standing with Kaldemor

    4 Shells for the Mayor 2 Hale Bring 15 Green Snail Shell (taken) 120 EXP, Wooden Buckler x1, +30 standing with Kaldemor
    5 Strength of a Warrior 3 Tobin Reach level 5; Use Power Strike 5 times 200 EXP, Bronze Helmet x1, Skill: Warrior's Resolve, +20 standing with Kaldemor
    6 The Clock Stopped 4 Ewan Defeat 20 Shroom; Deliver 1 Clock Gear 400 EXP, Old Pocket Watch x1, +50 standing with Kaldemor, Sets kaldemor.clock_running
    7 Hale's Bounty 3 Hale Defeat 30 Field monsters (Snail, Blue Snail, Red Snail) 100 EXP, 200 mesos
    8 A Smith's First Bar 3 Gareth Gather 4 Copper Ore; Make 1 Copper Ingot 120 EXP, 200 mesos, Recipe: Bronze Axe (Smithing), +20 standing with Kaldemor
    9 A Head for Heights 3 Garrick Reach the top of the hills in Kaldemor Hills 150 EXP, Skill: Rope Up, +20 standing with Kaldemor
