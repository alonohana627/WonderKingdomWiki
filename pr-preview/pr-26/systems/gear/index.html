    Gear Tiers¶

    Equipment comes in tiers every ten levels. A tier's main stat (attack for weapons, defense for armour) follows one smooth curve, so each tier is a clear step up. These are the plain pieces every town shares; each town adds themed pieces of its own, listed on its town page.
