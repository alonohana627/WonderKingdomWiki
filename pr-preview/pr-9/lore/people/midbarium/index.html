    Great Hall¶

    Hask¶

    The Old One

    Role: Ruler · Age: Adult · Family: Uskari

    Quiet, patient and hard to rattle; Hask listens to a whole loud council without a word, then speaks once and plainly.

    Hask was born an Uskari and led the family as its chieftain until Old One Varra of the Brenna died and the four families chose Hask to follow her. Hask gave up the Uskari chieftainship that same night, as custom asks, and is one of the youngest Old Ones the Brenna songs name. Hask's private worry is that the canyon patrols are stretched thinner every season, and that the families are too proud to say so in council.

    Relationships:

    • Zirra: older sister
    • Tavi: successor
    • Ulm: keeper of the fire and trusted counsel
    • Dorrek: blunt but loyal critic
    • Odda: host of the choosing and adviser

    Ulm¶

    Keeper of the Council Fire

    Role: Official · Age: Elder · Family: Kragvar

    Gruff, slow-moving and fiercely proud of his duty; he talks to the fire more kindly than to most people.

    Ulm has tended the fire in the great hall for forty years and has never once let it burn low. He knows every Kragvar song about the fighters who stayed beneath the World Tree, and lately he wonders aloud whether anyone down there still remembers the Kragvar.

    Relationships:

    • Dorrek: son
    • Korrin: grandson
    • Hask: the Old One he serves

    Thrun¶

    Drummer of the Gatherings

    Role: Performer · Age: Elder · Family: Brenna

    Loud, warm and impossible to embarrass; Thrun laughs from the belly and drums the same way.

    Thrun beats the great drum that calls the four families to every choosing and every feast. Thrun drummed for the choosing of Old One Hask and swears it was the finest night of drumming in a generation, though the drum's old hide has since split and needs replacing with the skin of a beast few hunters would dare to track.

    Relationships:

    • Odda: wife
    • Lessa: daughter
    • Vosk: old friend

    Terraces¶

    Gorra¶

    Cistern Keeper

    Role: Worker · Age: Adult · Family: Kragvar

    Brisk, practical and always counting; Gorra measures water the way merchants measure coin.

    Gorra keeps the stone cisterns that water the terraces and knows to the bucket how long the town can last in a dry year. One of the oldest cisterns has started losing water through a crack nobody can find, and she would quietly reward anyone who could track it down.

    Relationships:

    • Dorrek: husband
    • Brakka: daughter
    • Korrin: son
    • Yarrek: water-sharing neighbour

    Korrin¶

    Chieftain's Son

    Role: Child · Age: Child · Family: Kragvar

    Bold, scabby-kneed and always climbing something he was told not to climb.

    Korrin is Dorrek's youngest and spends his days scrambling between the terraces with his friend Kesh. He wants to join the Uskari on the trail watch when he grows up, which his Kragvar family finds very funny and slightly insulting.

    Relationships:

    • Dorrek: father
    • Gorra: mother
    • Ulm: grandfather
    • Kesh: best friend
    • Bekk: hero

    Imre¶

    Stargazer

    Role: Scholar · Age: Adult · Family: none

    Dreamy, absent-minded and delighted by everything; Imre forgets meals but never forgets a star.

    Imre came to Midbarium to study its famous night skies and stayed for the stars and the hospitality. Imre spends nights on the highest terrace charting the heavens and swapping star stories with the Brenna, and hopes one day to be invited to recite at a gathering.

    Relationships:

    • Ahvi: friend
    • Corwell: landlord

    Kragvar Quarter¶

    Dorrek¶

    Chieftain of the Kragvar

    Role: Ruler · Age: Adult · Family: Kragvar

    Blunt, broad-shouldered and stubborn as granite, but scrupulously fair once he has made up his mind.

    Dorrek was chosen chieftain when his uncle died in a rockfall, and he still climbs the highest scaffold himself before he lets any mason on it. He argued long in council for a Kragvar Old One, lost to the families' choice of Hask, and has stood by that decision without a word of complaint ever since.

    Relationships:

    • Gorra: wife
    • Brakka: daughter
    • Korrin: son
    • Ulm: father
    • Vosk: cousin
    • Hask: respected chieftain

    Brakka¶

    Apprentice Mason

    Role: Craftsman · Age: Young · Family: Kragvar

    Eager, restless and quick with a chisel; Brakka would rather carve something new than mend something old.

    Brakka is Dorrek's daughter and is learning the mason's trade from Vosk. She dreams of carving a new stairway down the cliff to the valley floor, the first new route in a hundred years, and has already sketched it on every flat stone she can find.

    Relationships:

    • Dorrek: father
    • Gorra: mother
    • Vosk: mentor

    Vosk¶

    Master of Bridges

    Role: Craftsman · Age: Elder · Family: Kragvar

    Careful, dry-humoured and suspicious of anything that sways; he trusts a knot only after he has tied it himself.

    Vosk keeps every rope-and-plank bridge in Midbarium sound and has taught three generations of Kragvar how to splice a cable. Twice this season he has found ropes on the far canyon bridge gnawed half through in the night, and he does not know what is doing it.

    Relationships:

    • Brakka: apprentice
    • Dorrek: cousin
    • Thrun: old friend

    Tolmach Camps¶

    Sennu¶

    Chieftain of the Tolmach

    Role: Ruler · Age: Adult · Family: Tolmach

    Weathered, wry and unhurried; Sennu has a herder's patience and a herder's habit of counting heads twice.

    Sennu leads the Tolmach from a hide tent like any other herder, and moves the camps with the seasons as the family always has. This year Sennu has kept the herds closer to town than ever before because of the monsters in the low valleys, and the high pastures are starting to suffer for it.

    Relationships:

    • Mabbu: mother
    • Harra: sister
    • Ennek: son
    • Tavi: uneasy ally on the trails

    Mabbu¶

    Elder of the Camps

    Role: Resident · Age: Elder · Family: Tolmach

    Sharp-tongued, sharp-eyed and endlessly hospitable; she will scold a guest and feed them in the same breath.

    Mabbu is mother to Sennu and Harra and has walked every herding path in the highlands at least a hundred times. Her knees no longer let her follow the herds, so she keeps the home camp and remembers, out loud, every spring that ran in every dry year.

    Relationships:

    • Sennu: daughter
    • Harra: daughter
    • Lessa: healer

    Yarrek¶

    Goat Herder

    Role: Worker · Age: Adult · Family: Tolmach

    Gentle, slow to speak and devoted to his goats, each of which he knows by name.

    Yarrek leads the family's goats between the high pastures and the camps. Last month three of his goats came back from the valley wild-eyed and biting, and he keeps them penned apart, hoping they will calm.

    Relationships:

    • Harra: wife
    • Kesh: son
    • Gorra: water-sharing neighbour

    Kesh¶

    Young Goatherd

    Role: Child · Age: Child · Family: Tolmach

    Chatty, curious and fearless; Kesh asks every traveler where they came from and what the road was like.

    Kesh is Harra and Yarrek's son and is trusted with the kids of the herd, though not yet with the grown goats. He and Korrin have a secret lookout above the terraces where they watch for monsters and, so far, mostly see eagles.

    Relationships:

    • Yarrek: father
    • Harra: mother
    • Ennek: cousin
    • Korrin: best friend

    Uskari Rim¶

    Tavi¶

    Chieftain of the Uskari

    Role: Ruler · Age: Adult · Family: Uskari

    Restless, direct and competitive; Tavi would rather walk the trail than talk about it, and hates losing at anything.

    Tavi left the rim as a young adventurer and spent a season training with the swordsmen of the capital before coming home to hunt. When Hask was chosen Old One, the Uskari chose Tavi to lead them, and Tavi has since doubled the trail patrols and still walks the worst stretch personally.

    Relationships:

    • Hask: former chieftain
    • Skarra: friendly rival
    • Ketta: worried-over hunter
    • Sennu: uneasy ally on the trails
    • Rhys: old training partner

    Zirra¶

    Bowyer

    Role: Merchant · Age: Elder · Family: Uskari · Sells: Bows, arrows, climbing ropes and hunting knives

    Precise, sardonic and impossible to flatter; Zirra judges people by how they hold a bow.

    Zirra is the Old One's older sister and makes the bows the Uskari hunt and patrol with. She teases Hask mercilessly in private and defends Hask fiercely to anyone else.

    Relationships:

    • Hask: younger brother
    • Ketta: former pupil

    Skarra¶

    Captain of the Trail Watch

    Role: Guard · Age: Adult · Family: Uskari

    Stern, watchful and dryly funny once the patrol is safely home.

    Skarra commands the Uskari patrols that guard the highland trails and see travelers from camp to camp. She marks every monster sighting on a hide map in her tent, and the marks have been creeping closer to town each season.

    Relationships:

    • Tavi: friendly rival
    • Bekk: son

    Bekk¶

    Trail Watch Scout

    Role: Guard · Age: Young · Family: Uskari

    Cocky, quick and kind to children, who follow him around in a small admiring crowd.

    Bekk is Skarra's son and the fastest climber on the rim. He wants to prove himself by tracking a beast that has been raiding the high pastures, and his mother has forbidden him to try it alone.

    Relationships:

    • Skarra: mother
    • Korrin: admirer

    Ketta¶

    Returned Adventurer

    Role: Adventurer · Age: Young · Family: Uskari

    Bright and talkative about everything except one subject, which makes her go suddenly quiet.

    Ketta left home to adventure across Veligor, like many young Uskari, and came back last spring with a new scar and a pack full of stories. She will happily tell any of them except what she saw in a deep canyon cave on her way home.

    Relationships:

    • Zirra: teacher
    • Tavi: chieftain
    • Ennek: sweetheart

    Brenna House¶

    Odda¶

    Chieftain of the Brenna

    Role: Ruler · Age: Elder · Family: Brenna

    Warm, shrewd and endlessly calm; Odda can settle a quarrel between two chieftains with one well-chosen story.

    Odda leads the Brenna, who host every choosing, and she hosted the one that named Hask after Old One Varra died. She keeps up an old friendship in Fairy Forest from her envoy days and still writes letters there each spring.

    Relationships:

    • Thrun: husband
    • Lessa: daughter
    • Neffa: granddaughter
    • Ahvi: cousin
    • Hask: friend
    • Calyra: old friend

    Lessa¶

    Bonesetter

    Role: Healer · Age: Adult · Family: Brenna · Sells: Herbal salves, splints and highland remedies

    Calm in a crisis and fussy everywhere else; Lessa will set a broken leg without blinking and then complain about the mud on your boots.

    Lessa is Odda and Thrun's daughter and the best bonesetter in the highlands. She has treated more monster bites this year than in her first ten years of healing together, and she is running short of the highland herbs that grow only on the far slopes.

    Relationships:

    • Odda: mother
    • Thrun: father
    • Neffa: daughter
    • Mabbu: grumbling patient

    Neffa¶

    Song Learner

    Role: Child · Age: Child · Family: Brenna

    Solemn, serious and proud; Neffa practises the long songs under her breath all day long.

    Neffa is Lessa's daughter and is learning the long song of the Old Ones from Ahvi. Her great wish is to recite the whole song at a gathering without a single mistake before she turns twelve.

    Relationships:

    • Lessa: mother
    • Odda: grandmother
    • Ahvi: teacher

    Ahvi¶

    Song-Keeper

    Role: Scholar · Age: Elder · Family: Brenna

    Soft-voiced, exact and gently stubborn; Ahvi corrects anyone who gets a chieftain's name wrong, however important they are.

    Ahvi keeps the long songs that name every chieftain and Old One back to the Age of Division, and was the sister of the late Old One Varra. Lately she has been troubled by an old verse about beasts growing wild before the Burning came, and she cannot decide whether to sing it at the next gathering.

    Relationships:

    • Neffa: pupil
    • Odda: cousin
    • Imre: friend

    Trading Post¶

    Harra¶

    Wool and Cheese Seller

    Role: Merchant · Age: Adult · Family: Tolmach · Sells: Wool, hides, goat cheese and travel rations

    Shrewd, cheerful and loud enough to be heard across the Trading Post; Harra never lets a customer leave without a taste of cheese.

    Harra brings the Tolmach herds' wool, hides and cheese to market and drives a famously hard bargain with lowland traders. She worries that fewer traders make the climb each year now that the canyon roads are dangerous.

    Relationships:

    • Mabbu: mother
    • Sennu: sister
    • Yarrek: husband
    • Kesh: son
    • Corwell: friendly rival trader

    Ennek¶

    Canyon Guide

    Role: Service · Age: Young · Family: Tolmach

    Easygoing and confident, with a herder's eye for weather and a habit of whistling on dangerous ledges.

    Ennek is Sennu's son and leads traders and travelers up the old Tolmach paths, as his family has since the founding. A spring on the south path that has run for as long as anyone remembers has gone dry this summer, and Ennek has not yet told his mother.

    Relationships:

    • Sennu: mother
    • Kesh: cousin
    • Ketta: sweetheart
    • Corwell: employer

    Corwell¶

    Keeper of the Trading Post

    Role: Merchant · Age: Adult · Family: none · Sells: Lowland goods, lamp oil, salt, tools and travel supplies

    Talkative, round and comfortable, with a lowlander's love of a good chair and a highlander's love of a good bargain.

    Corwell came up the canyon with a trade caravan twenty years ago, was given water and a place by a Tolmach fire, and never went home. He runs the Trading Post where lowland goods meet highland ones, and he quietly worries about the caravans that now arrive later and later, or not at all.

    Relationships:

    • Harra: friendly rival trader
    • Ennek: guide for hire
    • Imre: lodger
