    Heart Tree¶

    Calyra¶

    Eldest of the Grove

    Role: Ruler · Age: Elder

    Patient and dry-humoured, she lets everyone finish speaking before she answers, and her answers are short. She still corrects students' spellwork out of habit.

    Calyra taught at the Grove School for forty years before the forest's teachers and elders chose her from among themselves to be Eldest, and she now leads the Grove Council from the Heart Tree. Her quiet worry is the seal scholars, who sleep too little and find too little. An old friend among the Brenna of Midbarium still visits her each spring.

    Relationships:

    • Thessaly: old rival and friend
    • Ilwen: steward
    • Amaryl: former student
    • Isaura: confidante
    • Halcy: friend
    • Odda: old friend

    Thessaly¶

    Grove Councillor

    Role: Council · Age: Elder

    Proud, precise and quick to object, but the first to admit when she has been out-argued. She keeps a ledger of every promise the Council makes.

    Thessaly was the other name spoken most often when the elders chose their Eldest, and she lost to Calyra by a handful of voices. She took it graciously and has been Calyra's sharpest critic and most reliable ally ever since.

    Relationships:

    • Calyra: old rival and friend
    • Briony: sister

    Ilwen¶

    Steward of the Heart Tree

    Role: Official · Age: Adult

    Calm, tidy and endlessly polite, with a habit of answering questions with the exact page and shelf where the answer can be found.

    Ilwen keeps the Council's records, its meeting days and its guest list, and greets every visitor who climbs the Heart Tree's stair. He would like, just once, to take a holiday, but cannot think who would water the Council chamber's vines.

    Relationships:

    • Calyra: employer
    • Wynne: friend

    Isaura¶

    Keeper of the Old Records

    Role: Scholar · Age: Elder

    Stern, careful and secretive by duty rather than nature. She is gentler with her granddaughter than with anyone else.

    Isaura guards the oldest Magician records, including what little remains about how the great seal was made, and leads the scholars studying it. Fifty years of reading have taught her a great deal about the seal and nothing yet about how to strengthen it. She has begun to suspect that some of the oldest pages were never written down at all.

    Relationships:

    • Calyra: confidante
    • Caelum: student
    • Wynne: granddaughter

    Caelum¶

    Seal Scholar

    Role: Scholar · Age: Adult

    Brilliant, restless and short on sleep, he talks quickly and forgets to eat. He hates admitting he is stuck.

    Caelum has spent his whole working life comparing the old records with reports of how the monsters are changing, hoping the pattern will point to the seal's weak places. Every theory he has built so far has fallen apart, and he keeps a drawer of them labelled "wrong".

    Relationships:

    • Isaura: mentor
    • Wynne: rival
    • Juniper: colleague

    Wynne¶

    Copyist of the Old Records

    Role: Scholar · Age: Young

    Quiet, observant and stubborn. She notices what others skip and says so only when she is sure.

    Wynne copies crumbling records onto fresh paper so the originals can rest. While copying she found a gap in the numbering of one old seal record, as though several pages were removed long ago, and she has not yet decided whom to tell.

    Relationships:

    • Ilwen: friend
    • Isaura: grandmother
    • Caelum: rival

    Oriel¶

    Master Treewright

    Role: Craftsman · Age: Elder

    Gruff, gentle-handed and slow to trust anything that grew in less than a century.

    Oriel tends the oldest living-tree homes around the Heart Tree, some of which the first Magicians coaxed from the trunks. Lately a few of the oldest homes have been slow to wake in spring, and he wants to know why before anyone else notices.

    Relationships:

    • Aspen: old friend
    • Linnet: apprentice
    • Niv: friend

    Grove School¶

    Sorrel¶

    Teacher of Listening

    Role: Scholar · Age: Adult

    Soft-spoken and unhurried, he believes most mistakes come from talking too much. His lessons are mostly silence, and his students love him anyway.

    Sorrel teaches the first and oldest Magician lesson, how to listen to trees, wind and creatures before trying any magic at all. Lately he says the forest sounds uneasy at its edges, and he cannot explain what he means.

    Relationships:

    • Aurel: student
    • Liri: student
    • Amaryl: colleague

    Amaryl¶

    Teacher of Growing

    Role: Scholar · Age: Elder

    Brisk, warm and demanding, with no patience for shortcuts. She praises rarely and means it every time.

    Amaryl teaches the coaxing of living wood, the craft behind the forest's tree homes. She was once Calyra's student, and still sometimes calls her "teacher" in Council by mistake.

    Relationships:

    • Calyra: former teacher
    • Sorrel: colleague
    • Siora: student

    Aurel¶

    Junior Teacher

    Role: Scholar · Age: Young

    Eager, nervous and over-prepared, he writes out every lesson word for word and then forgets the notes.

    Aurel finished his own schooling only three years ago and now teaches the youngest students. He wants badly to prove he belongs at the front of the room.

    Relationships:

    • Sorrel: mentor
    • Galen: brother

    Galen¶

    Staff and Wand Seller

    Role: Merchant · Age: Adult · Sells: Staves, wands, spell scrolls and practice robes

    Cheerful, talkative and a born salesman who never sells a student something too strong for them, however much they beg.

    Galen keeps the little shop at the foot of the school stair and fits every new student with a first staff. He worries about his younger brother Aurel, and drops by his classroom far more often than he needs to.

    Relationships:

    • Aurel: brother

    Liri¶

    Grove School Student

    Role: Child · Age: Child

    Bright, bold and impatient, she wants to learn the exciting spells first and the listening never.

    Liri was born in the forest and has been at the Grove School for one year. She has a plan to sneak out to the Forest Edge and see a real monster, and has told only her best friend.

    Relationships:

    • Sorrel: teacher
    • Lumi: best friend
    • Tansy: mother

    Siora¶

    Grove School Student

    Role: Child · Age: Child

    Shy, thoughtful and hard on herself, she practises long after everyone else has gone home.

    Siora's gift is real but slow to wake, and her spells fizzle more often than they work. She is afraid of being sent home before it does.

    Relationships:

    • Amaryl: teacher
    • Alder: friend

    Alder¶

    Grove School Student

    Role: Resident · Age: Young

    Easygoing and funny, he hides his homesickness behind jokes about the forest having no sea.

    Alder came from a harbour town to study at the Grove School, which teaches anyone with the gift. The road here was more dangerous than he expected, and he dreads the journey home at the end of term.

    Relationships:

    • Siora: friend
    • Kestrel: hero

    Lantern Paths¶

    Ondine¶

    Keeper of the Lanterns

    Role: Craftsman · Age: Elder

    Exacting, proud and secretly sentimental. She can tell which of her lanterns is dimming from across the forest.

    Ondine leads the lantern-makers who light every path, bridge and stair in the forest. She has made the lanterns along the outer paths brighter each year as the woods beyond grow less friendly, and does not like what that says.

    Relationships:

    • Faelan: apprentice
    • Vesper: daughter

    Faelan¶

    Lantern-maker's Apprentice

    Role: Craftsman · Age: Young

    Dreamy and clumsy with his hands, but with a real eye for colour. He hums while he works.

    Faelan has broken more lantern glass than any apprentice Ondine has ever taken. He is saving up to make a lantern of his own design as a gift for Linnet.

    Relationships:

    • Ondine: mentor
    • Linnet: sweetheart

    Vesper¶

    Lantern Seller

    Role: Merchant · Age: Adult · Sells: Lanterns, glow-oil and travelling lights

    Sharp, practical and quick with a bargain, she has none of her mother's patience and all of her skill.

    Vesper sells her mother's lanterns and her own, and her travelling lights have become popular with anyone who must use the roads after dark. She and Ondine argue about the shop's prices every single day.

    Relationships:

    • Ondine: mother
    • Tansy: friend

    Tansy¶

    Keeper of the Dewdrop Tea House

    Role: Service · Age: Adult · Sells: Dew tea, honey cakes and a bed for the night

    Warm, busy and impossible to surprise, she remembers every guest's favourite cake.

    Tansy runs the tea house where students, travellers and scholars all end up sooner or later. She is raising her daughter Liri alone and suspects the girl is planning something.

    Relationships:

    • Liri: daughter
    • Vesper: friend
    • Lyra: friend
    • Halcy: supplier

    Lyra¶

    Singer of the Old Songs

    Role: Performer · Age: Adult

    Bright, theatrical and generous, she will sing for anyone who asks and many who do not.

    Lyra sings the forest's old songs on the Lantern Paths each evening, including the ones about the Balrog War. She learned most of them from her grandfather and is collecting the verses nobody else remembers.

    Relationships:

    • Tansy: friend
    • Aspen: grandfather

    Aspen¶

    Retired Treewright

    Role: Resident · Age: Elder

    Talkative, nostalgic and stubborn about everything except his granddaughter.

    Aspen was a young man when the expedition came back with news of the Children of the Tree, and he tells the story of that day to anyone who sits still long enough. He swears the forest was quieter before the Age of Disturbance, and he is probably right.

    Relationships:

    • Lyra: granddaughter
    • Oriel: old friend
    • Sefa: friend

    Linnet¶

    Keeper of Living Homes

    Role: Craftsman · Age: Young

    Patient, practical and a little too serious for her age. She talks to the trees she tends as if they were old neighbours.

    Linnet keeps the living-tree homes along the Lantern Paths healthy, coaxing new rooms and mending storm damage. She is Oriel's apprentice and hopes one day to grow a home from a seedling herself.

    Relationships:

    • Oriel: mentor
    • Faelan: sweetheart

    Children's Glade¶

    Lumi¶

    Glade Dweller

    Role: Resident · Age: Young · Kind: Child of the Tree

    Endlessly curious and happily blunt, Lumi asks questions nobody else dares and laughs at most of the answers.

    Lumi joined the Grove School to learn the forest's ways and has become Liri's best friend. Lumi knows about Liri's plan to visit the Forest Edge and is torn between keeping the secret and telling Tansy.

    Relationships:

    • Liri: best friend
    • Sefa: friend

    Sefa¶

    Glade Storyteller

    Role: Performer · Age: Adult · Kind: Child of the Tree

    Warm, playful and a natural entertainer, Sefa loves an audience and loves listening even more.

    Sefa trades stories with the forest's children in the glade every afternoon, and with Aspen over tea. Sefa is gathering the forest's tales into one great collection, and refuses to say which stories are Sefa's own.

    Relationships:

    • Aspen: friend
    • Lumi: friend

    Niv¶

    Glade Dweller

    Role: Resident · Age: Young · Kind: Child of the Tree

    Shy, gentle and easily overwhelmed by crowds, Niv is fiercely loyal to the few people Niv trusts.

    Niv lives in a tree home Oriel tended and visits him almost daily. Niv often stands at the edge of the glade looking toward the World Tree and has never told anyone why.

    Relationships:

    • Oriel: friend
    • Halcy: guide

    Orla¶

    Glade Dweller

    Role: Resident · Age: Young · Kind: Child of the Tree

    Mischievous, quick-witted and never cruel, Orla thinks the forest takes itself far too seriously.

    Orla is the one playing tricks on Thorne at night, mostly moving his things a little to the left. Orla has decided Thorne needs to laugh more and will not stop until he does.

    Relationships:

    • Thorne: target

    Halcy¶

    Garland Weaver

    Role: Merchant · Age: Adult · Kind: Child of the Tree · Sells: Woven baskets and glade-flower garlands

    Kind, practical and welcoming, Halcy remembers everyone's name after one meeting.

    Halcy was among the first of the Children to settle in Fairy Forest and helps newer arrivals find their way. Calyra counts Halcy as a friend and sometimes asks for advice over a basket of flowers.

    Relationships:

    • Calyra: friend
    • Tansy: customer
    • Niv: friend

    Herb Gardens¶

    Hesper¶

    Mistress of the Herb Gardens

    Role: Healer · Age: Elder

    Blunt, kind and always smelling faintly of mint. She treats grown adults exactly the way she treats children with scraped knees.

    Hesper has tended the forest's healing gardens for most of her life and taught half its herbalists. More hurt travellers come to her each year from the roads, and she has started keeping a list of what attacked them.

    Relationships:

    • Celandine: apprentice
    • Merrow: gardener
    • Briony: friend

    Celandine¶

    Herbalist

    Role: Merchant · Age: Adult · Sells: Healing salves, herb teas and potions

    Gentle, orderly and a little anxious, she labels everything twice.

    Celandine runs the remedy stall by the garden gate and makes most of what she sells. She wants to find a plant that grows only near the Forest Edge, but her husband begs her not to go.

    Relationships:

    • Hesper: mentor
    • Merrow: husband

    Merrow¶

    Gardener

    Role: Worker · Age: Adult

    Big-hearted, slow-talking and fiercely protective of his beds of seedlings.

    Merrow does the digging, weeding and watering that keeps the gardens alive. He is proud of his wife's remedies and quietly afraid of the Forest Edge, where she keeps wanting to go.

    Relationships:

    • Hesper: employer
    • Celandine: wife
    • Ailo: friend

    Briony¶

    Grove Councillor

    Role: Council · Age: Elder

    Merry, gossipy and wiser than she lets on. She prefers to settle disagreements over tea rather than in the Council chamber.

    Briony taught herb-lore at the Grove School for decades and still spends more time in the gardens than at the Heart Tree. She is Thessaly's younger sister, and the only person who can make her laugh during a Council meeting.

    Relationships:

    • Thessaly: sister
    • Hesper: friend

    Ailo¶

    Garden Helper

    Role: Worker · Age: Adult · Kind: Child of the Tree

    Calm, unhurried and deeply content, Ailo can work for hours without a word and seems to enjoy every minute.

    Ailo helps in the Herb Gardens and has become close friends with Merrow. Hesper says the gardens have never grown so well, and Ailo only smiles when asked why.

    Relationships:

    • Merrow: friend

    Forest Edge¶

    Seren¶

    Warden of the Forest Edge

    Role: Guard · Age: Adult

    Steady, watchful and few-worded, she would rather turn a visitor back politely than draw a weapon.

    Seren commands the small watch at the forest's edge, which once only stopped visitors from taking more than the forest could spare. Now it also keeps monsters from the paths, and she has asked the Council more than once for more hands.

    Relationships:

    • Thorne: guard
    • Kestrel: friend
    • Juniper: friend

    Thorne¶

    Edge Guard

    Role: Guard · Age: Young

    Brash, brave and much too eager for a fight. He is a better guard than he looks and a worse one than he thinks.

    Thorne joined the edge watch to see some action and has seen plenty. He is convinced someone in the forest is playing tricks on him at night, and he is right.

    Relationships:

    • Seren: captain
    • Kestrel: rival
    • Orla: secret prankster

    Kestrel¶

    Wandering Magician

    Role: Adventurer · Age: Young

    Restless, cocky and loyal, she treats every monster on the road as a personal insult.

    Kestrel finished at the Grove School and went straight out onto the roads, clearing monsters from the paths between towns. She stops at the Forest Edge between journeys to trade news with the watch and tease Thorne.

    Relationships:

    • Alder: admirer
    • Seren: friend
    • Thorne: rival

    Juniper¶

    Wilds Naturalist

    Role: Adventurer · Age: Adult

    Curious, fearless and careless about her own safety, she sketches creatures while they are still trying to bite her.

    Juniper travels the wilds recording how creatures have changed during the Age of Disturbance, and brings her notes back for the seal scholars. Her notebooks are full, but nobody has yet found what the changes mean.

    Relationships:

    • Caelum: colleague
    • Seren: friend
