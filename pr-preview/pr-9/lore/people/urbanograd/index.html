    Great Hall¶

    Rook¶

    Caller of the Open Table

    Role: Ruler · Age: Adult

    Dry, patient and quick to laugh, with a voice that can cut through a shouting hall without ever sounding angry. Rook hates making speeches and loves listening to them.

    The Open Table chose Rook as this year's Caller, which means convening the Table, keeping order and making sure the quiet ones get a turn; it does not mean ruling anyone, and Rook reminds people of that often. Rook keeps up a long, warm exchange of letters with the capital and reads the funniest parts aloud at the Table.

    Relationships:

    • Dimity 'Inkpot' Ash: colleague at the Table
    • Bastian 'Bellows' Krell: favorite heckler
    • Quint: old friend and adviser
    • Mags: friend
    • Saucer: tea seller
    • Nana Gristle: scolding elder
    • Brindle: captain who reports to the Table
    • Aveline: frequent correspondent

    Dimity 'Inkpot' Ash¶

    Scribe of the Open Table

    Role: Official · Age: Adult

    Precise, ink-stained and secretly delighted by every argument. She writes down the jokes as carefully as the decisions.

    Dimity has kept the Table's record for twelve years, and her shelves hold every heckle and every vote. She is quietly worried that the oldest record books, from the first winters, are crumbling and nobody has copied them.

    Relationships:

    • Rook: colleague at the Table
    • Ozzie Lantry: fellow keeper of records

    Quint¶

    Keeper of the Charter Scroll

    Role: Official · Age: Elder

    Solemn about exactly one thing, the Open Charter, and cheerfully unserious about everything else.

    Quint guards the hall's copy of the Open Charter ruling and reads it aloud every Walkout Day morning before the play. He likes to remind young hotheads that the kingdom chose to welcome the Walkers, and that this is worth being proud of.

    Relationships:

    • Rook: friend
    • Ozzie Lantry: old friend
    • Dina Finch: director

    Bastian 'Bellows' Krell¶

    Loudest Voice at the Table

    Role: Council · Age: Adult

    Enormous voice, enormous opinions, enormous heart. He changes his mind loudly and without shame.

    Bastian has spoken at every Open Table for twenty years and has never once been short. This year he is campaigning, at length, for a covered bridge over Lantern Square so the night market can run in the rain.

    Relationships:

    • Rook: patient listener
    • Mags: wife

    Mags¶

    Cook of the Great Hall

    Role: Service · Age: Adult

    Brisk and bossy in the kitchen, soft as bread dough everywhere else. She judges people by whether they wash their own bowl.

    Mags feeds the Open Table, which can run until dawn, from one huge pot of stew that she claims has never been fully emptied since her grandmother's day. She would like someone to invent a stove that does not smoke.

    Relationships:

    • Rook: grateful regular
    • Bastian 'Bellows' Krell: husband
    • Old Parsnip: vegetable supplier

    Ozzie Lantry¶

    Historian of the Walkers

    Role: Scholar · Age: Elder

    Gentle, rambling and easily sidetracked by a good anecdote. He keeps three pairs of spectacles and can never find any of them.

    Ozzie collects letters, diaries and songs from the Walkers and their children, and is writing a history of Urbanograd's first hundred years. He is missing every account of the first winter and would pay well for anyone who finds one in an old attic.

    Relationships:

    • Dimity 'Inkpot' Ash: fellow keeper of records
    • Quint: old friend
    • Wendel 'Dog-Ear' Tuck: teasing bookseller

    Lantern Square¶

    Lux¶

    Keeper of the Lanterns

    Role: Official · Age: Adult

    Calm, watchful and fond of heights. Lux speaks softly and notices every lantern that has gone out.

    Lux climbs the towers every dusk to relight the lanterns strung between them, a job that has passed through a dozen hands since the Walkers hung the first ones. Lately some of the lanterns facing the wilds have been found smashed in the mornings, and Lux would like to know by what.

    Relationships:

    • Tinsel: glass supplier
    • Perch: friend
    • Prism: friend

    Jinx¶

    Juggler of Lantern Square

    Role: Performer · Age: Young

    Restless, cheeky and terrible at keeping secrets. Jinx drops things on purpose to make children laugh, and almost never by accident.

    Jinx juggles anything handed over, from apples to Margo's spare parts, and passes the hat afterwards. The dream is to juggle burning torches on top of the theatre cart on Walkout Day, which the troupe has firmly refused so far.

    Relationships:

    • Drum: street partner
    • Figgy: favorite juggler
    • Tinder: friend

    Drum¶

    Bucket Drummer

    Role: Performer · Age: Adult

    Says almost nothing and lets the rhythm talk. Drum smiles only when someone in the crowd starts dancing.

    Drum plays a ring of upturned buckets and pans every evening, and the night market sets its pace by the beat. Drum is trying to work out a rhythm loud enough to keep monsters away from the roads, half as a joke and half not.

    Relationships:

    • Jinx: street partner
    • Saffi: bandmate

    Saffi¶

    Fiddler of Lantern Square

    Role: Performer · Age: Young

    Fierce, fast and impatient with anything slow, including her own fingers. She laughs at her own mistakes before anyone else can.

    Saffi came from a family of respectable musicians who wanted her to play slow court dances, so she plays everything twice as fast. She is writing a tune for every street in the city and has finished nine.

    Relationships:

    • Piper: admirer
    • Drum: bandmate

    Ochre¶

    Muralist

    Role: Craftsman · Age: Adult

    Big-hearted and messy, always speckled with paint. Ochre talks to walls as if they were old friends.

    Ochre has painted half the walls around the square and repaints the oldest ones every spring so the Walkers' first pictures never fade. Ochre wants to paint the whole history of the city along the Gate Walk but cannot get enough red paint.

    Relationships:

    • Figgy: daughter
    • Splash: rival painter

    Figgy¶

    Chalk Artist

    Role: Child · Age: Child

    Bright, bossy and stubborn, with chalk dust up to the elbows. Figgy insists every drawing is finished only when it rains.

    Ochre's daughter draws on every flagstone in the square and charges passers-by one copper to step on her pictures. She wants a real wall of her own.

    Relationships:

    • Jinx: favorite audience
    • Ochre: mother

    Nana Gristle¶

    Bench-Sitter of Lantern Square

    Role: Resident · Age: Elder

    Sharp-tongued, sweet-toothed and impossible to shock. She knows everyone's grandparents and all their embarrassing stories.

    Nana Gristle's mother was carried out of Selsior in a basket on the day of the Long Walk, and Nana treats this as the city's most important fact. She sits on the same bench every day and tells newcomers which rooftops to avoid.

    Relationships:

    • Rook: fond listener
    • Saucer: old friend

    Ivo Sable¶

    Street Healer

    Role: Healer · Age: Adult

    Unhurried, gentle and very hard to rattle. He talks to patients about music to take their minds off the stitches.

    Ivo runs a little clinic under an awning on the square, mostly patching up rooftop tumbles and burnt fingers. More and more of his patients are travelers hurt on the roads, and he is running short of the bandages and salves that come in from outside.

    Relationships:

    • Crow: stubborn colleague
    • Patch: fellow healer
    • Tinder: frequent patient

    Tinder¶

    Fire-Eater

    Role: Performer · Age: Adult

    Swaggering and showy on stage, careful and nervous off it. Tinder checks the water bucket three times before every show.

    Tinder learned to eat fire from a traveling show and settled here because nobody told her to stop. She refuses to perform anywhere near the old stories of the Burning, which she says are bad luck.

    Relationships:

    • Jinx: friend
    • Ivo Sable: healer

    Workshops Row¶

    Margo 'Sparks' Venn¶

    Inventor of Sparking Things

    Role: Craftsman · Age: Adult · Sells: Gadgets, spring-traps and sparking lamps

    Brilliant, scatterbrained and slightly singed. Margo explains every invention three ways before she lets anyone touch it.

    Margo's workshop is the one with the smoke stains around the door. She is building a lamp that throws sparks to frighten off road monsters, and needs someone brave enough to test it outside the walls.

    Relationships:

    • Bolt: apprentice
    • Rivet: rival
    • Zinnia 'Zip' Calloway: gadget tester

    Cobble¶

    Bootmaker

    Role: Craftsman · Age: Elder · Sells: Boots, rooftop slippers and repairs

    Grumpy, honest and proud. Cobble says a boot should outlive its owner and means it.

    Cobble makes the soft-soled boots that rooftop runners swear by, and has made them for three generations of Kites. He wants an apprentice but has rejected every one so far for having the wrong sort of hands.

    Relationships:

    • Kite: loyal customer
    • Edgar Penhallow: would-be apprentice

    Hex¶

    Puzzle-Box Maker

    Role: Craftsman · Age: Adult · Sells: Puzzle boxes, locks and clever latches

    Sly, playful and secretive. Hex answers every question with a riddle unless you are buying something.

    Hex builds boxes that open only for the right sequence of taps and turns. One of Hex's earliest boxes was sold years ago and has come back unopened with a note saying the buyer could not solve it, and Hex cannot remember the trick either.

    Relationships:

    • Velvet: friend

    Bolt¶

    Tinker's Apprentice

    Role: Craftsman · Age: Young

    Eager, clumsy and full of ideas that almost work. Bolt apologizes to every machine he breaks.

    Bolt is Margo's apprentice and does most of the sweeping and all of the testing. He secretly thinks some of Rivet's ideas are better than Margo's and is afraid to say so.

    Relationships:

    • Margo 'Sparks' Venn: mentor

    Tinsel¶

    Glassblower

    Role: Craftsman · Age: Adult · Sells: Lantern glass, bottles and glass beads

    Dreamy, slow-spoken and fascinated by light. Tinsel holds every bit of glass up to the sun before selling it.

    Tinsel makes the colored lantern glass that gives Urbanograd its glow at night. Tinsel is trying to make a glass that glows on its own and so far has only made some very pretty paperweights.

    Relationships:

    • Lux: customer
    • Starling: friend

    Ferro¶

    Smith of Odd Metals

    Role: Craftsman · Age: Adult · Sells: Daggers, throwing stars, claws and knuckle guards

    Gruff, exacting and secretly sentimental. Ferro names every blade before it leaves the forge.

    Ferro used to make only metal sculptures, but with the roads getting worse the adventurers keep asking for weapons, so now Ferro makes those too and grumbles about it. Ferro still welds a sculpture every Walkout Day and gives it away.

    Relationships:

    • Shade: customer
    • Hatch: old customer

    Dyna¶

    Tailor of Loud Clothes

    Role: Merchant · Age: Adult · Sells: Patchwork coats, bright scarves and stage clothes

    Flamboyant, frank and generous with compliments. Dyna thinks beige is a moral failing.

    Dyna dyes cloth in colors no guild would allow and sells clothes stitched from a dozen fabrics at once. Visitors from the capital buy her coats to wear at home, which she finds very funny.

    Relationships:

    • Thimble: haggling customer

    Rivet¶

    Builder of the Rooftop Lift

    Role: Craftsman · Age: Young

    Cocky, competitive and very practical. Rivet would rather build something that works than something that sparkles.

    Rivet built the rope-and-pulley lift that hauls crates up to the Rooftops and wants to build a second one, if the Open Table agrees. Rivet and Margo argue about everything and would both be lost without each other.

    Relationships:

    • Margo 'Sparks' Venn: rival
    • Edgar Penhallow: admirer
    • Vane: partner on rooftop projects

    Edgar Penhallow¶

    Guild Apprentice from Selsior

    Role: Craftsman · Age: Young

    Polite, earnest and slowly loosening up. Edgar still says please to doors.

    Edgar's guild in Selsior sent him to study Urbanograd's workshops for a season, and he has filled four notebooks. He is beginning to suspect he does not want to go back, and has not written home about it.

    Relationships:

    • Rivet: friend
    • Cobble: unimpressed teacher

    Night Market¶

    Moth¶

    Keeper of the Night Market Stalls

    Role: Official · Age: Adult

    Wry, tireless and allergic to mornings. Moth knows which stall is where by smell alone.

    Moth assigns the stalls each evening by drawing names from a hat, the way the first traders did, so no one owns the best corner for long. Moth has noticed fewer traders arriving from outside since the roads turned dangerous and worries about the winter.

    Relationships:

    • Glim: daughter
    • Hester 'Ladle' Cray: friend

    Hester 'Ladle' Cray¶

    Skewer Seller

    Role: Merchant · Age: Adult · Sells: Grilled skewers, pepper buns and hot cider

    Loud, cheerful and always waving a ladle as if conducting a choir. She remembers every regular's order.

    Ladle's stall is the first one lit and the last one dark. She is quietly feeding a few rooftop children for free and pretends not to know it.

    Relationships:

    • Moth: friend
    • Scrap: grateful friend
    • Remy Marlo: stall neighbour

    Velvet¶

    Trader of Curios

    Role: Merchant · Age: Adult · Sells: Curios, trinkets and oddities from across Veligor

    Silky, mysterious and a little theatrical. Velvet tells a story with every sale, and some of the stories are true.

    Velvet buys odd things from travelers and sells them at a mark-up to anyone charmed enough. Among her stock is a dark stone that stays warm to the touch, bought from a frightened wanderer off the deep roads, and she will not say what she thinks it is.

    Relationships:

    • Hex: friend
    • Nix: friend

    Crow¶

    Tonic Seller

    Role: Merchant · Age: Adult · Sells: Healing tonics, mana draughts and remedies

    Deadpan, sharp and suspicious of anyone who haggles too sweetly. Crow wears black because it hides spills.

    Crow brews tonics in the back of the stall and sells them by lantern light. Ivo keeps telling Crow to label the bottles properly, and Crow keeps ignoring him.

    Relationships:

    • Ivo Sable: exasperated colleague

    Nix¶

    Mask Maker

    Role: Merchant · Age: Young · Sells: Masks, face paint and Walkout Day costumes

    Shy behind a counter, bold behind a mask. Nix speaks more easily in a voice that is not their own.

    Nix makes the paper masks everyone wears on Walkout Day, and this year has been asked to make a new Juna mask for the play. Nix is terrified of getting her face wrong.

    Relationships:

    • Velvet: friend
    • Pip Marrow: model

    Wendel 'Dog-Ear' Tuck¶

    Seller of Old Books and Banned Plays

    Role: Merchant · Age: Elder · Sells: Second-hand books, play scripts and song sheets

    Cheerfully disorganized and fiercely well-read. He folds down page corners and calls it a public service.

    Dog-Ear sells books that were once frowned on somewhere and are now simply old. He claims to own a script of Juna's play written in her own hand, and Ozzie has been trying to see it for ten years.

    Relationships:

    • Ozzie Lantry: frustrated would-be buyer

    Glim¶

    Paper Lantern Seller

    Role: Merchant · Age: Young · Sells: Paper lanterns and candle stubs

    Cheerful, chatty and a born salesperson. Glim can sell a lantern to someone already holding two.

    Moth's daughter runs her own little stall at the end of the market and is saving up to buy a cart. She wants to take her lanterns to other towns one day, if the roads ever get safer.

    Relationships:

    • Moth: father
    • Saucer: adopted grandparent

    Remy Marlo¶

    Smoked Fish Seller

    Role: Merchant · Age: Adult · Sells: Smoked fish, sea-salt crisps and pickled kelp

    Breezy, patient and homesick for the sound of waves. He hums sea songs when business is slow.

    Remy came inland from the coast two years ago because he fell in love with the night market on a visit. The fish deliveries are getting harder to bring in safely, and he is wondering whether to learn a new trade.

    Relationships:

    • Crumb: fellow newcomer
    • Hester 'Ladle' Cray: stall neighbour

    Saucer¶

    Tea Stall Keeper

    Role: Merchant · Age: Elder · Sells: Spiced tea, honey cakes and fortune leaves

    Serene, mischievous and full of terrible puns. Saucer reads tea leaves and always predicts something nice.

    Saucer has run the same tea stall for forty years and claims to have served tea to every Caller of the Open Table. Saucer is getting slow and hopes someone will take over the stall, but will not admit it.

    Relationships:

    • Rook: regular customer
    • Nana Gristle: old friend
    • Glim: helper

    Crumb¶

    Baker from the Farmlands

    Role: Merchant · Age: Adult · Sells: Bread, rolls and sweet buns

    Warm, sturdy and a little baffled by city life. Crumb still wakes at dawn even though he works all night.

    Crumb grew up on a farm in the green country and came to Urbanograd to bake bread shaped like whatever he liked. His dragon-shaped loaves are a hit, and his family back home still thinks it is a phase.

    Relationships:

    • Remy Marlo: fellow newcomer
    • Rue: friend from the farmlands

    Rooftops¶

    Vane¶

    Rooftop Bridge-Builder

    Role: Worker · Age: Adult

    Fearless, practical and calm in a high wind. Vane tests every plank by jumping on it.
