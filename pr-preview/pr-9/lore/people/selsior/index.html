    Palace¶

    Aveline¶

    Queen of Wonder Kingdom

    Role: Ruler · Age: Adult

    Warm, quick to laugh and very hard to fool. She listens to everyone to the end, then decides in one sentence.

    Crowned under the Great Bell, Aveline believes the crown's task is to keep the kingdom together, not to command it. She keeps a sworn alliance with Cavelor's Keeper of the Oath and trades long, argumentative letters with Urbanograd's Caller of the Open Table. Lately the reports of monsters on the royal roads keep her up at night.

    Relationships:

    • Leofric: husband
    • Elinor: daughter
    • Alaric: son
    • Osbert: trusted steward
    • Melisende: adviser
    • Marrun: sworn ally
    • Rook: frequent correspondent

    Leofric¶

    Queen's Consort

    Role: Council · Age: Adult

    Gentle, absent-minded and happiest with ink on his fingers. He forgets supper but never a road.

    Leofric was a road surveyor before he married the queen, and he still keeps a great map of the royal roads on the palace wall. Each week he marks where the patrols met monsters, and he does not like how the marks are spreading.

    Relationships:

    • Aveline: wife
    • Elinor: daughter
    • Alaric: son
    • Gervase: old colleague

    Elinor¶

    Princess of Wonder Kingdom

    Role: Resident · Age: Young

    Bold, restless and stubborn as a mule. She hates embroidery and loves anything with a blade or a horse in it.

    Elinor sneaks out at dawn to take sword lessons at the Knights' Court under a borrowed cloak. She wants to pass the Guard's trials like anyone else, and she is terrified her parents will find out before she does.

    Relationships:

    • Aveline: mother
    • Leofric: father
    • Alaric: brother
    • Sybil: secret keeper
    • Aylwin: sweetheart
    • Rowena: secret teacher

    Alaric¶

    Young Prince

    Role: Child · Age: Child

    Curious, chatty and always muddy. He asks why about everything and remembers every answer.

    Alaric spends more time in the palace gardens and kitchens than in his lessons. He collects beetles, keys and rumours, and he is sure there is a secret passage behind the throne hall tapestries.

    Relationships:

    • Aveline: mother
    • Leofric: father
    • Elinor: sister
    • Godwin: gardening friend
    • Peterkin: friend

    Osbert¶

    Royal Steward

    Role: Official · Age: Elder

    Precise, dry and secretly sentimental. He can tell how many candles burned last night by sniffing the hall.

    Osbert has run the palace household for more than thirty years and knows where every spoon and every secret is kept. He would like to retire, but he has not found anyone he trusts with the keys.

    Relationships:

    • Aveline: queen he serves
    • Cuthbert: cook
    • Agnes: maid
    • Peterkin: page

    Melisende¶

    Mistress of the Envoys' Hall

    Role: Official · Age: Adult

    Polished, patient and fluent in every town's manners. She never raises her voice and never loses an argument.

    Melisende hosts the envoys every town keeps at the palace, and she knows which ones want highland tea, which want sea salt on their bread and which want the windows open. She advises the queen whenever two towns quarrel over trade.

    Relationships:

    • Aveline: queen she advises
    • Petronella: friend

    Cuthbert¶

    Royal Cook

    Role: Service · Age: Adult

    Loud, red-faced and generous. He shouts at the pots and sings to the bread.

    Cuthbert cooks for the palace and for every envoy's feast, and he insists on learning each town's dishes properly. His dream is a great harvest feast with one dish from every town on a single table.

    Relationships:

    • Osbert: employer
    • Agnes: daughter

    Agnes¶

    Palace Maid

    Role: Service · Age: Young

    Cheerful, nosy and quick on her feet. She hears everything and repeats only half of it.

    Agnes grew up in her father's kitchen and now cleans the upper halls. She has noticed the princess's boots are muddy every morning and has said nothing, yet.

    Relationships:

    • Osbert: employer
    • Cuthbert: father

    Peterkin¶

    Palace Page

    Role: Child · Age: Child

    Eager, breathless and proud of his uniform. He runs everywhere, even when nobody is waiting.

    Peterkin carries messages inside the palace and dreams of becoming a royal courier one day. He practises by racing the clock tower bells from the cellars to the roof.

    Relationships:

    • Osbert: employer
    • Alaric: friend

    Godwin¶

    Palace Gardener

    Role: Worker · Age: Elder

    Slow, patient and full of proverbs. He talks to his roses more than to people.

    Godwin has tended the palace gardens since the old king's day. He grows a cutting from every town's favourite flower, and he worries that the wild bees have turned mean this year.

    Relationships:

    • Alaric: gardening friend

    Petronella¶

    Royal Scribe

    Role: Scholar · Age: Adult

    Neat, serious and fond of long words. She corrects other people's spelling out of kindness.

    Petronella copies every royal letter and decree into the palace books. She has the best handwriting in the kingdom and a secret ambition to write a history of her own.

    Relationships:

    • Melisende: friend
    • Benedict: former teacher

    Ansel¶

    Palace Physician

    Role: Healer · Age: Elder

    Calm, gentle and slightly deaf. He treats a queen and a scullery boy with the same cold hands.

    Ansel studied healing at the Scholars' Quarter and has served the palace for decades. More guards come to him with claw wounds every season, and he keeps careful notes on what bit them.

    Relationships:

    • Aldith: sister

    Sybil¶

    Royal Wardrobe Mistress

    Role: Service · Age: Adult

    Elegant, sharp-tongued and kind underneath. She can judge a person by their hem.

    Sybil keeps the royal family's clothes, from the coronation robes to the princess's riding boots. She lent Elinor the cloak she wears to the Knights' Court and pretends she has no idea where it went.

    Relationships:

    • Edith: sister
    • Elinor: mischief she covers for

    Plaza¶

    Perrin¶

    Royal Courier

    Role: Official · Age: Young · In the game: Perrin

    Fast-talking, self-important and always in a hurry. Letters wait for no one, and neither does he.

    Perrin carries the crown's letters to every town, to the highlands, the harbours and even the World Tree. He calls himself the fastest courier in the kingdom and has the blisters to prove it, though the monsters on the roads make him take the long way more often than he admits.

    Relationships:

    • Tilda: cousin

    Gideon¶

    Retired Herald

    Role: Resident · Age: Elder · In the game: Gideon

    Booming, nostalgic and a little lonely. He laughs loudest at his own stories.

    Gideon cried the king's news in the plaza for forty years and now lives in a perch above the square, where he can hear every bell in Selsior. Nobody climbs the ladder to hear him any more, and he would dearly love a visitor.

    Relationships:

    • Aldous: old friend
    • Clement: pupil

    Mirabel¶

    Dancer

    Role: Performer · Age: Young · In the game: Mirabel

    Dreamy, light-hearted and impossible to hurry. She dances for the pleasure of it, and the coins are a bonus.

    Mirabel dances by the Great Bell every evening and swears she hears it sing at dusk when the wind is kind. The Bell Warden insists it has not rung since the coronation, and the two of them have argued about it, happily, for years.

    Relationships:

    • Hugh: friend and sparring partner in argument
    • Fulke: musician who plays for her

    Clement¶

    Royal Herald

    Role: Official · Age: Adult

    Nervous in private and magnificent on the steps. He rehearses every proclamation to the pigeons first.

    Clement took over the herald's post when Gideon retired and still feels he is standing in a giant's boots. He climbs the ladder every week to ask the old man's advice and pretends it is only a social call.

    Relationships:

    • Gideon: mentor

    Hugh¶

    Warden of the Great Bell

    Role: Guard · Age: Elder

    Solemn, stubborn and proud of a duty most people forget. He polishes the bell's dais every morning.

    Hugh guards the Great Bell and tells anyone who asks that it rings only when a monarch is crowned, and for no one else. He has never admitted that on still evenings he sometimes thinks he hears it hum.

    Relationships:

    • Mirabel: friend and sparring partner in argument
    • Baldwin: brother

    Emmeline¶

    Flower Seller

    Role: Merchant · Age: Young · Sells: Flowers, garlands and festival ribbons

    Sunny, flirtatious and quick with a compliment. She can sell a posy to a man on his way to buy bread.

    Emmeline sells flowers on the plaza steps and makes the garlands for every festival in the square. She is saving to open a real shop, and she is courting the juggler who performs beside her stall.

    Relationships:

    • Lambert: sweetheart

    Lambert¶

    Street Juggler

    Role: Performer · Age: Young

    Showy, cheeky and kind to children. He never drops a ball unless it makes the crowd laugh.

    Lambert learned juggling from a puppeteer on Theatre Row and now draws the biggest crowds in the plaza. He wants to juggle for the queen at the next royal festival.

    Relationships:

    • Emmeline: sweetheart
    • Rafe: teacher

    Piers¶

    Pie Seller

    Role: Merchant · Age: Adult · Sells: Meat pies, apple pasties and hot cider

    Jolly, round and full of gossip. He knows who bought what pie and why.

    Piers sells pies from a cart by the palace steps and claims his recipe is older than the Great Bell. He is raising his daughter alone and hopes she will take over the cart, though she would rather run off with the circus.

    Relationships:

    • Mab: daughter

    Baldwin¶

    Lamplighter

    Role: Worker · Age: Elder

    Quiet, unhurried and fond of the dark hours. He knows every lamp in the city by its flicker.

    Baldwin lights the plaza lamps at dusk and walks the long way home through the old quarters lighting more. He has started leaving extra lamps burning near the city gate, just in case.

    Relationships:

    • Hugh: brother
    • Gilbert: supplier

    Mab¶

    Pie Seller's Daughter

    Role: Child · Age: Child

    Bold, giggly and fearless. She tries every trick she sees the street performers do.

    Mab helps at her father's pie cart when she has to and practises cartwheels by the Great Bell when she can. She wants to be the most famous performer in Selsior.

    Relationships:

    • Piers: father
    • Hodge: friend

    Fulke¶

    Ballad Singer

    Role: Performer · Age: Adult

    Charming, theatrical and a terrible liar. Every ballad he sings is mostly true.

    Fulke plays the lute for Mirabel's dances and sings ballads of the Balrog War to anyone with a coin. He collects new songs from travellers at the gate and wants to write one that the whole kingdom sings.

    Relationships:

    • Mirabel: dancer he plays for
    • Cecily: pupil

    Knights' Court¶

    Rhys¶

    Captain of the Royal Guard

    Role: Guard · Age: Adult · In the game: Rhys

    Steady, demanding and fair. He believes a sword is only as good as the arm behind it, and says so often.

    Rhys trains the Guard's warriors in the Swordsman's way and sets the trials for every rank on the road to Swordmaster and beyond. In his youth he trained alongside an Uskari warrior of Midbarium, and he still says no one ever pushed him harder. Since the Age of Disturbance began he has more recruits than ever, and less time to make them ready.

    Relationships:

    • Pim: recruit
    • Tobe: recruit
    • Avice: recruit
    • Rowena: fellow instructor
    • Aubrey: former teacher
    • Ida: night watch guard
    • Tavi: old training partner

    Pim¶

    Recruit

    Role: Guard · Age: Young · In the game: Pim

    Keen, clumsy and endlessly cheerful. He asks for one more round of drills even when he can barely stand.

    Pim joined the Guard the day he was old enough and salutes everyone, just in case. Captain Rhys says his guard is full of holes, and Pim is determined to close every one of them.

    Relationships:

    • Rhys: captain
    • Tobe: friend
    • Avice: rival
    • Bertrand: drill sergeant

    Tobe¶

    Recruit

    Role: Guard · Age: Young · In the game: Tobe

    Thoughtful, bookish and a little anxious. He knows the manual by heart and his arms do not.

    Tobe read every book on swordsmanship in the Scholars' Quarter before he came to the Court, and none of them mentioned how heavy swords are. He is still stuck on the third cut.

    Relationships:

    • Rhys: captain
    • Pim: friend
    • Mariota: sister

    Avice¶

    Recruit

    Role: Guard · Age: Young

    Fierce, proud and short with words. She trains harder when anyone is watching.

    Avice walked to the capital from the highlands to try the Guard's trials, and she means to be the first of her year to earn the Swordsman's rank. She has noticed that the cloaked stranger who trains at dawn holds a sword like someone raised with fine manners.

    Relationships:

    • Rhys: captain
    • Pim: rival

    Bertrand¶

    Drill Sergeant

    Role: Guard · Age: Adult

    Gruff, bellowing and fond of every recruit he shouts at. He has a voice that could crack a shield.

    Bertrand runs the dawn drills and swears he can turn anyone into a guard given a year and enough porridge. He keeps a list of every recruit he has trained and checks it against the patrol reports.

    Relationships:

    • Pim: recruit
    • Helewise: wife

    Rowena¶

    Swordmaster of the Guard

    Role: Guard · Age: Adult

    Cool, precise and sparing with praise. When she smiles, recruits remember it for a week.

    Rowena earned the Swordmaster's rank young and teaches the Court's advanced classes. She agreed to give a cloaked stranger private lessons at dawn without asking her name, and she knows exactly whose daughter she is.

    Relationships:

    • Rhys: captain
    • Elinor: secret pupil

    Edric¶

    Lieutenant of the Road Patrol

    Role: Guard · Age: Adult

    Weathered, plain-spoken and tired in a way sleep does not fix. He jokes to keep his patrols calm.

    Edric leads the patrols that ride the royal roads between the towns. Every season there are more monsters and bolder ones, and he wants the Court to train faster than the wilds grow worse.

    Relationships:

    • Aylwin: patrol guard
    • Tristan: brother
    • Merrick: friend
    • Gervase: friend

    Helewise¶

    Guard Quartermaster

    Role: Official · Age: Adult

    Practical, thrifty and unshakeable. She knows how many arrows the Guard has to the last fletching.

    Helewise feeds, arms and clothes the Royal Guard, and she haggles with the Crown Market's armourers as if the gold were her own. Patrols are wearing out boots faster than she can buy them.

    Relationships:

    • Bertrand: husband
    • Vessa: armourer

    Wystan¶

    Stablemaster

    Role: Worker · Age: Elder

    Grumbling, gentle with animals and rough with people. He trusts a horse's opinion over a captain's.
