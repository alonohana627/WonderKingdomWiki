    Quests of Selsior¶

    Selsior has 207 quests, grouped below into quest lines in the order they are played. A line is a chain of quests that each need the one before.

    The Swordsman's Trial¶

    Story line, 1 quest, from level 30. Starts with Rhys.

    # Quest Level Giver Objectives Rewards
    1 The Swordsman's Trial 30 Rhys Defeat 30 Downs monsters (Downs Boar, Chalk Hive Bee); Deliver 5 Guard's Trial Seal 4,000 EXP

    Story line, 1 quest, from level 70. Starts with Rhys.

    # Quest Level Giver Objectives Rewards
