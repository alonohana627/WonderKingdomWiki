    Elite and Epic monsters¶

    Each defeat raises the chance that the next monster to appear is Elite by 0.2% (up to 5%). After 3 elites, an Epic monster appears.

    Elite Epic
    Modifiers 1 2
    HP multiplier x8 x30
    EXP multiplier x6 x25
    Touch multiplier x2 x3
    Attack multiplier x2 x3
    Defense multiplier x2 x3
    Extra drop rolls 1 2
    Meso multiplier x3 x8

    Modifiers an elite can roll:

    • Elemental: one of fire, ice, lightning, poison
    • Shielded: a shield of 40% of its HP that refills after 5 s without being hit
    • Regenerating: heals 2% of its HP per second after 3 s without being hit
    • Shockwave: a ground slam for 150% damage every 5 s
    • Swift: moves at 140% speed
