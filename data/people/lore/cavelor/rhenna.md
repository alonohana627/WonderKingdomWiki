Rhenna is nine years old, the daughter of Ysra, Healer of the Halls, and she has grown up in the Oath Hall, among the healer's cots and the long benches where the Wardens gather between shifts. She is shy, thoughtful and serious beyond her years. She already knows the whole Oath Below by heart, every line of it, and she can recite it faster than most adults and more correctly than some.

### A child of the Oath Hall

The Oath Hall is the heart of Cavelor, where Keeper Marrun listens and decides, where the Oath is sworn and sung, and where Ysra tends to the Wardens who come up from the Deep Watch with scrapes, burns and the bone-weariness of long shifts. Rhenna has spent her whole life watching tired Wardens come in and rested ones go out. She fetches water and clean cloths for her mother, sits very still while Ysra hums the old Oath songs over a burn, and listens to everything the Wardens say when they think no child is paying attention.

She knows more than she lets on. She knows which Wardens are limping and pretending not to. She knows that more of them come to her mother than used to, because she has counted, the way Captain Brask counts things. She has not told anyone that she counts. She is not sure whether it is allowed.

### Learning the Oath

Rhenna learned the Oath from Annick, the Singer of the Oath, who teaches the old songs to every child in Cavelor. Annick says Rhenna is the best pupil she has had in years, and is trying very hard to teach her to sing loudly. Rhenna knows every word and every note and sings all of them in a voice like a moth's wings. Annick says it will come. Rhenna is not convinced, but she practises anyway, alone, in the empty corners of the Archive stairs where the echo is kind.

She is also practising standing very straight. She stands straight while she waits for her mother, straight at the back of the Oath Hall when Marrun speaks, straight while she eats her supper. When she is grown she will take her first turn as a Warden, like every adult in Cavelor, and she wants to be ready. She has decided that a Warden who stands straight will not be afraid, and so she has been standing straight for years, just in case.

### Ottel

Her best friend is Ottel, Brask and Halvara's son, a year younger and her opposite in every way: loud where she is quiet, stubborn where she is careful, fearless about everything. They play at Wardens in the Root Gardens, and Rhenna always insists on doing it properly, with real roster turns and the full Oath at the start of each shift. Ottel complains, and then does it anyway. She privately thinks he will make a very good Warden one day, if he ever stops arguing.

### The sky

Rhenna has never been to the surface, and she is a little afraid of it. When Doran came back last autumn talking about the sky, she listened to every word and lay awake afterwards. A sky, he said, has no roof. It goes up forever. Rhenna cannot imagine standing under something that goes up forever and not falling into it. Ottel says the whole thing is made up. Rhenna knows it is not, which is worse.

She has never said this to her mother. She thinks a girl who knows the whole Oath ought not to be afraid of the sky.

### Hooks

A quest might need someone who knows the Oath word for word, and find a nine-year-old who does. One day, someone might take Rhenna up the Upper Stair and stand beside her while she looks at the sky for the first time.
