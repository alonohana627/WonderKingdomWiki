Tavi has never been good at sitting still. Born on the Uskari Rim, Tavi learned to climb the canyon walls before learning to read, and left home at seventeen, like so many young Uskari, to see what the rest of Veligor had to offer. Tavi is thirty-nine now, chieftain of the Uskari for six years, and still restless, direct and fiercely competitive. Tavi would rather walk the trail than talk about it, and hates losing at anything.

### The season in the capital

At eighteen, Tavi walked into the Knights' Court in Selsior and asked to train with the capital's swordsmen. The Guard is open to anyone who can keep up, and the old Grand Swordmaster who ran the yard then took one look at Tavi and paired the newcomer with a nineteen-year-old recruit named Rhys, who was every bit as fierce and every bit as stubborn.

From spring until the first frosts of autumn, Tavi and Rhys fought a bout every evening after drill, eighty in all, and scratched the tally into a post in the practice yard. When Tavi left in the autumn, it stood at forty-one to thirty-nine in Tavi's favour, and Tavi has reminded Rhys of it in every letter since. Tavi left Selsior before the Guard's trials and took no rank, wanting the sword's lessons, not its titles. Tavi still fights with the capital's controlled cuts mixed into the Uskari way of the hunt, and says no one before or since has made Tavi work as hard as Rhys did.

They write once a year, around midwinter, and have met only a handful of times. The last was three years ago, when Tavi came down to Selsior with a party from Midbarium and the two of them fought one more bout in the old yard. The recruits' drill bell rang before either won. Both agree the bout is unfinished; Tavi is certain who would have won it, and suspects Rhys is equally certain of the opposite.

### Chieftain of the Uskari

After two more years of adventuring, Tavi came home to hunt, and spent the next years tracking beasts along the canyon rims, first under old Chieftain Orvek and then under Hask. Six years ago, when Hask was chosen Old One, the Uskari gathered around their fire the same night and chose Tavi to lead them. Hask and Tavi have always got on well; Hask is the patient one and Tavi the impatient one, and each finds the other useful. Hask worries that Tavi still walks the worst stretch of trail personally. Tavi has no intention of stopping.

Since becoming chieftain, Tavi has doubled the trail patrols. Skarra, captain of the trail watch, is Tavi's friendly rival in everything from climbing races to arguments about patrol routes, and the two of them keep each other sharp. Ketta, who came home last spring with a new scar and a pack full of stories, worries Tavi more than any monster, because she will tell every story but one, and Tavi knows that kind of silence from the inside.

Tavi and Sennu of the Tolmach are uneasy allies on the trails. Tavi wants the herds close to town, where the patrols can guard them; Sennu wants the patrols pushed farther out so the herds can return to the high pastures. Each respects the other. Neither has given ground.

### What Tavi wants

Tavi wants the trails safe enough that the patrols can go back to being hunters. Tavi fears that will not happen, and that the patrols are already stretched as thin as they can go. The monsters are creeping closer every season, Skarra's hide map says so, and Tavi hates nothing more than a fight that cannot be won by walking harder.

A quest might send someone along the worst stretch of trail with Tavi, find out what Ketta saw in the deep canyon cave, or carry a challenge down to the Knights' Court to finish an old bout.
