Keziah is a sword for hire, and she has been in Bain Harbour since the end of last winter. She is twenty-three, cool, careful and quietly funny, and she counts the exits whenever she walks into a room. She sits with her back to the wall in the Salt and Bread, says little, and makes the people at her table laugh about once an hour with a single dry remark that they repeat for days.

### From inland

Keziah grew up on a farm in the orchard country outside Kaldemor, where her parents still grow apples and have long since stopped asking when she is coming home. She left at seventeen to guard carts and caravans on the roads, which grew more dangerous every year, and she has walked most of the inland roads of Veligor at the side of somebody else's wagon. She learned her trade from the roads: to watch the treeline rather than the road, to sleep lightly, and to know where the way out is before she needs it.

Last winter she heard in a roadside inn that the captains of Bain Harbour were paying adventurers to sail with them, and paying well. She had never seen the sea. She walked to the coast, stood on the docks for a whole afternoon looking at it, and signed on with the first captain who would have her.

### The Bright Morrow

That captain was Seraphine of the Bright Morrow, the fastest trading ship in the harbour, who hires adventurers for every voyage and pays them better than anyone. Keziah likes her: Seraphine is bold where Keziah is careful, and each finds the other useful. Keziah has sailed on three voyages with the Bright Morrow so far. Seraphine has twice told her she is the best hire she has ever had, and once asked her to sign on for the whole summer.

The trouble is that Keziah gets terribly seasick, and she has told no one, least of all Seraphine. She is sick from the moment the ship clears the harbour wall until the moment it ties up again, and she has become very skilled at being sick quietly over the side when no one is looking. Before every voyage she slips round to the back door of Clemence the healer at dawn for a bottle of seasickness tonic, which helps a little. Clemence has never told anyone, and teases her only gently. Keziah is afraid that if Seraphine finds out, she will be put ashore, and she has nowhere she would rather be than this harbour, which is the strangest thing she has ever discovered about herself.

### Tristram

Her partner in most things on shore is Tristram, the harpooner, who sails as Captain Corwin's regular hire on the Heron. They met in her first week on the Waterfront, when both were turned down by the same captain on the same morning, and took a job together that afternoon clearing the rocks below the headland of crabs grown big enough to take a boot off. Tristram can wait for hours without moving and Keziah never stops watching, and between them nothing gets close. He laughs rarely, but when Keziah makes him laugh he laughs completely, and she counts it as a small victory every time.

Tristram has, she suspects, noticed the seasickness. He has said nothing, which is exactly why she trusts him.

### What she wants and fears

Keziah wants to earn enough to stop guarding other people's cargo one day. She fears being sick at the wrong moment, at the rail, when something comes up out of the deep water and the ship needs her sword.

### Hooks

A quest could find a cure for seasickness better than Clemence's tonic, sail on the Bright Morrow in Keziah's place when she cannot face it, or keep her secret when Seraphine starts asking questions.
