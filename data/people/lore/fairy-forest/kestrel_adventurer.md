Kestrel is a wandering Magician, which is a polite way of saying she is almost never anywhere for long. She is twenty-three, restless, cocky and fiercely loyal, and she treats every monster on the road as a personal insult. Where other travellers see a dangerous stretch of path and go around it, Kestrel sees an affront to be corrected, and corrects it, usually with more fire than strictly necessary.

### From the orchards to the Grove

Kestrel was not born in the forest. She grew up in an orchard family on the farmland near Kaldemor, where her parents still grow apples and still write to ask when she is coming home for the harvest. At ten she set a hayrick alight by sneezing near it, and the family decided, after the fire was out, that the Grove School might be a better place for her than the orchard. The forest's schools teach anyone with the gift, from any town in the kingdom, and the forest took her in.

She was not a model student. She loved the spellwork and loathed the listening lessons, and she spent a great many mornings sitting under a tree being told to hear it, while hearing only how much she would rather be somewhere else. She learned anyway, in the end, mostly so she could stop being made to sit there. She finished at nineteen and went straight out onto the roads the same week.

### The roads

For four years now Kestrel has walked the paths between the towns, clearing monsters from them. Nobody pays her to do it, though merchants and carters often buy her supper. She does it because the roads were safe once, according to everyone old enough to remember, and she cannot see why they should not be safe again. She knows the bad stretches better than almost anyone alive, and which creatures have turned up where they never used to be.

Between journeys she stops at the Forest Edge to rest, trade news and eat someone else's cooking. Seren, the Warden of the Edge, is her friend, one of the few people whose advice she actually takes. Kestrel brings Seren reports from the roads; Seren gives her a bed in the watch hut and tells her, in very few words, which of her plans are foolish.

### Thorne

Thorne, one of Seren's edge guards, is her rival, and Kestrel would not have it otherwise. He is brash and eager for a fight, and so is she, which is exactly the trouble. They argue over who has seen worse monsters, who reacted faster, and whose method of dealing with them is less reckless. Kestrel teases him every time she visits. Lately he has been complaining that someone keeps moving his things in the night, and he half suspects her. She is not the culprit, and has told him so truthfully, and enjoys very much that he does not believe her.

### Admirers

Alder, a student from a harbour town, has decided Kestrel is a hero. He follows her around the Edge whenever she visits, asking about the roads, and she is far more flattered than she lets him see. She knows he dreads the journey home at the end of term. She has been thinking about offering to walk him there, and has not yet worked out how to say it without sounding kind.

### What she wants and fears

Kestrel wants the roads clear, and she wants to be the one who clears them. She fears, though she would never say it, that she is only holding back a tide: that every monster she drives off is replaced by two, and that the thing making them is far beyond the reach of any Magician with a staff.

A quest might send a traveller out with her to clear a stretch of road, ask them to carry her report back to Seren when she is too stubborn to come in wounded, or help her escort a nervous student home.
