Isaura is Keeper of the Old Records, and the Heart Tree's oldest secrets sleep in rooms she holds the keys to. She guards the forest's most ancient Magician writings, including what little remains about how the great seal was made, and she leads the scholars who study it. She is stern, careful and secretive, and those who know her best say the secrecy is duty rather than nature. She is gentler with her granddaughter Wynne than with anyone else alive, and Wynne is the only person who can make her put down her pen in the middle of a page.

### Fifty years of reading

Isaura is seventy-two. She came to the Heart Tree's record rooms at twenty-two, as reader and assistant to old Keeper Anselm, in the same summer that the Great World Tree Expedition came home. She remembers the forest gathering under the Heart Tree to talk about the Children, and Anselm muttering that there would be nothing in the records about them, and being right.

What nobody yet knew that summer was that the expedition had hurt the seal. When the wilds began to change, and it became clear what had happened, the forest turned to its records, and the records turned out to be thinner than anyone had hoped. The Magicians who made the seal had guarded the knowledge so closely that much of it was never shared even with their own people. Isaura became Keeper when Anselm died thirty years ago, and she has led the seal scholars ever since.

Fifty years of reading have taught her a great deal about the seal: what it was meant to hold, roughly where its strength was placed, which old Magicians worked on it and what they argued about. They have taught her nothing yet about how to strengthen it. Lately she has begun to suspect that some of the oldest pages were never written down at all: that the most important parts of the making were passed from one Magician to another by word of mouth, and that the last mouth closed a very long time ago. It is not a thought she shares with her scholars. It would take the heart out of them.

### The people she trusts

Calyra, the Eldest, is her confidante. They have known each other most of their lives, and late at night the two of them talk in the innermost record room of the Heart Tree, where nobody else is allowed to bring a lantern. Isaura tells Calyra things she tells no one else, including her doubts. Calyra listens, as she always has, and says very little, which Isaura finds a comfort.

Caelum has been her student for twenty years. He is the most brilliant scholar she has trained and the one most likely to wear himself to nothing, and she is harder on him than on anyone because she fears for him most. She has never told him that his drawer of wrong theories is the most honest work in the Heart Tree.

Wynne came to live with her five years ago, at fourteen, from Selsior, where Wynne's mother, Isaura's daughter Mirren, lives with her husband and keeps a bookbinder's shop. Isaura's husband Corvin died fifteen years ago, and the house had been very quiet until Wynne came. Wynne now copies crumbling records onto fresh paper so the originals can rest. Isaura does not know that Wynne has found a gap in the numbering of one old seal record, as though pages were taken out long ago. If she knew, it might bear on her suspicion in a way she has not considered.

### What she fears

Isaura fears that the answer was lost before she was born. She fears even more that it was not lost, but hidden, and that she has spent fifty years reading around a hole she should have seen.

A quest might ask a traveller to retrieve an old record from a ruined Magician site, to help Wynne decide whom to tell, or to carry a sealed letter from Isaura to Calyra that must not be opened on the way.
