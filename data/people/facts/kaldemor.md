# Established facts: kaldemor

Written by NPC lore writers so later writers stay consistent. Not shown on the wiki.

## Nell (nell)

- Age 8. Only child of Hob and Mabel; born in the family's house near the windmills in the Park.
- Tally book: stitched by Hob from flour-sack wrapping paper; one line per day; total kept secret (only Orin has seen the last page and says he could not read it without glasses).
- Wren's cap is soft blue with a lopsided bobble; Nell wears it to bed.
- Tobin tries to guess the total about once a month.
- Hook: on three evenings this summer she drew a low grey "cloud" above the woods past the hills that did not move and was gone by morning; shown to no one.

## Tam (tam)

- Age 9. Grew up on Wilbur and Hattie's farm east of the square.
- His stick is named Shellbreaker; he patrols the pumpkin rows with it every morning.
- Was there when the snails ate Wilbur's prize pumpkin (week before last autumn's fair); put his head through the hole.
- Once knocked off Tobin's hat with the stick.
- Quill always tells him "when you are bigger"; shouts footwork lessons louder when Tam watches.
- Thinks of Digby as a brother in arms because Digby worked on his father's farm.
- Wears Wren's cap only when nobody from the Gauntlet is looking.

## Fen (fen)

- Age 8. Born in a small round freckled mushroom house at the end of the lane from the Park to the Workshops. Her family is deliberately left unstated.
- Measures her "adopted" mushroom houses each spring with knotted cords, one per house, marked with coloured thread. Biggest grower last year: the tall yellow house by the Market steps; least: the one with the green door.
- Bertie keeps promising she may fit a window "next spring".
- Painted one shutter for Daisy, an unnameable colour.
- Carries flour twice a week from Hob's windmills to Rosa's oven; paid in buns and sometimes the heel of the loaf.
- The clock tower stopped this spring (late spring); the silence frightened her.
- Hook: the squirrel behind the big birch in the woods really vanishes (hole/door/unknown).

## Hob (hob)

- Age 44. Orin and Wren's only child.
- Began carrying sacks for the old miller Tibbet (dead) at 14; ran the gears and stones by 20; took over the mills when Tibbet died.
- Married Mabel 12 years ago; met over a broken flour crock (he then broke another on purpose). Their house stands near the windmills.
- Orin's joke: Hob "fells wheat instead of trees".
- Grinds Rosa's flour first every morning; grinds the harvest grain for the Bain Harbour carts each autumn.
- Twice this spring, on windless mornings, his sails shuddered and the stones rattled as if the ground shrugged; he has told no one, checks the foundations daily.

## Rosa (rosa)

- Age 45. Never married, no children; no living family.
- Grandmother Ada (dead) built the terrace oven as a young woman newly come to Kaldemor, the year the town raised the clock tower. Rosa's mother (unnamed, dead) kept it 30 years after Ada and died 16 years ago; Rosa has run it alone since.
- Bakery is at the top of the Market steps ("terrace oven"). Tobin eats her mushroom bread every day.
- Bakes a special loaf for Tobin and Quenna's autumn price-settling at the harvest fair.
- Quarrel with Bram: 9 years ago, the morning of the harvest fair, Bram added his own spore tincture to a batch of her mushroom bread; it came out greenish and bitter in front of the fair. She barred him from her kitchen; he said he was right in principle. Before that he ate at her table weekly.
- She still sets aside the heel on Bram's old weekly day and gives it to the birds.
- While Wilf was away she had a young adventurer fetch spores and blue snail shells.

## Ewan (ewan)

- Age 72. Friends with Tobin since boyhood (they were chased out of the tower together as boys).
- Watched the works as a boy (Absalom let him stay after the tower chase); apprenticed at 22, 10 years under old clockkeeper Absalom (dead); became keeper at 32 when Absalom died; keeper 40 years. Never married.
- Absalom left him a brass pocket watch; Ewan gave it to the young adventurer who returned the gear.
- The clock stopped late this spring (first time ever); a Shroom left damp round tracks to the window. Lia spread the news before breakfast. No bell for nine nights; Tobin sat up with him all nine; gear returned on the tenth day by a young adventurer.
- Garrick once waved at him through the clock face; Ewan has written to Tobin about Garrick four times.
- Knows the ladder rung was cracked last spring and badly mended; suspects Archie (who goes pale) but has not confronted him.
- Has seen Alfie's carving in the treehouse beam; told no one, not even Tobin; goes back to look at it.

## Orin (orin)

- Age 78. Woodcutter family (father and grandfather woodcutters, dead). Took up the axe at 18; felled trees in the woods past the hills (west) for 40 winters; retired 20 years ago at 58 because of his back.
- Married Wren 53 years ago, in the Park under the clock tower. Hob is their only child.
- Claims he chose Wren because she alone laughed at his stories; she says she was laughing at something else.
- Ned: friends since boyhood; argue over a race neither remembers.
- Asked Wren loudly for nine days running whether she heard the clock had stopped.
- Hook: 40 years ago he cut his sign into a great old oak in the woods, the finest tree he left standing.

## Wren (wren)

- Age 75. Daughter of a shepherd (parents dead), grew up in a cottage by the farms.
- Knits a new cap for every Kaldemor child each autumn before the frosts. Nell's: soft blue, lopsided bobble. Fen has asked for one with a pocket for squirrels.
- Yarn from Winnie (spun from Dottie's wool); she and Winnie have noticed Dottie's worry.
- Began sitting in the Park afternoons when Orin retired (20 years ago).
- Hook: knitting an oversized grey cap "for someone who will need it before the winter".

## Wilbur (wilbur)

- Age 43. Farm east of the square, in the family four generations; father Amos (dead, died 9 years ago, the autumn Tam was born) and Amos's mother farmed it before him.
- Married Hattie 11 years ago (her hives stand at the edge of his fields).
- Has sold to Old Marta for more than 20 years; she slips something extra in his basket for Tam every year.
- This year's prize pumpkin sleeps under a net inside a ring of crushed shell; he sometimes sits up with a lantern.
- Has seen snails coming from the meadow in lines, "as if driven"; told Hale, who only nodded.
- Gave Digby his blessing and a pair of Ned's boots when Digby left last year; still pays him for harvest help.

## Bram (bram)

- Age 51. Unmarried, no children. Only sibling: Bess (older).
- Learned brewing from the old potion seller who kept the stall before him (unnamed, gone); has kept the stall more than 20 years. Striped awning at the far end of the Market.
- Hears news of Rosa through Wilf and Fen.
- Quarrel with Rosa: see Rosa (9 years ago, the spore tincture). Privately knows he was the stubborn one.
- When a bad bite comes in, Bess and Bram stop arguing and work side by side.

## Digby (digby)

- Age 19. Born on a small farm far out in the eastern countryside, past the end of the Royal Road; fourth of six children. Parents and five siblings (unnamed) still live there and play no part; he sends money each autumn with carters heading east.
- Walked to Kaldemor at 14; Wilbur took him in (bed in the barn loft). Farmhand 4 years; left last year to become a warrior; still helps (paid) at harvest.
- Passed Quill's monster yard on his second try; has failed the hanging course 11 times; practises on the fence rails behind Wilbur's barn.
- Rivalry with Meg: Meg passed the hanging course first try; Meg once fell in the Park fountain. Each has quietly seen the other home safe from the meadow at least once.

## Hale (hale)

- Age 47. Born in Kaldemor, son of a hedger (dead) who kept the field boundaries east of the square. No spouse or children.
- Ages 22-28: hired caravan guard on the Royal Road (Selsior to the eastern countryside), paired with Quill from his first trip; six years together. Each thinks he saved the other's life at least once; they disagree which time.
- Tobin asked him home at 29; park ranger for 18 years. Quill came to Kaldemor later ("in time"; exact year left open) to run the Gauntlet.
- Pays the bounty from the town purse and sometimes his own.
- One night this spring he heard something huge settle in a hollow past the woods (grinding, like a cart of stone tipped out); told no one until a young adventurer reached the forest edge; then identified it as the old giant snail Mano, "the Snail King", and told Tobin.
- Knows Meg has been past the hills farther than she admits; waiting for her to tell him.

## Mabel (mabel)

- Age 39. Born in Kaldemor, daughter of a thatcher and his wife (both dead).
- Apprenticed at 15 to Primrose (Tobin's late wife, a potter) in the pottery on the Workshops lane; worked under her 12 years; ran the wheel in Primrose's last, ill year; inherited the workshop when Primrose died 12 years ago.
- Met Hob in Primrose's last year (the broken crock); married him a few months after Primrose died. Moved into Hob's house near the windmills.
- Keeps one of Primrose's jugs on the top shelf, never sold or used. Tobin sometimes sits on the stool by her door.
- Fills the pottery crates for the Bain Harbour harvest carts; lost a season of blue-glazed bowls on last autumn's missing cart.
- Sends Nell's supper out to the square in a covered bowl. Has noticed Hob checking the foundations; has not asked.
- Hook: one windless morning this summer her jugs crept to the edge of the drying shelf and one fell; she blamed the mill. Tobin has asked her for great crocks to keep his fence's ground shell dry.

## Quill (quill)

- Age 49. Grew up in Selsior, son of a lamplighter and a laundress (both dead). No spouse or children.
- Went out as a caravan guard at 22; had been on the road 2 years when paired with Hale on Hale's first trip (Quill 24). Six years together.
- Quill's version of a life saved: a flooded ford, a wolf, Hale standing in the wrong place. Hale's version is a different night. Still unresolved.
- Stayed on the road 9 more years after Hale went home (guard, then trainer of caravan guards).
- Came to Kaldemor 10 years ago (age 39) at Hale's short letter; Tobin pays him. Designed the Gauntlet; Alfie's carpenters built it to his drawings.
- Has never told Meg she is good. Since learning of the hollow, trains the last stand longer than one minute.
- Hook: designing a fourth Gauntlet stage modelled on the Snail King's sweeps.

## Alfie (alfie)

- Age 52. Born in Kaldemor, son of a joiner (dead 20 years). Apprenticed at 13 to old Fennick (dead), head of the Carpenters' Guild; Fennick died 15 years ago and the carpenters chose Alfie to lead them.
- No spouse or children named.
- Keeps the great treehouse (including Lottie's schoolroom) and the clock tower stairs; checks the Gauntlet's hanging course each spring. Meets Ewan most evenings at the foot of the tower.
- Took Archie as apprentice 3 years ago.
- The carving: found early this spring (before the clock stopped) on the hidden face of an old treehouse beam where it lay against the living trunk; carved with a good blade; the trunk's bark had grown over its edges (very old). Archie had been sent for nails and did not see it. Alfie put the beam back cradled in new oak; the carving is hidden again. What it shows is deliberately not described.
- Does not yet know about the cracked ladder rung.

## Archie (archie)

- Age 17. Born in a village on the Royal Road toward Selsior; second of four children of a carter and his wife (alive, live there, play no part).
- Came to Kaldemor at 14 with his father's timber load; refused to leave; apprenticed to Alfie 3 years.
- Lodges in a room over a woodshed at the Park end of the Workshops lane, next door to Ewan's cottage at the foot of the clock tower.
- Has drawn his taller treehouse 11 times (seven storeys, rope lift, lookout, slide); once on the workshop wall.
- The rung: last spring, sent to replace a worn stair tread in the clock tower, he climbed the inside ladder to see the bell, came down hard and cracked a rung; mended it himself with glue, twine and an ash splint. Has not owned up.
- Thinks Alfie and Ewan's low talks are about him (they are about the carving).

## Bertie (bertie)

- Age 74. Born in Kaldemor. Learned shaping at 24 from old Bracken (shaper before him), who taught him 10 years and died in the oldest mushroom house in town; has worked alone since. Shaping 50 years.
- Wife Heather, a seamstress, died 6 years ago; he talks to her hat by the door.
- Their son (unnamed) and his wife, Daisy's parents, moved to Selsior when Daisy was 12 to keep a shop; alive, play no part, write at midwinter.
- This spring several mushroom houses nearest the woods grew crooked, all leaning away from the trees; he could not "hear" them.
- Has not forgotten Fen's window; waiting for the right house.

## Clem (clem)

- Age 25. Grew up in the orchard keeper's cottage at the end of the farm track; only child. Father died when he was too small to remember; mother kept the orchards and died 5 years ago (winter). Keeps them alone since. Not related to the orchard families near Kaldemor named in other towns' facts.
- Oldest trees nearest the farms were planted by Kaldemor's first settlers; newest rows (his mother's) run toward the woods.
- Apples go to Old Marta, Rosa (tarts) and the cider for the harbour carts. Pays Hattie in cider and windfalls for Tam.
- Courting Lia since last autumn's harvest dance (she asked him while Jem played).
- The roots: since this spring, something digs at night and strips roots deep underground on the trees nearest the woods; no silver trail; bark above ground untouched; two young trees dead. He told Hale, who nodded.
- Means to propose to Lia; practises on a pear tree.

## Daisy (daisy)

- Age 22. Born in Kaldemor; stayed with Bertie and Heather at 12 when her parents moved to Selsior; with Bertie 10 years.
- Paints the mushroom houses a week behind Bertie's spring rounds; also half the shutters, the Sleepy Snail's doors, the sign over Bram's stall, the harvest fair booths.
- Mixes her own paint; once made a brilliant blue from ground blue snail shell and has not matched it since. The tall yellow house by the Market steps is painted in a yellow she calls Tuesday.
- Hung Fen's unnameable-colour shutter on her own window.
- Once painted the Sleepy Snail's shutters the colour of Jem's fiddle ("brown left over"). Whether she knows about the song stays open.
- Paint on the houses nearest the woods cracks in the mornings.

## Dottie (dottie)

- Age 10. Lives with Elsie in the stone farmhouse (cheese room at the back).
- Parents: Elsie's only daughter Hyacinth and Hyacinth's husband Tom, both died of a winter fever 8 years ago when Dottie was 2. Has her mother's crook and her father's whistle.
- Was given the sheep at 7 (3 years ago) when Elsie's knees gave up the hills.
- Flock: 31 sheep and 9 lambs. Ewe Duchess (black ear), ram Mister Pike, lamb Button (speckled, bent ear).
- Button wandered toward the woods a week ago on a misty morning. She found a tuft of his wool on a bramble at the woods' edge, with large, round, damp tracks beside it (told no one). Searches every evening, turns back at the bramble. Counts 40 aloud for Elsie, 39 under her breath.

## Elsie (elsie)

- Age 72. Born on the dairy farm she keeps. Married Walt, a hedge-layer; kept the dairy together nearly 40 years; Walt died 15 years ago.
- Only child: Hyacinth (dead, see Dottie).
- 14 cows, including Bluebell, Hepzibah, Old Moll, Clover, Martha the Second. Her butter goes to Rosa.
- Rivalry with Marta: 20 years of cheese vs pickles at the harvest fair; each keeps a count of wins and they disagree; no exact score set. One winter when Elsie was ill, a jar of Marta's best pickles appeared on her doorstep with no note; neither has mentioned it.
- Has noticed Dottie's evening trips toward the woods; waiting for her to tell.
- Cows have refused the far pasture since this spring; she drove them twice, they came home alone. Told Hale (he nodded). Keeps Dottie's sheep away from it.

## Gareth (gareth)

- Age 48. From a mining village in the hills toward Selsior; father dug ore (parents both dead). No spouse or children named.
- Walked to Kaldemor at 16 to be striker for old Bryn, the Market smith (dead); a year of copper before iron ("Copper forgives. Iron remembers."). Bryn died 18 years ago and left him the forge.
- Sells iron tyres and axle-pins to Percy; nails and hinges to the carpenters.
- Mends Hilda's storage-door hinges each midwinter; she gives him tea.
- Secretly forging a small sword, a boy's arm long, for Tam; kept under the bench.

## Jem (jem)

- Age 24. Born in Kaldemor, in a narrow house behind the Market.
- Fiddle was his grandmother Nan Dilly's (dead 9 years); she played every Kaldemor harvest dance for 40 years. He has played them since he was 15.
- Parents moved to Selsior when he was 18 to keep a lodging house; alive, play no part.
- Lodges in the room over Molly's kitchen at the Sleepy Snail; she feeds him.
- Song for Daisy: 43 verses written, 41 crossed out. Wrote a song about Lia and Clem in an afternoon; Clem begged him not to play it.

## Lia (lia)

- Age 23. Grew up at her parents' small inn on the Royal Road, a day's walk toward Selsior; parents alive, write monthly, play no part.
- Came to Kaldemor at 16 on a harvest cart to sell her mother's preserves; Tobin hired her as guide on the spot. Guide 7 years.
- Heard the clock's silence from the square the morning it stopped and spread the news; later sat with Ewan and sent newcomers to keep him company.
- Keeps the darker road news (Snail King, missing cart, Pell's cab) for Tobin, not newcomers.
- Knows about Clem's gnawed roots; has told no one.

## Lottie (lottie)

- Age 34. Born in Kaldemor; parents a cart-painter and a beekeeper (both dead). No spouse or children.
- Was a pupil in the treehouse schoolroom under old Philippa (taught 40+ years; dead, died the winter after Lottie took over).
- Studied in Selsior's Scholars' Quarter from 18; came home at 25; teacher 9 years. Keeps Philippa's ruler on the windowsill.
- Schoolroom: round room at the very top of the great treehouse; sways gently in wind.
- Pupils include Nell, Fen, Tam. Told Fen the one-seed story. Tam asked her last week whether the seal would break.
- Green notebook of one-seed story versions (nearly 200 pages); has versions from Orin, Wren, Ned (Ned's short and grumpy).
- Hook: one version (from an old farm woman, dead, unnamed) says the traveller left a sign on the tree "so it would know her when she came back". Lottie has not found it. (Possible link to Alfie's carving; deliberately unconfirmed.)

## Meg (meg)

- Age 18. Born on a small turnip-and-geese holding at the far edge of the meadow, east of the town square (the game puts the meadow east of the square). Parents (unnamed, alive) gave up the holding 2 years ago after snails ate three plantings; moved east to relatives in the countryside past the end of the Royal Road; play no part. Meg (16) stayed.
- Lodges in the loft of the old seed shed by the windmills in the Park.
- Snail count she quotes: 1,412 (and rising by a few each telling).
- Ran the Gauntlet last summer; passed the hanging course first try. Same afternoon walked the Park fountain rim on her hands and fell in; Garrick (from his ledge) applauded, Digby saw. Nicknames: she calls Digby "the farmer", he calls her "the fish".
- This spring, before the Snail King was known, she went through the woods past the hills to a clearing beyond them and felt a long low shudder underfoot (not Hale's grinding sound); ran home, told no one.

## Mildred (mildred)

- Age 57. Born in Kaldemor, only child of a schoolmaster's assistant and a seamstress (both dead). Never married.
- Office: ground floor of the great treehouse. Keeps the family list in a green leather book.
- Came to the office at 24 as assistant to old Pomfrey (clerk under Mayor Hettie; dead); took the pen at 31 when his eyes failed; clerk 26 years (from Tobin's 4th year as mayor).
- Her exact count of Kaldemor: 294 at the last count (Tobin adds the snails; she crosses them out).
- Wrote Archie's father's name beside Archie's in the list. Files Ewan's four Garrick letters under "Clock tower, climbing of".
- Records Hale's self-paid bounties in a separate column, meaning to repay him.
- Writes Rufus's notices each evening; since this spring writes road warnings in shorter, plainer words.
- Hook: ledger shows far farms by the woods reporting more losses (geese, hens, pumpkins, young orchard trees) this year than in any five years before.

## Molly (molly)

- Age 52. Never married. Elder sister of Hilda (Hilda is the younger; exact gap unset).
- The Sleepy Snail: long low inn of honey-coloured timber, mossy roof, 9 rooms upstairs; sign of a green snail asleep in a nightcap, hung by Molly's grandfather (dead). Her parents (dead, unnamed) kept it after him; Molly and Hilda grew up there.
- Molly took the inn at 27; has run it 25 years.
- Has heard all 43 of Jem's verses through the kitchen ceiling; watches Jem and Daisy closely since the fiddle-brown shutters.
- Sends guests with heavy packs to Hilda: "She will keep it safer than you would."
- Fewer eastern traders this summer; travellers tell of carts lost in the dark, creatures at the wheels, lights in the trees.
- Keeps a folded list in her apron of travellers who promised to stop on the way back. Hook: a pedlar who stops every summer is 3 weeks late.

## Percy (percy)

- Age 56. Never married. Father (dead 22 years, unnamed) was wheelwright before him; Percy swept the yard from 8, made his first wheel (a wobbly barrow wheel, still hung over the door) at 15; alone in the yard since his father died.
- Yard near the Market end of the Workshops lane (open shed, tyre pit, iron truing ring).
- Built the town cab 18 years ago for Pell; Pell brings it in every spring.
- Last month: Pell brought the cab in late one evening, near rear wheel cracked through two spokes, splinter torn from the felloe; Percy mended it overnight, two new ash spokes, charged for one; kept the old felloe.
- Odd marks on Royal Road wheels this year: curved gouges as if something closed on the rim and pulled; one iron tyre bent inward. Showed some to Gareth. Percy and Gareth have a wager on what made them (guesses unrevealed).
- Percy built the harvest cart that went missing last autumn.

## Wilf (wilf)

- Age 46. Never married. Born in his squat stone cottage in the wettest hollow at the far end of the farms; father a spore gatherer, mother dried and sorted spores (both dead, unnamed). Grows brown, orange and an unnamed pale cap behind the cottage.
- Sister (unnamed, alive, 3 years older) married a farmer and lives in a village a long way south of the hills; plays no part; they write at midwinter.
- This spring she was ill; Wilf stayed with her a month (first time in 9 years away more than a night); came home at the start of summer.
- Has supplied Rosa and Bram for 20 years; Rosa pays on the nail, Bram argues about every bag. Carries news between them.
- On his first trip back to the woods past the hills, Shrooms followed him in threes and fours, did not scatter; one knocked his basket off his arm. He came back empty-handed.
- Hook: Shrooms in the deepest woods are carrying shiny things away toward one place.

## Winnie (winnie)

- Age 58. Workshop halfway down the Workshops lane (between the potter's and the joiners'); lives over it alone. Learned on her mother's loom; mother a weaver, father a shepherd on the hills (both dead, unnamed).
- Married young to Gilly, a carter (dead; fever, when Winnie was 30). No children.
- Keeps a finger-long scrap of every cloth she has made, pinned to sacking sheets in her back room. Scraps include Hob and Mabel's wedding blanket, Nell's cradle cloth, Tobin's cloak from his first harvest fair as mayor, and a grey blanket for Wren's mother (her first sale).
- Friends with Wren 30+ years (since Wren came to complain about a skein). Spins Wren's yarn first every autumn.
- A few days ago (after Button was lost) walked up to the hill pasture (buns as excuse), counted 8 lambs, not 9: Button missing (Dottie had promised Winnie Button's first clip). Told only Wren, and only that Dottie is keeping a worry. Has walked up twice more since.

## Bess (bess)

- Age 64 (13 years older than Bram). Never married. Half raised Bram.
- Grew up with Bram in a narrow house on the lane behind the Market; parents a Market porter and a washerwoman (both dead, unnamed).
- At 14 cut her hand on a sickle; old Mother Sedge (herb-woman, no daughter; dead) treated her and then taught her. Sedge died when Bess was 26; Bess has kept the green awning (stall in Kaldemor Market) 38 years.
- Keeps a count book of wounds; this year a separate page for bad bites from the far meadow and the woods past the hills that swell fast and some won't close even under comfrey or red potion. Showed Bram; he did not argue.
- Told Bram 9 years ago to apologise to Rosa, every spring since. Buys Rosa's bread twice a week; leaves a slice on Bram's counter one of those days.

## Garrick (garrick)

- Age 38. Origin deliberately vague: walked into Kaldemor 9 years ago over the hills (not by the road) with a rope and a pack; told Lia he came from "up". No known family.
- Ledge above the Park fountain, reached by a long rope ladder he pulls up when he wants no visitors; canvas-and-plank shelter, a stove, many ropes.
- Teaches Rope Up only to those who climb the long vine on the west side of Kaldemor Hills to the lookout (matches game quest "A Head for Heights").
- Waved to Ewan through the clock face once; Ewan dropped his oil can. Tobin read him Ewan's third letter aloud, laughing. Garrick never touches anything that swings.
- Saw Meg fall in the fountain and applauded. Wants to climb the great treehouse to its top if Alfie allows.
- This spring, from the hills' lookout, saw a line of pushed-apart and fallen treetops far past the hills, leading toward Mano's hollow from somewhere much farther off. Told only Hale (who nodded).

## Ned (ned)

- Age 79 (a year older than Orin). Never married. Born in Kaldemor; parents a drover and a dairymaid (both dead, unnamed).
- Apprenticed at 15 to old Joss (cobbler; dead), who made him stitch one seam daily for a year. Took the shop at 29 when Joss died; 50 years making Kaldemor's boots.
- Shop: dark little room near the Park end of the Workshops lane; wall of named wooden lasts, including dead customers'.
- Made Digby's boots himself (double-stitched, wider heel) when Wilbur bought them last year. Made Orin's woodcutting boots for 30 years.
- Ned says he won the race because he is older; Orin says that is why Ned was slower. They meet on Orin's Park bench every few days.
- Working by lamplight this spring for the first time in 20 years; soles come back torn by "things that are not stones".
- Hook: an unfinished pair of boots on his shelf, begun for someone (unnamed) 40 years ago.

## Pell (pell)

- Age 52. Born in Kaldemor; family ran the old posting stable behind the Market (parents dead, unnamed). No spouse or children.
- Drove a carrier's wagon for a Kaldemor carter from 18; has driven the cab 18 years (Percy built it).
- Cab: tall green two-wheeled cab, folding hood, squeaky step, seats two (three if friends). Horse: Turnip, a brown mare with a white nose.
- Rules (match the game): towns with a taxi stand only, never into the wilds, fixed fare, only to towns the fare has walked to once ("a person ought to know the road they are paying to skip"). Lia sends him most fares.
- Last month, coming home toward Kaldemor at dusk with an empty cab, something ran at it from the trees by the Royal Road; he galloped home (Turnip's first gallop in 10 years); two spokes cracked, felloe splintered. Has told no one what it was. Now carries a lantern even by day.

## Rufus (rufus)

- Age 44. Born in Kaldemor, son of a carpenter and a market-woman (both dead, unnamed); grew up in the lanes behind the Market. Never married.
- Rings his handbell three times at 8 each morning on the step of the signpost in the town square.
- Was runner as a boy for old Ludo (crier 30 years; dead the winter after retiring). Ludo gave Rufus the bell 15 years ago when his voice went ("Louder"). Crier 15 years.
- While the clock was stopped, rang at eight by the sun for nine mornings and announced (unauthorised) that Ewan sent his apologies.
- Once added a poem about a lost goat to Mildred's notices; she now leaves more room at the bottom of the page.
- Lost cat Mistress Whiskers: missing 3 weeks, last seen heading toward the woods.
