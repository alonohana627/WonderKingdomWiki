# Established facts: goa-harbour

Written by NPC lore writers so later writers stay consistent. Not shown on the wiki.

## The town's look (2026-10-15, Lith Harbor reshape)

- Goa Harbour is a busy timber port, not a town in the trees. The Inlet Quay is the harbour front: wooden quays and piers on piles, timber stacks, warehouses on the water, ships at anchor in the inlet. The Wardens' House stands at the quay's head, at the foot of the cliffs. The Lantern Market (with the Gap Inn) is the market street behind the quay, colourful painted timber houses. The Fishers' Coves (boats on shingle, net lofts, drying racks, smokehouses) lie under the cliffs at the seaward end; the Forest Shore (raft landing, timber crews) is round the point at the other end. Wooded hills rise behind the town.
- The Cliff Stairs (the Long Stair, the rope lift, the rope bridges) are the way up the cliff above the quay to the clifftop quarter (a row of timber houses along the top) and Gull Heath. Houses stand on rock: on the Long Stair's landings, on the lower cliff above the quay ("cliff houses") and in the clifftop quarter. The rope bridges carry the cliff paths across clefts in the cliff face; they do not join houses.
- Changed: Kestra's bridges and Gorm's description of them no longer "join the cliff houses"; Fionn runs from the quay to "the highest house in the clifftop quarter" (was "the highest cliff house").

## Arlo (arlo_child)

- Age 9. Only child of Iska and Dunmor. Born in the family's cliff house above the Inlet Quay.
- Has shadowed Varro on the quay at dawn for some time; Varro first spoke to him with "stand where you can see the eddy". Varro gives no praise beyond "aye".
- Iska and Dunmor both know about the dawn visits and say nothing.
- Best friend Eilidh; they read the tide together from the cove rocks in the evenings. Tolerates Ronan tagging along.
- Sits at Ysmay's window above the Cliff Stairs and writes down her complaints about pilots.
- Wants to take a ship through the gap before 12 (no one in living memory has); wants to crew the Gap Runner.
- Overheard Nessa at the Wardens' table about too few young pilots.
- Has noticed disturbed water on the lowest quay steps at dawn; has told no one (he does not know of Dougal's tracks).

## Sorcha (sorcha_fisher)

- Age 38. Daughter of Agna. Father Donal, a cove fisher, died of a winter fever 15 years ago (invented, dead).
- Fishes from Donal's old boat, the Sea Thrift, much patched.
- Married Calder 13 years ago. Children: Eilidh 10, Ronan 7.
- Puts aside a little of each good catch toward Calder's bigger boat.
- Has stopped taking the children past the point; has not explained why to Eilidh.
- Brodie's favour: two winters ago his smokehouse roof fell in and Sorcha gave him a season's catch on credit; he has not repaid it.

## Torva (torva_hunter)

- Age 36. Grew up on the Forest Shore. Mother Una, a timber cutter, killed by a falling tree when Torva was 11 (25 years ago; invented, dead). Father not mentioned.
- Has led the hunters for 9 years; never formally chosen.
- Reads Bethoc's sighting board at the Gap Inn every morning.
- Long scar along her jaw from a creature that climbed the cliffs three winters ago; Elspeth stitched it and most of her wounds.
- Now goes out with Caddoc to watch his sprung traps.
- Lets Fionn follow as far as "the last stump" at the forest edge, always with a hunter between him and the trees.
- Has tracked that monsters are coming from deeper in the forest. Does not know why.

## Varro (warden_varro)

- Age 76. Born to a cove family. Sea warden since 20 (56 years). Has never lost a ship in the gap.
- Wife Maili, a cove fisher, died of a fever 14 years ago (invented, dead). No children. Keeps her oilskin by his door; walks the quay again at dusk on her death's anniversary.
- Began teaching Iska when she was 7 (34 years ago). Also taught Nessa. Spoke for Iska as First Warden.
- Served beside Ysmay for 40 years. Ailsa is the other colleague left from his early years.
- Stopped piloting ships through the gap 5 years ago (knees).
- Has sensed the tides shifting (eddies out of place) but has not asked Ailsa and does not know of her Tide Book worries.

## Calder (calder_fisher)

- Age 40. Parents both died in the same fever winter when he was 5 (35 years ago; invented, dead). Raised by his grandmother Morven, a singer of the camp songs, who died when he was 20 (invented, dead). Lived in a stone cottage above the far end of the coves.
- Knows a few of Morven's camp-song verses no one else in town seems to know; Cormac has tried to coax them out of him at the Gap Inn.
- Friends with Tamsin since boyhood on the Inlet Quay (he held planks for her).
- Married Sorcha 13 years ago.
- Has seen Ronan's hidden stone once and promised not to tell; the stone is always faintly warm.
- Has drawn his deep-water boat many times; Tamsin promised to help build it once the Gap Runner beats Bain Harbour. Knows Sorcha saves toward it; hasn't told her.

## Eilidh (eilidh_child)

- Age 10. Elder child of Sorcha and Calder. Agna taught her knots.
- Sorcha stopped taking the children past the point "a few years ago".
- Keeps a tide slate hidden in a crack in the cove rocks; writes each evening's tide on it.
- Knows Arlo gets dawn lessons from Varro; jealous, never says so.
- Agna cut a notch on the cove rocks as a girl to mark the highest spring tide. This spring the water has gone over it twice. Eilidh has told no one yet.
- Suspects Ronan has a secret in his pocket; has not found out what.

## Ronan (ronan_child)

- Age 7. Younger child of Sorcha and Calder (3 years younger than Eilidh). Keeps treasures in a driftwood box under his bed. Agna calls him "the magpie".
- The stone: found last autumn at the bottom of the deepest tide pool beyond the far cove. Smooth, egg-shaped, dark grey with a paler band, fits a closed hand, always faintly warm; Ronan thinks it glows faintly on dark nights near water. Its nature is unknown (keep open).
- Has shown the stone only to Calder. Not to Eilidh.
- Once carried Lachlan's rope down to the quay. Lachlan tells him "when you are bigger".
- Has heard of Gwenna's list of those lost in the sea caves.

## Tamsin (tamsin_boatwright)

- Age 40. Grew up on the Inlet Quay; father a rope-maker who died when she was small (invented, dead). Childhood friend of Calder.
- Apprenticed at 12 to old Fergus, boatwright of the quay yard (invented, dead). Fergus built the previous race boat, the Grey Heron.
- The Grey Heron carried Iska to all three of Goa's wins as skipper; in the last (6 summers ago) she finished with her keel sprung and never sailed again. Fergus died that winter and left the yard and the next boat to Tamsin.
- Tamsin built the Gap Runner that winter. It has raced 5 summers, lost all 5 to Bain; 2 of those losses by a single length (consistent with 4 of Bain's 9 wins by a length).
- Blames the timber; wants deep-forest timber. Running argument with Haldor.
- Jory works in her yard when not hauling cargo. Still holds a grudge over one of Iska's tacks two summers ago.

## Bethoc (bethoc_innkeeper)

- Age 45. Born at the Gap Inn. Father Malcolm kept the inn 35 years (since before Bethoc was born) and died 11 years ago (invented, dead); mother died when Bethoc was a baby (invented, dead). The inn has been hers for 11 years; old regulars call it "Malcolm's".
- The Gap Inn: long, low timber house, stone hearth at one end, view of the inlet at the other.
- Started the sighting board (a plank by the door, chalked) 5 years ago. Wipes it monthly; keeps a yearly tally in a book under the bar. The tally grows each year.
- Cormac has a fixed seat by the fire. Mhairi plays most nights and at the race send-off.
- Sweethearts with Brodie for 3 years; not engaged. Brodie comes every evening after market.

## Dunmor (dunmor_lamplighter)

- Age 44. Father Tormod was lamplighter before him and died 22 years ago (invented, dead); mother died when Dunmor was a boy (invented, dead). Lamplighter for 22 years, never formally appointed.
- Lights the quay lanterns at dusk and puts them out at first light. Brings failing lanterns to Gunna; does not know how they are made and has never asked.
- Met Iska holding a lantern on the bottom quay step when she came in late from a night pilotage; did so most nights for a year. Married 12 years ago (Iska 29).
- Saw Arlo on the quay the first morning; says nothing; puts out the lowest-step lantern last.
- Ysmay thinks him too soft, and the best thing that happened to Iska (never in Iska's hearing).
- This spring the lowest-landing quay lanterns have guttered together on windless nights; he has told no one.

## Elspeth (elspeth_healer)

- Age 71. Healer for 45 years, in a low stone house on the Forest Shore. Learned from old Fenella, the healer who kept the same house before her (invented, dead).
- Husband Aonghas, a woodsman, died 30 years ago of a chill caught clearing a windfall in the rain (invented, dead). Osric is their son.
- Osric has never told her what he saw; she has stopped asking and leaves a lamp in her window every night.
- Thinks Brigid will be a better healer than she is (hasn't said so). Has noticed Brigid quieter and her baskets lighter, but does not know about the withering beds.
- Hunters bring wounded several times a week. Stitched Torva's jaw scar.

## Esk (esk_adventurer)

- About 34. Arrived 4 years ago on a trading ship working round the coast, with one sea-chest, a short sword and a long knife. Origin unknown (keep it so).
- Old burn scar on the back of her left hand; says an unknown name in her sleep. Rents a loft above a net store on the Inlet Quay from an unnamed woman.
- Met Corrie the night she landed (Corrie, then an errand girl on the quay, found her the loft). Friends since.
- Sails as a guard with fishing boats that go far beyond the gap. Has seen: a long shadow pacing a boat half a morning, a patch of sea gone flat and dark in a fresh wind, lights below the surface on a moonless night. Tells only Corrie; believed Corrie's sighting last month and told her it was not the first.
- Buys rope only from Gwenna; asked never to be on Gwenna's list.
- Has not yet answered Teague about guarding the hill road.

## Gorm (gorm_stairs)

- Age 46. Born in a cliff house halfway up the Long Stair (the oldest stairway, from the Inlet Quay to the clifftop).
- Father, a quarryman who cut stone for the quay walls, died of a fall when Gorm was 13 (33 years ago; invented, dead). No other family.
- Ulric took him on at 13 (put a chisel in his hand on the steps). Master of the Stairs 11 years; Ulric stepped aside to carve only the hardest steps.
- Has never read Ulric's carvings in order (does not know they tell the first camp's story).
- Shouts at Kestra for refusing a safety line. Fond of Fionn.
- Promised Iska last autumn a new stair down to the far cove before the race, so the town can watch the boats go out from the rocks. Behind: hard broken rock, a flight cracked in the spring frost, crews refuse the lowest landings after dark.
- Has found deep scratches in sets of four on the high landings; told only Ruan. Cuts new steps sharper on the seaward side.

## Ysmay (grizel_elder)

- Age 70. Grew up on the Inlet Quay. Sea warden from 20 to 60 (retired 10 years ago: eyes and back). Overlapped Varro all 40 years.
- Lives in a narrow stone house near the top of the Cliff Stairs; its window sees the whole inlet to the gap.
- Was asked to stand for First Warden more than once and refused (says she wanted the water, not a table; the rest of the reason is unknown).
- Husband Lorn, a deckhand on coastal trading ships, lost overboard in a winter gale when Iska was 5 (36 years ago; invented, dead). Iska is her only child.
- After Lorn's death took Iska to the quay daily; let Varro teach her from age 7.
- Was at her window, not in the room, when Iska was chosen 7 years ago (retired wardens don't attend); saw Varro look up at the cliff.
- Has noticed pilots swinging wide where the old eddy was; blames the pilots aloud, fears the water has moved. Varro has not argued.

## Haldor (haldor_timber)

- Age 48. Born on the Forest Shore; joined the crews at 15.
- At 23 (25 years ago) was cutting on Una's crew and saw her killed by the falling tree. Has never spoken of it with Torva.
- Head of the timber crews 12 years; has never lost a cutter in that time. No family mentioned.
- Logs are lashed into rafts and towed round the point to the Inlet Quay at slack tide.
- Pulled crews back twice this year: end of winter (camp torn apart, every axe-handle bitten through) and this spring (something large followed a crew all afternoon unseen). Now cuts only at the forest edge within shouting distance of the shore, with Torva's hunters near.
- Never pushed Osric to say what he saw. Has told Merrin she will get a crew but is not ready yet.

## Ruan (ruan_cliffwatch)

- Age 43. Grew up above the Long Stair. Joined the watch at 18; captain 10 years. No family mentioned.
- The watch has grown to about a dozen guards. Alarm: an iron bar hung at the head of the Long Stair.
- Tests every rope on the cliffs monthly by hanging his weight off it (Kestra's twice).
- Dougal kept him awake with jokes when he was a recruit; they share tea at the bottom of the stairs at the night-watch change.
- Reports to Iska weekly at the Wardens' House.
- Three winters ago his watch saw the first creature climb the cliff face itself; Torva and two hunters drove it off the high landing (Torva's jaw scar). Ruan held the lantern while Elspeth stitched her.
- Has asked Gunna for lanterns on every high landing ("a good lantern cannot be hurried"); Tarran has promised something brighter, sooner.
- Holds Dougal's tracks and Gorm's scratches; has not yet put them together before Iska.

## Ailsa (ailsa_tidebook)

- Age 73. Came to the Wardens' House at 17 as copyist to old Keeper Innes (invented, dead), the same spring Varro became a warden (56 years ago). Keeper since 27 (46 years). No family mentioned.
- The Tide Book: oldest first-camp pages in Savage tally marks and a Magician hand, rebound many times; later volumes on a long shelf in a dry back room of the Wardens' House; current volume on a slanted desk under the window. Ailsa can only half read the oldest hand.
- Eats midday bread with Halvard at her desk most days; he straightens her crooked stack of notes, she re-crooks it.
- For the past few years: spring highs a hand's breadth higher, slack water coming later by minutes, strongest run drifting toward the forest-side cliff of the gap. Cause unknown (keep open).
- Iska saw the pages and asked once; Ailsa said "Not yet." Ailsa suspects Varro has felt it too because he has not asked.
- Does not know about Agna's notch (Eilidh's secret).

## Halvard (halvard_clerk)

- Age 46. From Linmoor, a farming valley far inland (invented place). Parents long dead (invented). Older sister (unnamed) works the family farm there, far away, plays no part; writes once a year.
- Came to Goa at 24 (22 years ago) to see the sea; sailed out on a timber ship, seasick 3 days, has never sailed since.
- Copied cargo lists for a Lantern Market trader, then clerk of the Wardens' House for 15 years.
- Counted Varro's ships in the ledgers; has not told him the number.
- Keeps a new ledger column of strange things in the gap: captains reporting hulls scraped by "something soft, and then something hard", and Corrie's sighting. Nine entries; shown to no one.

## Caddoc (caddoc_trapper)

- Age 42. Born to a highland trapping family in the hills above the hill road. Mother died when he was small; father lost checking a line in a snowstorm when Caddoc was 16 (26 years ago; both invented, dead). Walked down with a drover's flock and never went back.
- Trapper on the Forest Shore 26 years; lives in a cluttered hut there; builds all his own traps.
- Torva's hunting partner 7 years (she hunts by day, he traps by night).
- Greer pays him more as highland trappers dwindle; he grumbles anyway.
- For most of a season his heavy cage traps have been sprung: bait gone, doors dropped, no hair or blood, ground swept smooth. Rebuilt the triggers 3 times; each sprung within a week. Cause unknown (keep open).

## Cormac (cormac_singer)

- Age 79. Grew up on the Lantern Market, son of a sailmaker who sang at his work (invented, dead). Failed as sailmaker and as ship's crew; a singer since about 20. Never married (no family mentioned).
- Has had his seat by the fire at the Gap Inn since Malcolm kept it.
- Sang with Calder's grandmother Morven for about 40 years; she died 20 years ago and he could not finish his song for her at the shore. Believes she had another version of the gap verse and Calder may have heard it.
- Taught Mhairi the old tunes when she was a girl. Hurt she has not asked him for a verse of her race song.
- The gap verse: learned as a boy from an unnamed old pilot (dead) who was frightened and told him to keep it. Cormac has sung only its first line in public ("the hour the water forgets"). Unsure of its ending. Meaning unknown (keep open).

## Corrie (corrie_pilot)

- Age 19. Grew up on the Inlet Quay. Mother Rhona, a fish-gutter for the smokehouses, died of a fever when Corrie was 12 (7 years ago; invented, dead). Father a deckhand who sailed before she was born and never returned (far away, plays no part).
- Errand girl on the quay after her mother's death; found Esk the loft 4 years ago (aged 15).
- Rowed the wardens' pilot boats from 17; Kor's apprentice since last spring; first given a tiller after about 2 years at the oars.
- Copies her hand-notes nightly into oilcloth-covered books; has filled 4. Keeps Esk's sightings on numbered, dated pages at the back.
- The sighting (last month): from the pilot boat ahead of a timber ship at slack water; dark shape longer than the hull, moving against the tide. Kor was looking away and called it a cloud shadow; the wardens were kind about it. Halvard wrote it down; Corrie doesn't know why.

## Hamish (hamish_lift)

- Age 39. Unmarried. Keeper of the rope lift 15 years; took it over from old Murdo (invented, dead or retired; worked it 30 years) when Murdo's arms gave out. Has never dropped a crate.
- The lift: timber frame on the clifftop, windlass, counterweight of stone-filled baskets in its own channel, one heavy rope oiled weekly. Hamish and Fionn live in the little house beside the windlass.
- Younger sister Ishbel (Fionn's mother) died of a wasting cough 7 years ago, when Fionn was 3 (invented, dead). Her husband Duncan, a cove fisher, drowned in a storm 9 years ago, when Fionn was 1 (invented, dead). Hamish has raised Fionn since he was 3.
- Does not know Fionn follows the hunters; Torva and half the hunters know and have not told him.
- Wants a second lift down to the far cove; taunts Gorm about the late far-cove stair.
- Has felt the lift rope shudder on still nights, as if brushed on the cliff face.

## Fionn (fionn_child)

- Age 10. Son of Duncan and Ishbel (see Hamish); remembers only a blue shawl and a song about a gull. Lives with Hamish beside the lift windlass since age 3.
- Runs messages from the Inlet Quay to the highest house in the clifftop quarter and back.
- Shortcuts: the gap behind the smokehouse (drops two landings), the ledge under the third rope bridge, a goat path that halves the Long Stair.
- Saving coppers in a tin under the floorboards for a bow, hoping Torva will then take him into the trees.
- The hunters privately think him a very good tracker.
- One evening this spring, crossing the highest rope bridge after sunset, saw something pale, low and flat moving fast on the cliff face below it; days later Kestra found the ropes chewed. Has told no one (would reveal he was on the bridge after dark). What it was: unknown (keep open).

## Gunna (gunna_lanterns)

- Age 77. Workshop at the back of the Lantern Market (beeswax, green wood, an unnamed sharp smell).
- The craft passes to one pupil at a time and is never written down. Learned from her great-aunt Seona (invented, dead), who took her in at 10. Seona died 44 years ago; Gunna master since 33. No other family mentioned.
- Lanterns burn steady in any wind and need little oil. Hand lantern: a few days. Quay lantern: most of a month. How they work: unknown (keep open).
- Ruan asks her weekly for high-landing lanterns; she asks how many nights a hurried lantern would last.
- Has not decided if Tarran is worthy; wants to know if he understands why the first camp's Magicians never made a lantern to drive off monsters (the reason is not stated; keep open).
- Needs rare forest materials (unspecified) for her lanterns.

## Jory (jory_crew)

- Age 20. Born on the Inlet Quay. Mother (unnamed) was a quay porter for 30 years until her back gave out; now lives with her sister on a farm far inland (far away, plays no part); writes to him. Father not mentioned.
- Took over his mother's portering at 14. Turned up at Tamsin's yard every evening for about two winters before last summer's race; Iska picked him partly for that.
- Last summer's race (his first) was a loss to Bain; margin not stated. Was not in the crew two summers ago (Tamsin's tack grudge predates him).
- Boyhood friend of Kor on the quay. Knows Kor wants a race seat; privately resolved to give up his own seat if Iska must choose between them (told no one).
- Mhairi's race song has a rowdy verse for Jory.

## Kenna (kenna_hunter)

- Age 19. Grew up on the Forest Shore. Parents (unnamed) towed log rafts; moved along the coast to a distant timber camp when she was 16 (far away, play no part). She stayed, lodged with a crew family, mended nets.
- Torva took her on last autumn (first test: three days watching the forest edge).
- The race (shared with Merrin): 10 years ago, both aged 9, at low tide on the Forest Shore, from the raft landing to the end of the point and back. Kenna ahead at the turn; Merrin came back over the weed-covered rocks instead of the sand and won by a stride; Kenna slipped following her and skinned both knees. No rules had been agreed. Merrin counts it the first line of her tally.
- Secretly fond of Fionn; presses clear prints into the mud at the last stump and names them aloud when he is listening. Fionn still thinks no one has noticed him.

## Kestra (kestra_bridges)

- Age 23. Grew up in a cliff house above the Inlet Quay. Mother Shona, a cove net-maker, taught her knots; died of a fever when Kestra was 15 (8 years ago; invented, dead). Father not mentioned.
- Joined Gorm's crew at 14 as a rope-carrier; rigging her own bridges by 16; best rigger on the crew by about 19.
- Her bridges carry the cliff paths across the clefts above the harbour, from low spans over the quay to the highest bridge just below the clifftop quarter (they do not join houses).
- Walks every bridge each morning; splices rope at the top of the Cliff Stairs in the afternoons.
- The chewing: highest bridge only, from below, seaward side, strands bitten through one by one. First found this spring (a few days after Fionn's sighting); has recurred repeatedly since; she has never seen the culprit.
- Has sat out on the highest bridge after dark (Gorm forbids it; Ruan doesn't know). Tarran has promised her a lantern to light the cliff face beneath the bridge.
- Races Moira up the stairs some evenings; usually wins.

## Lachlan (lachlan_adventurer)

- Age 22. Grew up in the Fishers' Coves. Parents (unnamed) crew trading ships round the far side of the island; rarely return (far away, play no part). Raised by grandfather Alasdair, a cove fisher (invented), who once went a little way into the sea caves as a young man, came out fast and never said why; died 3 winters ago. Lachlan uses his oilskin and tide-reckoner.
- Keeps an oilcloth map of the cave mouths between the Inlet Quay and the far cove, with entry tides and depth reached (a few dozen yards in most; a little further in two).
- In the deepest cave he has reached he heard water moving where the tide should have been still and turned back (first time he ever turned back). Told no one. Cause unknown (keep open).
- Has never asked to see Gwenna's list. Has begun to worry Ronan will not wait.

## Merrin (merrin_timber)

- Age 19. Born on the Forest Shore. Mother Catriona steered the log rafts round the point (invented); died of a winter chill when Merrin was 12 (7 years ago). Father not mentioned. Lives alone in a small timber house among the crew houses.
- Joined the crews at 13 carrying water and sharpening axes (as Haldor once did, though he started at 15); first axe at 15.
- Tally notched on her axe handle and copied onto the inside of her door; not yet top of it. First line: the race with Kenna (see Kenna).
- Has begun quietly thanking trees like Haldor. Thinks bringing a deep-forest tree out for Tamsin would win her a crew.

## Mhairi (mhairi_fiddler)

- Age 21. Grew up on the Lantern Market above a ship's chandler's. Father (unnamed), a ship's carpenter, made her first squeaking fiddle from a mast-timber offcut when she was 7; lost with his coastal trader in a winter storm when she was 11 (10 years ago; invented, dead). Mother not mentioned. Keeps the old fiddle on her wall.
- Cormac began teaching her at 8. Bethoc took her on at 15 (6 years ago).
- Race song verses so far: the gap at dawn, Iska at the tiller, Tamsin on the slip, Jory. No last verse until Goa wins.
- Hasn't asked Cormac for a verse because she wants to give him the finished song as a gift; doesn't know he is hurt.

## Moira (moira_watch)

- Age 21. Grew up in a cliff house above the Inlet Quay (family not mentioned). Joined the Cliff Watch at 17 (4 years ago).
- Was on watch three winters ago when the first creature climbed the cliff face; she rang the iron bar and saw Torva and the hunters drive it off.
- Has asked Torva more than once to take her hunting. Has noticed climbers seek the stretches lanterns don't reach; told Ruan.
- Friendly with Tarran (he promised her lanterns sooner) and Kestra (they race up the stairs).

## Osric (osric_woodsman)

- Age 44. Was 14 when his father Aonghas died (30 years ago). Took up his father's work and axe; Walked the deep forest ahead of the crews marking trees for about 30 years; Haldor's lead woodsman since Haldor became head of the crews 12 years ago.
- Has always kept the old woodsmen's sayings (rowan over his door, knocks twice on stumps, out before sunset).
- Last autumn came out without his father's axe; it is still somewhere in the deep forest. What he saw: unknown (keep open).
- Lives in a small hut a short walk from Elspeth's house; on bad nights sits silent by her fire.
- Now marks trees and watches the deep woods along the forest edge. Worries about Merrin's eye on the deep-forest trees.

## Tarran (tarran_lanterns)

- Age 20. Grew up on the Lantern Market (family not mentioned). Apprenticed to Gunna at 14 (6 years ago); she handed him a broom after he stood at her door a week.
- Gunna still does some steps alone with the door shut; he cannot place the sharp smell.
- Once asked Gunna why no one made a monster-driving lantern; she asked why he thought; he said "nobody tried hard enough". She said nothing more. (Real reason still open.)
- Experiments: one lit the whole Lantern Market half a night then cracked its glass; one burned blue, never repeated.
- Has promised Ruan a brighter lantern sooner (Gunna knows, says nothing) and Kestra a lantern to light the cliff beneath the highest bridge.

## Teague (teague_drover)

- Age 50. Born in a herding hamlet high in the hills. Learned the hill road driving sheep behind his uncle (unnamed, invented, dead). Own flocks on the road 30 years; drover for Morag 22 years.
- The Saddle: a narrow stretch near the top of the hill road between two crags where flocks go single file (named place).
- Last spring at dusk something came out of the rocks at the Saddle into the flock; half lost. He had two dogs and a hired boy; no one killed; one dog not the same since; the boy went back up into the hills and never came down again. Teague hid behind a rock till dawn and never really saw it.
- Next trip due after the summer shearing. Has asked Esk (no answer yet) and many adventurers at the Gap Inn.

## Kor (warden_kor)

- Age 24. Grew up on the Inlet Quay (family not mentioned); boyhood friend of Jory.
- Began rowing the pilot boats at 15 and was Nessa's apprentice for 8 years; full warden last spring (age 23).
- Still believes Corrie's sighting was a cloud shadow, but lingers looking at the water on that stretch.
- Asks Jory about every stroke of last year's race; has not asked Iska outright for a seat.

## Nessa (warden_nessa)

- Age 42. Grew up in the Fishers' Coves (family not mentioned). Came to the Inlet Quay at 10 (32 years ago) and Varro took her on; Iska, a year younger, had then been his pupil 2 years. Full warden at 22 (20 years ago).
- At the choosing 7 years ago, she stood and spoke for Iska before any count; never explained why (jokes about it).
- Trained Kor. Has begun taking quay children out in the pilot boats at slack water, one at a time, without asking the Wardens' table.

## Agna (agna_netmender)

- Age 72. Born in the coves. Father Niall, a cove fisher; mother Kirsty, a net-mender (both invented, dead). Took over the coves' nets at 22 (50 years ago) when Kirsty's eyes failed.
- Cut the high-tide notch at age 10 (62 years ago) with her father's gutting knife, during the highest spring tide anyone then remembered. Has not walked out to it in years (knees); does not know the water has gone over it.
- Fished at night with lanterns in her youth, sometimes with Maili (Varro's late wife). Night fishing thinned while Sorcha grew up; no one in the coves has fished at night for more than 20 years.
- Husband Donal (see Sorcha). Approves of Calder. Calls Ronan "the magpie" and saves him bright things from the nets (sea-glass, a brass button, a little bell). Tells Eilidh no better than "not bad".
- Calls Kestra's bridge knots "Shona's knots, near enough".
- Torn nets: long bites through many meshes at once, always on boats that went past the point, over the last few years.
- Last month found a broken, curved, pale, hard fragment about a finger long caught in a torn net; not bone or shell she knows. Keeps it wrapped in a rag in her mending basket; has shown no one (not even Calder). What it is: unknown (keep open).

## Brigid (brigid_herbs)

- Age 17. Parents (unnamed, invented) drowned when a log raft they were working broke up rounding the point, when she was 2 (15 years ago).
- Raised by grandmother Oonagh (invented, dead), who gathered dulse and sea-herbs on the Forest Shore rocks and sold them to Elspeth for about 30 years and to the smokehouses. Oonagh died when Brigid was 11 (6 years ago).
- Elspeth took her in at 11; apprentice 6 years; sleeps in the loft of Elspeth's house.
- Withering: began last autumn in the oldest beds on the low rocks at the forest end of the Forest Shore. By this spring 5 of the 11 beds she knows there are withering, 2 nearly gone (leaves pale, then soft and dark at the root, shrinking from the bottom edge up). Cause unknown (keep open). She has noticed the wrack line on those rocks sits a little higher.
- Has not told Elspeth; walks further along the wild coast alone at low tide to make up the baskets.
- Holds bowls and lamps when the hunters' wounded come in; has seen Osric's bad nights and never asks him anything.

## Dougal (dougal_quaywatch)

- Age 66. Grew up on the Inlet Quay. Deckhand on timber ships for about 20 years (always seasick); quay porter in his early thirties; night watchman from age 36 (30 years).
- Predecessor: old Ewart (invented, dead or retired; legs gave out). His advice: count everything, every night, because one night the count will be wrong.
- Never married, no family. Lives in a stone watch-hut at the end of the quay near the foot of the Long Stair; sleeps by day.
- Shift: from Dunmor's lighting of the lanterns at dusk to his putting them out at first light. Has seen Arlo coming to the quay at dawn; says nothing.
- Tea with Ruan at the bottom of the stairs at the change of the night watch. Favourite joke: the gull on the lantern ("a light-house bird").
- Tracks: first found early this spring on the lowest quay steps; about a dozen mornings since. Wide, splayed, long toes with claw marks, wider than his boot; come up out of the water and go back down, never much above the bottom few steps; dry fast. He sluices them away with seawater before the quay wakes. Told only Ruan. (Number of toes not stated; keep open.)
- Has seen the lowest-landing lanterns dip together once or twice on still nights; assumes Dunmor knows; neither has spoken of it.

## Ulric (ulric_stonecarver)

- Age 79. Born on the clifftop, son of a goatherd (unnamed, invented, dead). Never married, no family. Lives alone in a narrow cliff house on an upper landing of the Long Stair.
- Taken on at 14 (65 years ago) by old Struan, then Master of the Stairs (invented, dead), who also carved small things (a shell, a knot) into some steps.
- Master of the Stairs from 41 to 68 (27 years; 38 to 11 years ago). Took Gorm on at 46 (33 years ago). Handed the post to Gorm 11 years ago.
- The story: on the Long Stair, recut step by step over about 50 years; read top to bottom: wild goat (Savage hunters from the hills), owl (Magicians from the forest), goat and owl together, cormorant diving (first fishing), crab among weed (sea-herbs), otter on its back watching the water (first wardens), moth at a lantern, gull over two cliffs (the gap). The bottom step, where the stair meets the quay, is left blank: the songs end differently and he does not know which ending is true (keep open). Has told no one what the carvings say.
- Learned the story from camp songs, Struan's tales, and Cormac and Morven at the Gap Inn.
- Teases Fionn that his shortcuts read the story "with the pages torn out".
- Now cutting the flight of the far cove stair that cracked in the spring frost; crews call that rock "the Anvil" (named place). Works by daylight only. Has noticed Gorm's sharper seaward edges; has not asked why (does not know of the scratches).
