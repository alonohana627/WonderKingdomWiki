# Established facts: kingdom

Written by NPC lore writers so later writers stay consistent. Not shown on the wiki.

All counts are "years before the present day". The present is 50 years after the Great World Tree Expedition.

## The kingdom and the crown

- King Bertram, "the old king": crowned 58 years ago, reigned 42 years, died 16 years ago. He sent the Great World Tree Expedition 50 years ago. Received Cavelor's warnings kindly but filed more than he answered.
- Bertram's kinship to Aveline, Aveline's parents, and how she came to the throne are deliberately unstated (how monarchs are chosen is an open question).
- The Great Bell last rang at Aveline's coronation, 16 years ago.

## Queen Aveline (queen_aveline)

- Age 44. Crowned 16 years ago, aged 28.
- Met Leofric over a disputed map in the Scholars' Quarter (he proved his road was right); married 20 years ago, 4 years before her coronation.
- Elinor is "nearly grown" (17 in facts/selsior.md); Alaric is a child (9 in facts/selsior.md).
- Has noticed Elinor's blistered hands and suspects something; has said nothing yet. Does not know who teaches her.
- Made Rhys Captain of the Royal Guard 8 years ago.
- Feels the crown owes Cavelor a debt because Bertram sent the expedition.
- Cavelor alliance: see Marrun below. Rook letters: see Rook below.

## Rhys (rhys)

- Age 40. Joined the Guard as a recruit at 18 (22 years ago); taught by Aubrey. Grand Swordmaster for 10 years; Captain 8 years.
- Has never met a Sword Saint; sets that test as "the old test".
- No family named.

## Rhys and Tavi (old training partners)

- 21 years ago (Rhys 19 and a recruit, Tavi 18 and adventuring), Tavi trained at the Knights' Court for one season, spring to the first autumn frosts. Aubrey paired them because both were too fierce.
- 80 evening bouts after drill, tally scratched on a post in the practice yard: Tavi 41, Rhys 39.
- Tavi left Selsior before the Guard's trials and took no rank.
- Met only a handful of times since. Last: 3 years ago, Tavi came to Selsior with a party from Midbarium; one bout in the old yard, stopped by the recruits' drill bell. Both agree it is unfinished; each privately thinks they would have won.
- Write once a year around midwinter; letters end with the tally.
- Rhys: no one ever pushed him harder. Tavi: no one made Tavi work as hard as Rhys did.

## Tobin (tobin)

- Age 71. Mayor 30 years (since age 41). Before that a cooper (barrel maker) with a workshop behind the market.
- Predecessor: Mayor Hettie (dead).
- Wife Primrose, a potter, died 12 years ago. No children.
- Ewan has been his friend since boyhood; Tobin sat up with Ewan every night while the great gear was missing.
- Has visited Bain Harbour only twice.

## Tobin and Quenna (trading partners)

- Began 19 years ago: Quenna, 27, newly master of her own ship the Kittiwake, walked to Kaldemor to buy direct instead of through Selsior middlemen; argued with Tobin about the price of cider for an afternoon.
- Every autumn Kaldemor's harvest carts go to Bain Harbour with cider, flour, pottery and woodwork, and return with salt, dried fish, rope and spices.
- Quenna still comes to Kaldemor's harvest fair each autumn to settle prices with Tobin over Rosa's bread.
- Each thinks the other the shrewder bargainer; Tobin says she is the only person who can out-talk him.
- Last autumn one harvest cart never reached the harbour; carts now travel with hired guards.

## Midbarium leaders

- Old One Varra (Brenna): chosen 31 years ago, Old One 25 years, died in her sleep at Brenna House 6 years ago, aged 81. Sister of Ahvi, so also Odda's cousin.
- The Old One before Varra was a Tolmach (unnamed).
- Hask's choosing: 6 years ago, one night in the great hall; Odda hosted, Thrun drummed till dawn, Ahvi sang the long song, Dorrek argued for a Kragvar Old One. The Uskari chose Tavi the same night.

### Hask (old_one_hask)

- Male (Zirra's younger brother). Age 48. Uskari chieftain from 15 years ago (age 33) for 9 years; Old One 6 years (chosen at 42).
- Predecessor as Uskari chieftain: Orvek, died of a winter fever 15 years ago.
- Never married, no children. Zirra is "many years" older (no exact gap set).

### Dorrek (dorrek_kragvar)

- Age 45. Chieftain 11 years.
- Uncle Baruk, Ulm's younger brother and the previous Kragvar chieftain, died 11 years ago in a rockfall on the old north cliff stair while inspecting it. Dorrek dug for him.
- Keeps a secret copy of Brakka's stairway sketch.

### Sennu (sennu_tolmach)

- Female. Age 50. Chieftain 13 years. Predecessor: old Bekku (no kin, dead).
- No spouse mentioned. Does not yet know about the dry spring Ennek is hiding.

### Tavi (tavi_uskari)

- Gender unset: no pronouns used for Tavi. Age 39. Left the rim at 17; trained in Selsior at 18; adventured 2 more years; came home at 20 to hunt under Orvek, then Hask. Chieftain 6 years.

### Odda (odda_brenna)

- Age 68. Brenna chieftain 20 years (since 48). Married to Thrun.

## Calyra (eldest_calyra)

- Age 74. Began teaching at the Grove School at 25; taught 40 years; Eldest 9 years (chosen at 65).
- Predecessor: Eldest Ferris, died 9 years ago.
- Never married, no children.

## Calyra and Odda (old friends)

- Met 47 years ago: Odda, 21, a young Brenna envoy, got lost on the Lantern Paths after dark in her first week; Calyra, 27 and in her third year of teaching, found her and walked her home.
- Odda stayed three winters. Odda taught Calyra bonesetting and highland herbs; Calyra taught Odda forest plant-lore and the oldest Magician script.
- Every spring since: Odda writes a letter ahead, then visits a few weeks later with highland herbs and goes home with forest herbs. Now travels with Uskari escorts.
- Calyra sets the Council aside for that week. Odda says Calyra has been telling people what to do since 27.

## Rook (rook_tallow)

- Gender unset: no pronouns used for Rook. Age 41. A chandler (candles and lamp wicks) with a workshop on Workshops Row.
- Great-grandmother was one of the Walkers; the family has kept the same workshop since.
- Chosen Caller this spring; first time as Caller.

## Rook and Aveline (frequent correspondents)

- Began 9 years ago, before Rook was Caller: Rook wrote an indignant letter about Guard patrols stopping short of the road to Urbanograd. Aveline replied herself, three pages, agreeing with half. Patrols were extended the next year.
- Letters roughly every fortnight; they argue about guilds, theatre, the Great Bell, whether a kingdom needs a crown.
- Met in person once: Walkout Day 6 years ago; Aveline watched Juna Marrow's play standing in the crowd; Rook found her an upturned crate.
- Rook reads the funniest parts aloud at the Table with her blessing; she now writes some on purpose. Rook thinks she argues fairly; she says she writes to the person, not the post.

## Marrun (keeper_marrun)

- Age 81. Walked the Deep Watch since 16 (65 years). Keeper of the Oath 22 years. Predecessor: Keeper Ardith, died 22 years ago.
- Never married, no children ("the Watch was his marriage").
- Grisel was his watch partner for nearly 30 years.
- 50 years ago (Marrun 31): Grisel was on shift the night the expedition came down; Marrun was asleep after a watch and Grisel sent for him.
- Has been to the surface (Selsior) once, 4 years ago; stood under the Great Bell; found the sky too large.

## Marrun and Aveline (sworn allies)

- Sworn 15 years ago, in the first year of Aveline's reign: she came down the Upper Stair, the first reigning monarch to do so, walked a full shift of the Deep Watch beside Marrun, and they swore in the Oath Hall.
- Terms: the crown answers every message from below; Cavelor sends word of every change in the seal.
- The Swordmaster's oath (canon, decided by Alon 2026-10-15): since the alliance, every Royal Guard warrior who passes the Swordmaster's trial (the game's 3rd job, level 70) walks down the Upper Stair and swears the oath in the Oath Hall before the Keeper of the Oath: to stand with the guardians if the seal fails and carry Cavelor's warnings to the surface. At the alliance, every serving Swordmaster and Grand Swordmaster (Aubrey among them; Rhys, then a Swordsman, was in the queen's escort and swore it later on passing the Swordmaster's trial) went down to swear it. Rowena swore it 10 years ago. Marrun hears every oath and keeps a page of the names.
- Letters by messenger each month since. Aveline has been down twice more (three visits in all).
- Aveline trusts Marrun most outside her family and fears she cannot make the kingdom believe him. Marrun: she is the first on the surface who listened to him to the end; he worries she carries too much.

## Quenna (harbour_master_quenna)

- Age 46. Went to sea as a deck girl at 12; master of her own ship the Kittiwake by 27. Harbour Master 8 years.
- Has skippered Bain Harbour's race boat for 14 summers (Dunstan builds it).
- Knows about Ambrose's locked chart; shares its worst marks with Iska each summer.
- Has not decided whether to let Jowan crew in the race.

## Iska (first_warden_iska)

- Age 41. First Warden 7 years. Skippered Goa Harbour's boat 12 summers.
- The Gap Runner was built by Tamsin after Goa's last win and has never beaten Bain Harbour.
- Pretends not to know Arlo sneaks onto the quay at dawn.

## Quenna and Iska (rival racers and friends)

- Met 22 years ago as young crew: a squall dismasted Bain's boat off a rocky headland; Goa's boat turned back and towed it clear. Iska (19) threw the line, Quenna (24) caught it. Neither finished; the race was declared void, the only time ever. Both crews spent the night in the nearest cove.
- Rival skippers for the last 12 summers: Bain 9 wins, Goa 3; four of Bain's wins by a single length. Goa's last win was 6 summers ago.
- Custom: the losing skipper buys the first round at the finish feast.
- At the feast they now compare sea-creature sightings. Quenna thinks Iska the finest pilot alive (never says so); Iska thinks Quenna the loudest, bravest and luckiest sailor on Veligor and her best friend outside her harbour.

## Set by the coordinator (applies everywhere)

- Geography (canon, decided by Alon 2026-10-15, from the game's world map): Selsior in the middle; the World Tree north of it; Midbarium northwest; Fairy Forest northeast; Urbanograd in the west; Goa Harbour on the west coast; Kaldemor in the southeast, inland; Bain Harbour on the east coast.
- Elite and Epic monsters are called "Disturbance-touched" in the world; the worst are "deeply touched".
- The first standing rank is "Neighbour" (was "Stranger"): the player is a native.

- Today is early summer, 50 years after the expedition. The Bain-Goa race is later this summer. Midbarium's dry spring "went dry this summer" means in the past few weeks.
- Tavi (Uskari chieftain) and Rook (Caller of the Open Table) have no stated gender: never use he/she for them; use their names or they/them.
