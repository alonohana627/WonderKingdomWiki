# Established facts: midbarium

Written by NPC lore writers so later writers stay consistent. Not shown on the wiki.

## Korrin (korrin_kragvar)

- Age 9. Brakka is 10 years older (19). Korrin is Dorrek and Gorra's only son and youngest child.
- Korrin and Kesh met as small children hiding under the same cistern lid; Gorra and Yarrek share terrace cisterns.
- Secret lookout on a ledge above the terraces with a low stone wall the boys built together; Brakka has secretly inspected it (she knows of it).
- Korrin may sit on the woodpile by the council fire while Ulm tends it; Ulm tells him the songs of the Kragvar who stayed beneath the Tree.
- Korrin copies Bekk (ties his boots like him); follows him in his admiring crowd.
- Twice the boys have seen something moving on the far canyon bridge after dark (Vosk's gnawed ropes); they have told no one.

## Ennek (ennek_tolmach)

- Age 22. Took his first caravan up alone at 16. Corwell hires him for nearly every lowland caravan.
- Father: Torvo, a Tolmach herder (Sennu's husband), lost in a winter storm on the high pastures when Ennek was 4 (18 years ago). Dead.
- The dry spring is the Greywater spring, in a cleft halfway down the south path. Ran even in the driest year of Mabbu's girlhood, one of the few springs on the path that did (others survived too; not the only one). Went dry at the start of this summer (a few weeks ago). Ennek carries extra water skins; has told no one, including Sennu.
- Ketta: they grew up teasing each other; Ennek guided her down the canyon road when she left at 17; last spring he met her on the south path on her way home. Sweethearts since last summer. He has never pressed her about the cave.

## Gorra (gorra_kragvar)

- Age 43. Born in the Kragvar Quarter. Married Dorrek when both were young.
- Learned from Old Marn, the previous cistern keeper; Marn died 18 years ago and Gorra has kept the cisterns since.
- Keeps tallies scratched on the wall of her cistern house above the terraces.
- The leaking cistern is the Founders' Cistern, the oldest on the upper terrace; loses a finger's depth every few nights; faint trickle heard from deeper in the rock. Gorra has not told Dorrek how worried she is.
- Sends Ulm a jug of cistern water every evening. Shares terrace cistern water with Yarrek's family.

## Kesh (kesh_tolmach)

- Age 9. Has tended the kids of the herd since last year; not trusted with grown goats. Names kids things like Grumble, Biter, Lady Snort.
- Yarrek sits beside the pen of the three wild goats every evening.
- Runs errands between Harra's stall and Corwell's counter; Harra and Corwell argue over prices all day and share a pot of tea when the market closes.
- Keeps a notched stick counting eagles seen from the lookout, and a second stick for things he cannot name: two notches, both for something on the far canyon bridge after dark.
- Mabbu's grandchildren: Ennek and Kesh (no others named).

## Lessa (lessa_brenna)

- Age 42. Set her first bone at 15; healing in earnest since 20 (22 years). Learned bonesetting from Odda; keeps a book of Odda's forest-herb lore by her bed.
- Husband: Corrick, a Brenna storyteller, died of a lung fever when Neffa was 2 (8 years ago). Dead.
- Neffa born 10 years ago (age 10).
- Visits Mabbu every few days with salve for her knees.
- Monster bites fester; she cleans them with rimwort, a grey bitter herb that grows only on the far slopes beyond the canyons. Stock nearly gone; Uskari used to bring it back from hunts. She is rationing it and counting bites.
- This year treated a Kragvar mason bitten on the far canyon bridge by something he never saw.

## Ahvi (ahvi_brenna)

- Age 76. Varra was 11 years older (would be 87). Never married, no children.
- Taught by old Hadda, the previous song-keeper (dead), from age 8; song-keeper 35 years, since Hadda died.
- Sang the long song at Varra's choosing (31 years ago) and through the night of Hask's choosing; at dawn added Hask's name after Varra's.
- Took Neffa as pupil 4 years ago (Neffa then 6).
- Meets Imre on the high terrace on clear nights to swap star stories and old songs.
- The verse: a short, rarely sung verse in the oldest part of the long songs, among Age of Division chieftains, about a season before the Burning when plateau beasts grew wild (biting goats, man-hunting cats, unsettled birds). Ahvi has sung it alone nightly this summer; Odda has not answered whether to sing it.

## Brakka (brakka_kragvar)

- Age 19. Apprenticed to Vosk at 15 (4 years).
- The last new route down the cliff was Durra's Stair, cut about a hundred years ago by a Kragvar mason named Durra (dead); there is a Kragvar song about it.
- Was 8 when Baruk died. The old north cliff stair was patched after the rockfall, not rebuilt.
- Her planned stairway runs along sounder rock farther south, landings cut into the cliff; she now also draws gates for it.
- She was with Vosk at dawn both times the far canyon bridge ropes were found gnawed.
- Does not know Dorrek keeps a copy of her sketch.

## Ketta (ketta_uskari)

- Age 21. Left the rim at 17; adventured 3 years (Selsior, Kaldemor farms, the harbour towns, Urbanograd, Fairy Forest); came home last spring at 20.
- Father died before she could remember him (unnamed). Mother: Sarra, an Uskari tracker, fell from the rim on a winter hunt when Ketta was 12. Both dead. No siblings.
- Zirra taught her the bow from 12 to 17.
- Scar across her left shoulder; she has told three different joking stories about it.
- Sheltered from a dust storm in a deep cave in the canyons below town on the last days of her journey home. What she saw is unknown (keep open). She refuses to patrol the trail stretch that passes the cave.
- Now hunts and patrols with the trail watch; takes unwanted night watches.

## Mabbu (mabbu_tolmach)

- Age 77. Sennu born when she was 27; Harra is 44 (born when Mabbu was 33).
- Husband: Garrak, a Tolmach herder known for his nose for weather; married over 30 years; died 20 years ago of a long illness after a last winter in the home camp. Dead.
- Lived through the driest year anyone in the camps remembers as a girl; the Greywater spring kept running then.
- Knees gave out about 5 years ago on a steep climb above the terraces; keeps the home camp since (tents and stone shelters closest to town, for elders, small children and stores).
- Does not yet know the Greywater spring is dry. Has started secretly testing her knees on longer morning walks.

## Neffa (neffa_brenna)

- Age 10. Was 2 when her father Corrick died; was 4 when Varra died (remembers her faintly).
- Ahvi took her as pupil 4 years ago after hearing her repeat an evening's singing the next morning (as Hadda chose Ahvi).
- The long song of the Old Ones takes most of a night. Neffa can sing three-quarters without a slip; always stumbles on Varra's verse.
- Counts her restarts scratched under her bed (hundreds).
- Has overheard Ahvi's beast verse through the wall; Ahvi told her it was "not for learning yet".

## Thrun (thrun_brenna)

- Age 70. Drummer of the Gatherings since 25 (45 years), succeeding an unnamed old drummer whose hands grew stiff.
- Married Odda the year after she came home from Fairy Forest (about 43 years ago; Odda ~25).
- Friends with Vosk since boyhood; Vosk rebuilt the great drum's stand when both were young and keeps it sound. They sit together at every feast.
- The drum: frame of Kragvar-cut timber bound with rope, taller than a man. Hide taken in Thrun's grandfather's time from a great horned plateau beast. Split across its face at the first feast this spring; patched, but it "mutters". New hide must come from a great horned plateau beast; few seen in years, and those seen are wild.
- Went quiet for a week when Corrick died (8 years ago).

## Ulm (ulm_kragvar)

- Age 74. Keeper of the council fire 40 years (since 34); was a mason before. Sleeps in a small room behind the hearth.
- When the Kragvar chieftain before Baruk died, the family looked to Ulm first; he refused (the keeper does not leave the fire), so his younger brother Baruk was chosen.
- Kept the fire alone the night Baruk died (11 years ago) while Dorrek dug.
- Wife: Hessa, a Kragvar stone-carver, died 16 years ago. Dead.
- Hask sits with him late after councils. Brakka brings him wood (when she remembers).
- Knows there is a city of guardians beneath the Tree; does not know whether any of its people are Kragvar or remember the names Midbarium sings.

## Vosk (vosk_kragvar)

- Age 71. Mother Kelda (dead) was the eldest sister of Ulm and Baruk, much older than them; so Vosk is Dorrek's cousin though nearly Ulm's age. Uses her reground splicing awl.
- Started on bridges as a boy under an unnamed old bridge-keeper (dead). Never married, no children.
- Checks the drum stand's lashings before every feast; has promised Thrun to help fit the new hide.
- After the gnawing: spliced both breaks, doubled the lashings, no longer sends young ones out alone. Gnawing is on the side away from town. Has not raised his worry in council.
- The bitten mason (Lessa's) was working on the far canyon bridge earlier this year; unnamed.

## Yarrek (yarrek_tolmach)

- Age 47. Parents both dead (unnamed Tolmach herders); father taught him the herd, mother taught him to sing to it. Walks at the back of the herd.
- Married Harra 15 years ago. Kesh is their only child.
- The three goats slipped away one night last month and came back up from the valley on their own two days later. Won't eat from his hand, butt the fence.
- Now counts the herd twice each evening.

## Imre (imre)

- No pronouns used for Imre (gender unset). Age unset.
- Grew up in the lowlands; learned the stars from old star tables in Selsior's Scholars' Quarter.
- Came to Midbarium 5 years ago (after Hask's choosing) meaning to stay one summer; arrived at a Tolmach fire on the canyon road after dark.
- Lodges in a narrow room above Corwell's storeroom at the Trading Post; pays part of the rent in star charts that Corwell pins behind his counter.
- Keeps a book matching lowland star stories to Brenna song names; secretly composing a star song for a gathering; has not told Ahvi.
- On a handful of cold, still nights this year, stars low toward the island's heart have shimmered as if through heat; Imre logs dates; told only Ahvi. Cause unknown.

## Bekk (bekk_uskari)

- Age 18. Father Haldo, an Uskari hunter, was gored and killed by a great horned plateau beast while tracking it alone on the far slopes 13 years ago (Bekk 5). Dead.
- Uskari take scouts onto the trail watch at 16 after a year carrying water and messages; Bekk did his year at 15, joined at 16.
- Something raids the high pastures (goats taken from herd edges; wide, heavy, deep tracks). Bekk wonders if it is a great horned beast (unknown). Has heard of Thrun's drum.
- Has noticed Korrin copying his boots; quietly checks Korrin's knots.

## Skarra (skarra_uskari)

- Age 41. Never left the rim to adventure. Joined the trail watch at 16; made captain 10 years ago by Hask (then Uskari chieftain).
- Friendly rivals with Tavi since Tavi came home (19 years ago).
- Hide map: red ochre dot plus date for every sighting since she became captain. This summer: dots on the high pastures, the south path, the far end of the far canyon bridge, and the trail stretch past Ketta's cave. Shows it only to Tavi.
- Has stopped sending hunters to the far slopes (game, hides, rimwort).
- Has measured the pasture beast's tracks; they frighten her.
- Dorrek asked her last month how young the Uskari take recruits; she said 16.
- Lets Ketta trade away the cave stretch without comment.

## Zirra (zirra_uskari)

- Age 63, 15 years older than Hask. Mother died when Hask was 6 (Zirra 21); father a hunter, often away; both dead (unnamed). Zirra largely raised Hask; taught him his first bow.
- Never married, no children.
- Workshop open on the rim. Best staves from twisted trees low in the canyon valleys; those valleys are now red on Skarra's map; wood stock at a lifetime low.
- Agrees with Vosk that nobody else's rope is quite good enough.
- Made Ketta a new bow and left it at her fire without a word.
