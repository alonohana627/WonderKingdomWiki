# Established facts: fairy-forest

Written by NPC lore writers so later writers stay consistent. Not shown on the wiki.

## Tansy (tansy_teahouse)

- Age 36. Runs the Dewdrop Tea House, in the hollow of an old tree on the Lantern Paths; tea room downstairs, a few guest rooms upstairs.
- Mother Hollyn (dead) ran the tea house before her; died 11 years ago. Tansy has run it alone since.
- Liri's father Edrin (dead), a beekeeper; died of a winter fever when Liri was 1 (8 years ago). Tansy keeps his hives; their honey goes into the honey cakes.
- Liri is 9; started at the Grove School 1 year ago (aged 8).
- Dew tea is gathered at dawn from leaves around the house.
- Lyra sings on the bridge outside the tea house on fine evenings. Halcy supplies the garlands and cake baskets weekly.
- Aspen sits by the tea house window most afternoons; Sefa often joins him there.

## Amaryl (amaryl_teacher)

- Age 63. Born on the Lantern Paths. Was 14 when Calyra began teaching (49 years ago) and was her pupil from then.
- Calyra's lesson to her: sit beside a young oak for a week and say what it wanted. Amaryl now gives students who snap living wood the same lesson.
- Learned Growing from Teacher Ostra (dead), the previous Teacher of Growing. Amaryl has taught Growing for 30 years.
- Never married, no children. Lives alone in a beech tree home she coaxed at 30.
- Is sometimes called to the Heart Tree to speak to the Grove Council about the school (she is not a councillor).
- Teaches outdoors in a grove of young trees beside the school, shaped by every class.
- Means to keep Siora; has not told her.

## Aspen (aspen_elder)

- Age 72; was 22 when news of the expedition's return reached the forest (50 years ago). He was mending a bridge on the Lantern Paths; a traveller ran up the path with the news; the forest gathered under the Heart Tree that evening.
- Apprenticed with Oriel under Master Brannoc (dead). Oriel is 4 years older (76).
- Kept the living homes on the Lantern Paths for forty-odd years; retired 6 years ago (aged 66). Linnet now keeps them.
- Helped coax homes for some of the first Children who settled in the forest.
- Wife Elda died 18 years ago. Only child Fennel (Lyra's mother) died 22 years ago, when Lyra was 10. Lyra's father, an unnamed travelling fiddler, died before Lyra could remember him. Aspen raised Lyra from 10. Lyra is 32.
- Meets Sefa over tea at the Dewdrop most afternoons.
- Keeps a box of letters and drawings from the year the Children first came; never shown anyone.

## Caelum (caelum_scholar)

- Age 38. Isaura's student since 18 (20 years). Came to the Grove School at 9.
- Born on the Lantern Paths, son of a lantern-glass grinder; both parents dead.
- The "wrong" drawer holds 31 failed theories; he rereads it once a year on the anniversary of the first.
- Wynne (19) has twice corrected a date he copied wrong. He does not know about her numbering gap.
- Works with Juniper on her notebooks in his study at the Heart Tree.
- Has never seen Hesper's list of what attacked travellers (quest hook).
- Tansy keeps a honey cake aside for him on late nights.

## Halcy (halcy_child)

- No pronouns used (as for all Children). No date given for Halcy's arrival; only "among the first", when Children were still a rare sight.
- Rarely talks about life in the Tree; answers with questions about the asker's home.
- Meets newer Children when they come down and settles them. Guided Niv, and introduced Niv to Oriel.
- Met Calyra before she was Eldest by sitting at the back of her listening lessons; Calyra asked Halcy's opinion of her teaching after a month.
- Delivers garlands and baskets to Tansy's tea house personally each week.
- Has lately been weaving spare garlands and setting them aside, unexplained (open hook).

## Hesper (hesper_herbalist)

- Age 70. Came to the Herb Gardens at 15 as a weeding girl under Mistress Yarrow (dead). Mistress of the Herb Gardens 33 years, since Yarrow died.
- Herb Gardens: terraced beds and glasshouse-hollows below the Heart Tree; Hesper has a potting shed with a bench beside it where Briony sits.
- Never married, no children.
- Started her list of what attacked travellers 2 years ago; written in the back of her seed book. Has shown it to no one at the Heart Tree.
- Gives patients a honey drop "for being brave".
- Has told Celandine once that she is the most careful herbalist she ever trained. Has not said whether she approves of Celandine's Forest Edge plant trip.

## Isaura (isaura_records)

- Age 72. Came to the Heart Tree record rooms at 22 as reader and assistant to Keeper Anselm (dead), the summer the expedition returned (50 years ago). Keeper of the Old Records 30 years, since Anselm died. Has led the seal scholars since then.
- The forest turned to its records once the wilds began to change and the damage became clear; no date set for that realisation.
- Husband Corvin died 15 years ago.
- Daughter Mirren (Wynne's mother) lives in Selsior with her husband and keeps a bookbinder's shop; they play no part. Wynne (19) came to live with Isaura at 14, 5 years ago.
- Isaura's private theory: the most important parts of the seal's making were passed by word of mouth and never written. She has not shared it with her scholars; she has told Calyra.
- She does not know about Wynne's numbering gap.
- Isaura and Calyra have known each other most of their lives.

## Kestrel (kestrel_adventurer)

- Age 23. Grew up in an orchard family on farmland near Kaldemor; parents (unnamed) are alive there and play no part.
- Came to the Grove School at 10 (after accidentally setting a hayrick alight); finished at 19; on the roads 4 years since.
- Unpaid; clears monsters from roads between towns.
- Sleeps in the edge watch hut when she stops at the Forest Edge.
- Thorne half suspects her of the night tricks; she has truthfully denied it. She does not know about Orla.
- Is thinking of offering to walk Alder home at the end of term; has not yet.

## Liri (liri_student)

- Age 9 (matches Tansy's entry). Lives at the Dewdrop Tea House. Does not remember her father Edrin.
- Can sit still in Sorrel's lessons for almost a quarter of an hour.
- Plan: sneak past the last lanterns to the Forest Edge to glimpse a monster from far off; has worked out when the edge watch changes. Told only Lumi. No date set for the attempt.
- Does not know Tansy suspects or that Lumi is wavering.

## Merrow (merrow_gardener)

- Age 41. Son of a deadfall gatherer from the forest's lower paths; both parents dead. Small gift.
- Hired by Hesper (after she saw him rescue a bird's nest) more than 20 years ago.
- Married to Celandine 12 years. No children.
- His fear of the Edge: years ago he saw the edge watch carry in a badly hurt guard (unnamed) from the woods beyond.
- Celandine's Edge plant is left unnamed.
- Hook: something has been digging up his seedling beds at night (unexplained).

## Oriel (oriel_treewright)

- Age 76. Apprenticed young to Master Brannoc (dead), who held the oldest homes before him. Aspen joined Brannoc 4 years later; friends for more than half a century.
- Never married, no children. Has never cut a living branch.
- Took Linnet as apprentice 6 years ago, when Aspen retired, to keep the Lantern Paths homes.
- Halcy brought Niv to Oriel not long after Niv came down from the Tree.
- Slow-waking homes: only a handful, only the very oldest (shaped by the first Magicians); late buds, thin sap, colder rooms. Nobody else has noticed yet. Oriel privately wonders about the roots and the World Tree; nothing is known.

## Seren (seren_warden)

- Age 41. Joined the edge watch at 19 (22 years ago). Learned from Warden Ebba (dead), who died 10 years ago; Seren has been Warden 10 years.
- The watch is fewer than a dozen strong; the Council has added one or two guards at a time after her requests.
- The watch carries hurt travellers to the Herb Gardens and walks the outer lanterns at night with spears.
- Keeps a bed for Kestrel in the watch hut. Has twice gone past the last lanterns to bring Juniper back.
- Has noticed Thorne's night trickster is harmless; does not know who it is and has let it be.

## Sorrel (sorrel_teacher)

- Age 45. Grew up in the forest; came to the Grove School at 8 as Calyra's listening pupil (37 years ago).
- Came back at 28 (17 years ago) to assist Calyra's listening lessons; Teacher of Listening 9 years, since Calyra became Eldest.
- Never married; lives alone in a small tree home near the school. No family named.
- The unease at the edges: on dawn walks to the outer lanterns, birds start later and stop sooner; a wind note he doesn't recognise; a feeling the woods are "listening back". Unexplained. The edge watch knows him by sight.
- Liri is in his youngest class. Mentors Aurel (one sentence, then sends him home to sleep).

## Thorne (thorne_guard)

- Age 21. Grew up on the Lantern Paths. Small gift; a few restless years at the Grove School, left early.
- Parents (unnamed) moved to Selsior to keep a stall when he was 16 (5 years ago); alive, far away, play no part. He refused to go.
- Joined the edge watch at 18 (3 years ago).
- The tricks have gone on "for some months": things moved a little, nothing taken, mostly to the left. He has suspected Kestrel, two other guards, a student, a squirrel. Checks his kit twice. Has not laughed yet. Does not know it is Orla.

## Wynne (wynne_copyist)

- Age 19. Born in Selsior; grew up in her parents' bookbinding shop (mother Mirren and unnamed father, alive, play no part). Can read a binding's stitching and repairs.
- Came to the forest at 14 (5 years ago). Small gift; sat Grove School lessons. Isaura set her to copying at 16 (3 years ago). Writes to her mother monthly.
- Lives with Isaura; they eat supper together most nights; avoid talking about the seal.
- The gap: found this spring in one old seal record. Several leaves were removed long ago, cleanly and skilfully, and the thread drawn tight again (not rot). Record left unnamed. Told no one.
- Asked Ilwen sideways whether an older shelf list of the oldest shelves exists; he gave page and shelf, didn't ask why. Ilwen greeted her on the stair the day she arrived; they lunch on the great stair's landing on fine days.

## Alder (alder_student)

- Age 16. From Goa Harbour; family of fishers (unnamed, alive there, play no part; they write when a carter heads inland).
- Gift showed at 15: his family's quay lanterns brightened when he was angry, dimmed when sad.
- Came last autumn with a party of carters; first year at the Grove School.
- On the journey, third night, two days short of the forest, something attacked the carts at night; hired guards drove it off; he never saw it clearly. Tells it as a joke.
- Stays with Siora while she practises late. First heard of Kestrel from a traveller at the Dewdrop; walks to the Edge on free afternoons hoping to see her.
- Finds Sorrel's listening easy (high wind sounds like surf). A little frightened of Amaryl.

## Aurel (aurel_teacher)

- Age 22. A student at the Grove School for 10 years (from 9 to 19); learned listening under Calyra with Sorrel assisting. Finished 3 years ago and was asked to stay as junior teacher.
- Teaches the youngest, including Liri and Lumi; has noticed them whispering and stopping when he comes near; decided it is not his business.
- Writes down Sorrel's one-sentence advice after each panic.
- Galen is his older brother (no age gap set; parents not mentioned).

## Briony (briony_councillor)

- Age 68. Thessaly is her older sister (no age gap set). Parents not mentioned.
- Went to the Herb Gardens at 12 to learn under Mistress Yarrow; Hesper (15) arrived in her second year (55 years ago); friends since.
- Taught herb-lore at the Grove School 36 years (from 27 to 63). Councillor 5 years. No spouse or children mentioned.
- Makes Thessaly laugh in meetings on purpose when debates sour; Thessaly hides behind her ledger.
- Has noticed Hesper writing in the back of her seed book; has not asked; has NOT seen the list.

## Faelan (faelan_apprentice)

- Age 21. Grew up on the Lantern Paths; small gift. Apprenticed to Ondine at 16 (5 years ago). Parents not mentioned.
- Vesper keeps a chalk tally of his breakages on the back of the workshop door; she sells his good pieces well.
- Ondine now asks his opinion on glass colours for the outer paths.
- Met Linnet about 2 years ago: dropped a lantern while hanging it from a tree home she was mending; glass in her hair; she made him apologise to the tree. Sweethearts since.
- The gift lantern: throws light like sunlight through leaves; needs eleven shades of green and gold glass, some bought from Lantern Paths glass-grinders, some he must mix. Coins in a jar under his bench (broken twice). Linnet and Ondine not told; Ondine suspects (good green off-cuts left on his bench).
- Counts the times Oriel almost smiles at his jokes.

## Ilwen (ilwen_steward)

- Age 34. Male. Came to the Heart Tree at 22 as steward to Eldest Ferris (12 years ago); has served Calyra the 9 years since. Never taken a holiday in 12 years.
- Would go to Kaldemor's harvest fair if he took one (has read every account of it).
- Waters the Council chamber vines morning and evening; the one time someone else did, half drowned.
- Thessaly checks her promise ledger against his minutes; finds a difference about once a season. Briony brings him gossip and seedcake.
- Knows exactly how many times Seren has asked for guards and how many she got; never says the numbers; has started underlining them.
- Met Wynne on the stair the day she arrived. Has noticed she has been quieter since spring; does not know about the gap.

## Juniper (juniper_adventurer)

- Age 34. Grew up in the forest; middling Magician, remarkable observer; loved the listening lessons (taught then by Calyra). First notebook (beetles) at 11. Parents not mentioned.
- Travelling the wilds 12 years (since 22). More than forty notebooks (where kept not set).
- Observations only (no cause): shy creatures now bold; kinds turning up in new valleys; some larger/bolder/angrier than in old drawings; change sudden in places, gradual in others.
- Stops at the Edge on the way out and back.
- Hook: last autumn lost a notebook in the wilds, dropped climbing out of a ravine with something large below; holds a season's notes from a place she has not been able to reach again.

## Linnet (linnet_treewright)

- Age 22. Grew up on the Lantern Paths; watched Aspen coax rooms as a child. Parents not mentioned.
- Studied Growing under Amaryl at the Grove School; Amaryl told her at the end of schooling she had good hands.
- Apprenticed to Oriel at 16 (6 years ago).
- Sometimes leaves a mend slightly unfinished on purpose so Aspen can point it out at the Dewdrop.
- Knows Faelan is hiding something; doesn't know it's the lantern; lets him keep his secret.
- Has chosen a young tree (shoulder height) on a quiet bend of the Lantern Paths for her someday seedling home; visits every evening; Oriel not told.
- Has not noticed Oriel's slow-waking homes (they are around the Heart Tree, not hers).

## Lumi (lumi_child)

- Child of the Tree: no pronouns; nothing on nature, appearance, origins or age. No date for joining the Grove School.
- In Aurel's and Sorrel's classes with Liri. Sorrel answers Lumi's questions with silence; Lumi then goes quiet and listens.
- Once asked Caelum at the Dewdrop why he kept a drawer of wrong ideas, then called it the cleverest thing Lumi had heard of.
- Visits Sefa in the glade most days; always asks if a story was true; Sefa never says; Lumi keeps score.
- Wavering on Liri's plan from hearing hurt travellers, edge guards and Sorrel's "uneasy edges". Asked Sefa (without saying the secret) what a friend should do; Sefa answered with a story; a week later Lumi still can't tell if it meant tell or don't. Has noticed Tansy watching Liri. Has not told Tansy.

## Lyra (lyra_singer)

- Age 32. Mother Fennel (Aspen's only child) had a fine voice; died of a fever in a hard winter 22 years ago (Lyra 10). Father: unnamed travelling fiddler, died before she could remember.
- Lived with Aspen and Elda from 10; Elda died 4 years later (18 years ago); then Aspen alone raised her. Aspen sang to her on sleepless evenings.
- Aspen hears her nearly every evening; she sometimes changes a word to make him correct her.
- 22 years ago Hollyn fed her honey cakes in the Dewdrop kitchen; Tansy (14) let her sing to guests standing on a chair. Tansy never lets her pay for cake. Lyra has taught Liri rhymes since Liri could talk.
- Sings the Balrog War songs every few evenings; travellers off the roads go still and don't clap.
- Collects variant verses from old people; one war song has three different endings from three singers. Trades songs each spring with the Midbarium visitors (unnamed); some war songs are sung there with same tunes, different words.
- Hook: a fragment of one of the oldest songs about the Magicians at the Tree, lines about what they did "in an order that seems to matter" with unexplained words. Aspen knew half; an unnamed old woman on the lower paths knew more and died last winter. Meaning unknown (open). Lyra hasn't thought to tell the Heart Tree scholars.

## Thessaly (thessaly_councillor)

- Age 71; 3 years older than Briony (68). Parents unnamed, long dead. Never married, no children.
- Taught the forms of spellwork (precise shapes, words and order of a working) at the Grove School for many years.
- Councillor 22 years (since 49). Bought the ledger on her first day; has never missed a meeting. Ledger now 14 volumes, kept in her tree home. Kept promises get one line drawn through; unkept ones are read out at every meeting.
- Counts "we will consider it" as a promise; several of the longest unlined entries are promises to consider Seren's guard requests.
- Lost the choosing to Calyra by a handful of voices 9 years ago; congratulated her publicly; only Briony saw what it cost.
- Defends Council decisions as her own once made.

## Ondine (ondine_lanterns)

- Age 68. Grew up on the Lantern Paths; came to the lantern workshop at 14 (54 years ago) as glass-dust sweeper under Keeper Lisbet (dead). Keeper of the Lanterns 31 years, since Lisbet died. Was 18 when the expedition returned.
- Has made the outer-path lanterns brighter every autumn for the last 19 years; began after an unnamed edge guard told her something came right up to the last lantern. Keeps a workshop book of every increase; never shown to the Heart Tree; nobody has asked.
- Took Faelan on because he described a light by how it would make a person feel (never told him). Keeps the prettiest panes he broke in a box under her bench. Suspects the gift lantern is for Linnet; says nothing.
- Daily price argument: Ondine thinks Vesper charges too much for travelling lights to people who must use the roads; Vesper thinks Ondine charges too little for everything else. Vesper's father, age and other details left unset.

## Siora (siora_student)

- Age 12. From Selsior; parents (unnamed, alive, play no part) keep a small dyer's shop. Writes home monthly, always saying she is doing well.
- Gift showed at 10: shop candles leaned toward her; dye turned brighter. Came to the Grove School at 11; second year.
- Lodges in the school's lodging-hollows with other students from far away.
- Was told in her first winter that students whose gifts never wake are sent home; doesn't know if true (unconfirmed rumour, not set as school policy).
- Spells come out better when Alder makes her laugh; hasn't told him. Has noticed his jokes about the road home are different.
- Hook: one spring evening, alone, a coaxing spell on a young hazel worked perfectly; never since; told no one.

## Niv (niv_child)

- Child of the Tree: no pronouns; nothing on nature, appearance, origins or age. One of the newer Children; no arrival date.
- Met and settled by Halcy; Halcy introduced Niv to Oriel. Lives in a small tree home Oriel tended (one round window). Walks the old homes with Oriel, often near-silent.
- Has noticed Oriel seems worried and lingers in a few of the oldest homes; does not know why and has not asked (does not know about the slow-waking homes).
- Looks toward the World Tree from the edge of the glade; reason never told; others who ask get a smile.
- Fears anything happening to Oriel.

## Sefa (sefa_child)

- Child of the Tree: no pronouns; nothing on nature, appearance, origins or age.
- Trades stories: a child must bring one to get one.
- Answered Lumi's question with a story about two foxes and a frozen pond; Sefa knows what it meant and wants Lumi to work it out.
- Meets Aspen at the Dewdrop window after the glade empties, most afternoons. Is collecting Aspen's stories (Aspen suspects, correctly).
- The great collection is kept on bundles of bark-paper in Sefa's home in the glade. People who planted invented stories found them in it a week later, improved.

## Ailo (ailo_child)

- Child of the Tree: no pronouns; nothing on nature, appearance, origins or age. No date for coming to the gardens.
- Plants Hesper had given up on in the glasshouse-hollows have started to flower. Reason never given.
- Has been quietly reading the gardens' old books about plants near the Forest Edge.
- Most mornings arrives before Merrow and replants the dug-up seedlings; Merrow has noticed; neither mentions it. Cause of the digging still unknown.
- Sometimes pauses at the garden gate in the evening looking toward the outer paths.

## Orla (orla_child)

- Child of the Tree: no pronouns; nothing on nature, appearance, origins or age.
- Rules for tricks: never take, never break, never frighten, never make someone look foolish in front of others. Has never been caught.
- How the spear was moved while Thorne held it is left unexplained.
- Knows Seren has noticed the tricks and let them be.
- After Thorne, has a list of targets; the Grove Council is on it.

## Corisande (corisande_key)

- Age 81. Lives in a tree home halfway along the Lantern Paths. Never married, no children; no heir for the key.
- The key: dark bronze, as long as her hand, bow shaped like a curled leaf; worn on a cord round her neck.
- Grandmother Eglantine (dead) kept it, wore it outside her dress, said it was "kept for when it is wanted"; mother Melusine (dead) had it from Eglantine. Whether anyone kept it before Eglantine is unknown. No family papers about it.
- Corisande took the key when Melusine died, 54 years ago, at 27. Has never been through the door.
- Checks the old wing's door every morning before the Grove School opens; does not touch the lock; chases off students daring each other to touch it. Lately hears faint rustling like pages behind the door on still nights; told nobody.
- Kept bees on the Lantern Paths and sold honey for most of her life; hives now empty (unsteady hands). Small gift (can make a candle lean).
- Neighbour of Aspen for many years; Aspen has never asked her about the key.
