# Established facts: bain-harbour

Written by NPC lore writers so later writers stay consistent. Not shown on the wiki.

## Alwyn (alwyn)

- Age 41. Has worked in the shipyard since early childhood (not "twenty-odd years"); does the heavy work now.
- Mother Senara (Dunstan's wife) painted the names on Dunstan's hulls in gold; died 9 years ago. Alwyn now paints the hull names in her style.
- Wants deeper keels, thicker planking, stronger ribs for deep-water ships; Dunstan refuses ("good enough for Old Bain").
- Tegan has been his friend since they were both children in the yard; she will not tell even Alwyn about the secret race sails.
- Pins Poppy's ship drawings up in the shipyard tool shed.
- Children: Poppy 11 (elder), Kit 9.

## Kit (kit)

- Age 9. Younger brother of Poppy (11).
- Learned most of his 14 knots from Dunstan, the last few from Wilmot the ropemaker.
- Carved "KIT" in crooked capitals with Alwyn's smallest knife inside the new race boat's hull, low near the keel, one evening. Has told nobody.
- Goes to Maren's lessons on Merchant Street. Draws ships with sea monsters in them; argues with Poppy over whose drawings are more accurate.
- Favourite person after Dunstan: Peregrine.

## Lowen (lowen)

- Age 17. Elder brother of Bree (10).
- Signed on as a deckhand aboard Corwin's Heron for its next coastal trading run, via his friend Caspian's word; the Heron had not yet sailed at present day ("just signed on").
- Gwennol shouted; Cobb said little and has not said he is proud.
- Thaddeus coaches him from his bench; Lowen takes the long way round but secretly follows some of the advice.
- The Harbour Master picks the race crew herself.

## Bree (bree)

- Age 10.
- Catches crabs in rock pools under the harbour wall; sells scrubbed shells to children (1 copper small, 2 large); keeps accounts on a slate. Ottilie once praised the slate.
- Hesketh complains about her and saves her the prettiest oyster shells. She feeds Barnacle scraps.
- Has asked Quenna twice to her face to crew the race boat; Quenna laughed both times.

## Corwin (captain_corwin)

- Age 52. Captain of the Heron 20 years. Went to sea as cabin boy; deckhand, bosun, first mate on others' ships; bought a share of the Heron at 32, the rest a few years later.
- The Heron is the most reliable, not the fastest, ship (Seraphine's Bright Morrow is fastest). Keeps every cargo list in a sea chest.
- No wife or children. Caspian's parents are not named (left open).
- Has privately carried his own sighting marks to Ambrose.
- Tristram is his regular hired harpooner. Has taken Lowen on as deckhand on Caspian's word. Has noticed Caspian goes pale in storms; said nothing.
- Argues with Annis about dues, loading order, and casting off under a new moon.

## Cobb (cobb)

- Age 44. Fishes from the Puffin, a small open boat his (paternal) grandfather built more than 60 years ago.
- Father Davey (Wenna's husband) fished from the Puffin; died of a winter fever 12 years ago.
- Nets torn twice this year in long straight rents, both in the deeper water just inside the headland. Wenna mended both. Cobb has not told the council; no longer goes past the middle of the bay.

## Dunstan (dunstan)

- Age 74. Came to the yard at 14 as apprentice to old Master Garth (dead); master of the shipyard 40 years.
- Wife Senara, a fisherman's daughter, painted his hull names for over 30 years; died 9 years ago.
- Has built the harbour's race boat for as long as the council remembers; this summer's is the eighth race boat of his life. Plans kept rolled in oilcloth under his bed. Gave Tegan sail measurements on a scrap of paper.
- Built the mast that broke in the squall 22 years ago (the void race); has overbuilt every race mast since.
- Built the Harbour Watch's launch.
- Has privately inspected scrapes on deep-water hulls and disliked them. Has not looked at Peregrine's faster hull.
- Keeps Poppy's drawings in a box. Does not know about Kit's carving.

## Jowan (jowan)

- Age 18. Joined the Watch last spring, a few months after turning 17.
- Born the year after Quenna took command of the Kittiwake (Quenna was 28). Spent early summers in a basket on its deck.
- Grew up around the Harbour Hall from small boyhood, long before Quenna became Harbour Master (sweets from Ottilie, stories from Mabyn).
- Has never beaten Brisa in a footrace. Has asked Halloran three times for the outer pier watch. Notes odd things on the water in his reports.
- Knows his father keeps a locked chart; has never looked; the key has moved.
- Has not dared ask Quenna directly about crewing the race.

## Poppy (poppy)

- Age 11. Elder sister of Kit.
- Bread round on Merchant Street includes Marisol's, Cordelia's and Ambrose's (a warm roll; he forgets breakfast).
- Sketches from the end of Merchant Street where the road turns; has begun drawing maps of the bay.
- Ambrose invited her in one morning; she now visits after every round and he teaches her (compass, dividers, small writing). He has not shown her the locked drawer; she has noticed it and that the marks are all out past the headland.
- Has not told her parents she wants to be a chartmaker.

## Ambrose (ambrose_chartmaker)

- Age 50. Born on Merchant Street, son of a bookbinder (dead). Apprenticed at 15 to old Hedley, the previous chartmaker; Hedley died 25 years ago and Ambrose took the shop.
- Not a sailor; seasick even in a rowing boat.
- Met Quenna when she came to his shop as a young sailor; married 21 years ago (Quenna 25).
- Started the separate locked chart when frightened sailors began steering around sighting marks on ordinary charts. Marks increase yearly and seem to creep toward the headland. Refuses to guess what they mean.
- Annis and Corwin have both given him marks.

## Annis (captain_annis)

- Age 76. Born on the Waterfront; went to sea at 10; captain of the Saltlark 44 years. Never married, no children.
- Rituals: boots left first, double knot, never on deck; pinch of salt over the stern when casting off. Will not cast off under a new moon.
- On the Saltlark's last voyage, something large brushed the keel bow to stern on a still night in deep water; whole crew felt it. Annis turned home and gave Ambrose the mark next morning. She knows Morwenna has not told her family and has not pressed her.
- Thaddeus: youthful rivals across the bay; quarrelled over who should skipper the race boat. More than 30 years ago Thaddeus had the helm and lost to Goa by a boat's length. Annis says she'd have won; Thaddeus says she'd have lost by two.

## Delphine (delphine)

- Age 43. The Salt and Bread has been kept by her family for 3 generations: grandmother opened it in an old net loft; mother Ysella (also Halloran's mother) kept it, died 8 years ago and left it to Delphine.
- Keeps debts and kindnesses in two columns of one book behind the bar.
- Bread comes from Bryony daily. Lorcan owes her money; she told him to write two endings to his race shanty.
- Halloran drinks there most evenings. She knows the outer pier watch is more than a precaution.
- Keeps a separate ledger of strange sea tales (teller, ship, date, place). Has considered showing it to Ambrose or the council; has done neither.

## Finch (finch)

- Age 10. Mother Liddy sold hot pies on the docks; died of a fever when Finch was 5 (5 years ago). Father a sailor who shipped out before Finch was born and never returned (absent, plays no part).
- Sleeps in the loft above the dock office at the head of the pier (where tally books are kept); Garrow took him in 5 years ago.
- Attends Maren's lessons most mornings; sits next to Kit; fastest at sums; likes the stars best.
- Believes Barnaby's cartwheel-eyes story completely.
- Has tried twice to sneak out along the outer pier at night; caught both times by Brisa, the only one faster than him. She threatened to tell Garrow and has not.

## Garrow (garrow)

- Age 49. Came to the docks at 14 as a porter; foreman 16 years. Never married; lives alone in a narrow house on the Waterfront.
- Not on the harbour council. Once stood outside the Harbour Hall through a whole council meeting holding a drawing of the second pier.
- Hobart taught him to count cargo as a boy porter (35 years ago); they argue counts weekly; Garrow visits the warehouses every evening.
- Gives Tully extra shifts first; knows about his boat savings.
- This spring Halloran asked him to stop unloading at the far end of the outer pier after dark, without saying why. Garrow agreed and uses it as an argument for the second pier.

## Halloran (halloran)

- Age 47; elder brother of Delphine (43). Grew up at the Salt and Bread.
- Father Edwy, a sailor, lost at sea in a winter storm when Halloran was 12 (35 years ago).
- Joined the Harbour Watch at 22 (25 years ago); captain 11 years. The Watch is a handful of people; has a launch built by Dunstan. Halloran is unmarried.
- Drinks at the end of the Salt and Bread bar nearest the door.
- Outer pier secret (told only to Quenna): one morning this spring at low tide he found the Watch's launch hanging from a mooring line pulled taut straight down, and long fresh grooves in the pilings below the waterline. Line went slack before help came. Has posted a night watch on the outer pier since and stands most of it himself.
- Refused Jowan the outer pier watch 3 times so as not to put Quenna's son over that water (Quenna knows what he found). Quenna's lore states she keeps the secret. Has not told Delphine; has not asked Brisa for her list.

## Keziah (keziah)

- Age 23. Grew up on an apple farm in the orchard country outside Kaldemor; parents still farm there (far away, play no part). Left at 17 to guard carts and caravans on inland roads.
- Arrived in Bain Harbour at the end of last winter; had never seen the sea. Signed on with Seraphine; 3 voyages on the Bright Morrow so far. Seraphine has asked her to sign on for the whole summer.
- Gets a bottle of seasickness tonic at Clemence's back door at dawn before every voyage.
- Met Tristram in her first week (both turned down by the same captain the same morning); first job together: clearing the rocks below the headland of oversized crabs. Suspects Tristram has noticed her seasickness; he has said nothing.

## Maren (maren)

- Age 35. Grew up on Merchant Street. Mother Elowen kept the books for Merchant Street merchants; died 10 years ago. Maren teaches from her desk.
- Studied 3 years in Selsior's Scholars' Quarter (ages 18-21); has taught the harbour children 13 years.
- No schoolhouse: lessons every morning in the front room of her narrow house on Merchant Street, about 20 children, some on upturned crates. Children come when they can.
- Asks the council for a schoolhouse every year; always deferred. Brings pupils' work to council meetings.
- Keeps unexplained beach finds on a labelled shelf in the front room: driftwood scored with long straight grooves, an unnamed fish, a shell larger than her hand. No longer makes lessons of them.
- A message in a bottle has washed up twice in her time.

## Morwenna (morwenna)

- Age 38. Born in the lighthouse. Father Kenver, a fisherman (Ysolde's husband), taught her to row and sing at the oars; died suddenly 16 years ago mending nets on the fish market steps.
- Worked the Fish Market a few years, then joined the Saltlark at 26 (12 years ago).
- Husband Tamar worked in the ropewalk on the Waterfront; died of a lung fever the winter after Ossie was born (Ossie barely 1, 7 years ago). Ossie is 8.
- When ashore she lives at the lighthouse in the little room at the bottom of the tower.
- Was at the Saltlark's wheel the night something brushed the keel.
- The lighthouse has 117 stairs.

## Ossie (ossie)

- Age 8. Does not remember his father Tamar; keeps a piece of rope Tamar made, tied in an unnamed knot, under his pillow.
- Helps Ysolde trim wicks, polish the lamp, light it at dusk. Writes down Ysolde's opinions of captains (told not to repeat them).
- Decided to be a keeper last winter after reading one of Ysolde's old logbooks. His own logbook (blue cover) was a gift from Maren: ships (name, time, weather) and birds (with drawings).
- One sleepless night this spring, alone at the top of the tower, saw a pale light moving under the water far out past the headland; logged it; has told no one. Does not know Ysolde has seen the same.

## Tristram (tristram)

- Age 39. Father (unnamed) hunted big fish in a narrow boat, the Patience; died in his bed at a great age 10 years ago. Tristram's harpoon was his father's (shaft replaced 3 times, head never).
- Hunted big fish alone in the Patience from 18 until the deep water changed; the Patience now lies hauled up under a sailcloth on the beach below the Waterfront.
- Stands the Heron's bow from the headland out to the headland back. Has thrown the harpoon at something only twice; says only that he missed once.
- Has noticed Keziah's seasickness; will not mention it.
- Nerissa will not tell even him why she stopped diving; he has not pressed her.

## Ysolde (ysolde)

- Age 70. Keeper 40 years (since 30), never missed a night. Her father, old Peder, kept the light before her; died 40 years ago.
- Husband Kenver (fisherman, Morwenna's father) died suddenly 16 years ago mending nets on the fish market steps; Ysolde lit the lamp that night as usual.
- Morwenna born in the lighthouse 2 years after Ysolde took the light.
- Clemence climbs up once a week with a knee salve; they drink tea at the top. Clemence is the only visitor Ysolde invites.
- Opinions: Annis sails as if the sea were listening; Corwin is "a clock with a hull on it" (a compliment); Seraphine comes in too fast; watched Quenna learn to sail as a deck girl.
- First saw the pale lights three summers ago; about a dozen nights since; always far out past the headland in deep water; seemingly a little closer each summer. Has told no one (not Clemence). Marks them only in the margin of the lighthouse log with a private sign.

## Barnaby (barnaby)

- Age 46. At sea since boyhood; bosun of the Bright Morrow 9 years (since Seraphine bought her). No children.
- Scars: left hand (swordfish story), chin (smugglers or a gull), forearm (true: parted line in a storm; caught it before it took a sailor's head off; Seraphine witnessed it; he never tells that one).
- The eyes: last autumn, moonlit night, deep water, alone on deck while the watch slept at the wheel. Story has never grown in the telling.
- Only Finch and Seraphine have never laughed at it; Seraphine heard it once in silence and shut herself in her cabin.
- Used to sling a hammock on deck; now sleeps in the forecastle; blames his back.
- Keeps barley sugar in his coat pocket for Finch every voyage.

## Brisa (brisa)

- Age 19. Grew up on the Waterfront; mother a fish-gutter, father a carter. Parents moved inland to a cousin's farm near Kaldemor 3 years ago (far away, play no part); Brisa stayed, rents a room above a sailmaker's loft. Letters: parents at harvest, she at midwinter.
- Joined the Watch last spring. Reads Jowan's reports secretly; never buttons her coat.
- Caught Finch twice on the outer pier at night since that watch began; has not told Garrow.
- Her list: sewn notebook inside her coat, 60+ entries with date and teller. Does not know about Delphine's ledger.

## Seraphine (captain_seraphine)

- Age 36. To sea at 14 as a ship's girl on a coasting trader. Bought the Bright Morrow at 27 (9 years ago), old and slow, from a merchant leaving the sea; rebuilt rig (taller masts, more canvas); fastest in harbour within a year, unbeaten since.
- Carries spices, cloth, urgent goods. Comes into harbour under too much sail (Garrow swears, Ysolde scolds; she blows Ysolde a kiss).
- Refuses to describe what she saw; buys the table a drink to end the subject. Has told no one. Has stopped taking dares involving deep water at night.
- Keeps a blank chart sheet in her cabin for waters past Ambrose's charts.

## Caspian (caspian)

- Age 20. Navigator of the Heron for 2 years (since 18); cabin boy on the Heron from 13 (7 years ago).
- Father Petroc (invented, dead), a sailor on a coasting trader, lost with his ship in a winter storm when Caspian was 7 (13 years ago). Caspian watched from the harbour wall with his mother; source of his storm fear.
- Mother Kerra (invented), Corwin's younger sister, remarried a lamp-oil merchant from Selsior and moved there 5 years ago (far away, plays no part). Writes every midwinter.
- Corwin looked after Caspian and Kerra after Petroc's death.
- Learned star names at Maren's lessons; learned navigation from the Heron's old navigator Jago (invented, died 2 years ago).
- Met Lowen on the harbour wall teaching him the stars (Caspian a cabin boy, Lowen a boy at the fish stall).
- Believes no one knows of his storm fear.

## Clemence (clemence)

- Age 52. Harbour healer 22 years (since 30). Born in the narrow house on the Waterfront where she works, a few doors from the Salt and Bread; front door and a back door onto the alley.
- Mother Tressa (invented, dead), harbour healer before her, died 22 years ago. Never married, no children.
- Seasickness tonic: Tressa's recipe, ginger and peppermint; buys ginger from Marisol. Ginger has come late; twice this spring she made the tonic weaker.
- Friendship with Ysolde began 16 years ago the day Kenver died: Wenna, mending nets beside Kenver on the fish market steps, sent a boy for Clemence; Clemence climbed the lighthouse to tell Ysolde and stayed the night at the top of the tower.
- Keeps a casebook of unexplained wounds (date, patient, ship, wound, patient's account): a deckhand with a row of coin-sized round welts on the forearm; a dockworker with a long straight shallow scrape on the calf; two stings matching no jellyfish she knows. Has shown it to no one.
- Does not know about Ysolde's lights; has noticed Ysolde looks past the headland longer than she used to.

## Marisol (marisol)

- Age 45. Came to Merchant Street at 16 to sweep for old Ferrand (invented, dead), the previous spice merchant; bought the shop from him at 24 (21 years ago).
- Pepper and cloves come on the Bright Morrow; tea in lead-lined chests that Hobart complains about counting. Poppy brings her a salt roll every morning.
- Partnership with Cordelia: separate shops, shared purse for 12 years; they hire cargo space by the hold and split cost and risk. Has promised to go halves on Cordelia's second shop once cargoes settle.
- Has spoken for the merchants on the council for 10 years. Counted the votes in advance when the council chose Quenna 8 years ago.
- This spring: 3 cargoes weeks late (captains hugging the coast); a consignment of blue dye never came (its coaster turned back and put into another harbour along the coast; master won't sail that stretch). Remembers the Kaldemor cart lost last autumn would have carried her spices home.
- Keeps a private book of late and missing cargoes (ship, route, days lost). Has not shown Cordelia how worried she is.

## Thaddeus (thaddeus)

- Age 79. Master of his own coasting ship, the Cormorant, for 30 years; gave up command at 65 (14 years ago) and sold her to a captain from another harbour, who still sails her.
- Wife Loveday (invented, dead), mended sails on the Waterfront; married 40 years; died 10 years ago. No children. Lives alone in a little house near his bench.
- The race: 33 years ago, aged 46 (Annis 43). He and Annis quarrelled so long over the helm the council nearly chose someone else; helm went to Thaddeus. Led most of the way; in the last mile the wind dropped on him and held for Goa; lost by a boat's length. Never skippered the race again; next summer the helm went to another (unnamed) captain; Annis never had the helm at all.
- Privately thinks Annis the finest sailor in the harbour.
- Has noticed Lowen avoiding him and improving as advised; pleased, has told nobody.
- Has told Lorcan the race story 4 times for his shanty, in exchange for drinks.

## Wenna (wenna)

- Age 71. Mother (unnamed, dead) mended nets on the same fish market steps; Wenna sat beside her from age 5.
- Husband Davey (already set) fished from the Puffin, built by Davey's father; died 12 years ago. Wenna mended his last net and gave it to Cobb.
- Has argued with Gwennol for about 20 years (Cobb and Gwennol married about 20 years); respects her, will never admit it.
- Sewed Lowen a double-seamed canvas kit bag for the Heron voyage after calling him a fool to the market.
- Was mending beside Kenver when he died 16 years ago; sent a boy for Clemence; held his hand. Keeps his place on the step clear.
- Cobb's torn nets: long straight rents longer than her arm, clean but edges pulled, not cut. Has kept a strip of the torn net rolled in her basket under spare twine; shown nobody.
- Barnacle sleeps beside her and takes her fish heads. Nerissa now sits on the step below hers on days she would have dived; Wenna has asked her nothing.

## Barnacle (barnacle)

- Female tabby, white chest, one torn ear. About 7 years old. Came ashore as a kitten down the gangplank of a coasting ship about 7 years ago and stayed.
- Hesketh and Gwennol each claim to have named her; they argue about it.
- Daily round: dawn at the Fish Market stalls (Hesketh's best scraps; Bree's secret fish), midday on the warmest step beside Wenna (fish heads), evening at the Salt and Bread's back door, night hunting rats in Hobart's warehouses (he leaves a side door open a crack). Jowan salutes her.
- Stopped going near the water a little over a year ago (not "since the Disturbance began": the Disturbance is 50 years old and she is 7): froze on the slipway one morning, fled, has not gone closer than the top step since. Some older fishermen now watch where she will and will not sit.

## Hobart (hobart)

- Age 78. Came to the warehouses as a porter at 20 (58 years ago); took the keys at 28 and has kept the warehouses 50 years. Predecessor an old warehouseman (unnamed, dead).
- Deaf in the LEFT ear since his thirties: a block came loose in a gale and swung past his head.
- Never married, no children. Lives in two rooms above the oldest warehouse.
- Counts every cargo with chalk, tally on the door post. Ottilie sometimes brings her ledger down to compare; the only figures he respects.
- Leaves a side door open for Barnacle (rats).
- Four crates of dark iron-bound wood with an unrecognised stamp at the back of the oldest warehouse; listed on the illegible first page of the oldest ledger; never opened; he claims they date from Old Bain's day. Mabyn jokes they hold Old Bain's spare boat-hooks.
- Keeps a second tally on the door post of ships expected versus arrived; the gap is widening.

## Lorcan (lorcan)

- Age 34. Son of Pascoe (invented, dead), a fiddler who played on ships and waterfront inns along the coast; grew up in a dozen ports. Pascoe died when Lorcan was 20 (14 years ago). Lorcan sold the fiddle within a year to pay a debt; regrets it.
- Came to Bain Harbour at 22 (12 years ago); Ysella hired him at the Salt and Bread for supper and a corner to sleep; Delphine kept him on after Ysella died.
- His debt is a tab down a whole column of Delphine's book. Not a gambler or drunkard; generous with money he lacks (rounds, loans). Bought Thaddeus 4 drinks for the race story.
- Race shanty: a verse per headland, one for Quenna, one for Thaddeus's lost race; chorus widely known; no ending yet. Has written the winning ending 5 times, the losing one never (calls it bad luck; Annis agrees).
- Has stopped singing the old sea-monster songs; once a captain (unnamed) asked him gently to sing something else.

## Mabyn (mabyn)

- Age 74. Went to sea at 15 as a cook's girl; a ship's cook on half a dozen coasting ships for most of her life.
- Was cook aboard the Sandpiper (small coasting ship, long since broken up) when Quenna, 12, joined it as a deck girl (34 years ago); fed and mothered her. Friends since.
- Came ashore 21 years ago (knees) and has kept the Harbour Hall door since. Never married, no children.
- Polishes Old Bain's boat-hook (ash and iron, nicked shaft) every morning from a stool; taught Jowan the nick stories.
- Her tales (legend, not fact): Old Bain sailed round Veligor with a crew of cousins and a goat; rowed into a winter storm and hauled a capsized boat home by its stern ring (the deepest nick); the race began as a dare from a crew from another harbour (no coast implied).
- Quenna confides in her on the hall steps after bad council meetings; Mabyn has never repeated any of it. Brings Ottilie a sweet bun every morning.

## Nerissa (nerissa)

- Age 27. Learned to dive from her grandmother Demelza (invented, dead), who dived the reef 50 years; Demelza died 6 years ago in her bed.
- Parents (unnamed) moved inland to Selsior when she was small to work for a cloth merchant; far away, play no part; they write.
- Dives the reef beyond the harbour wall; below it the shelf drops into deep water. Sells shellfish at the Fish Market and pearls to passing traders. Tells Wenna when she goes down.
- Met Tristram 4 years ago: he was still hunting from the Patience (so he still hunted big fish 4 years ago), saw her go down and not come up, rowed to rescue her; she surfaced by his oar laughing.
- Last dive: some weeks ago, late this spring, at the reef's edge; something moved below the shelf in the dark; seen only a moment. Has not dived since.
- Real reason she won't tell Tristram: fears he would go down with his harpoon to look.
- Now sits on the step below Wenna's sorting others' catch.

## Ottilie (ottilie)

- Age 38. Grew up on Merchant Street. Learned bookkeeping from Elowen (Maren's mother) from age 14; Elowen recommended her to the council.
- Clerk of the Harbour Hall 14 years (since 24): 6 years under the previous (unnamed) Harbour Master, 8 under Quenna.
- Desk just inside the hall door under the window. Sweets jar for correct forms. Mabyn brings her a sweet bun every morning. Sometimes checks her figures against Hobart's chalk tallies.
- Small-boat tally: began a few years ago, copied from Harbour Watch notes; she back-filled it from old Watch notes for ten summers. Ten summers ago a fair week saw 40+ small boats past the headland; last summer 22; this spring fewer still. Has told no one.

## Peregrine (peregrine)

- Age 19. Grew up in Selsior; parents (unnamed) still live there (far away, play no part); writes monthly with drawings.
- Apprenticed at 14 to a Selsior cabinetmaker; first saw Dunstan's yard delivering a desk to a Bain merchant. Finished his indenture and came to Bain a little over a year ago at 18; Dunstan refused 3 times, accepted the 4th.
- Fell off the end of the slipway into the harbour in his first week. Sleeps in the loft above the yard's workshop.
- Faster hull: longer, narrower, finer entry, flatter run aft. Pear-wood half-model; with Kit, towed it against a model of the old shape in the rock pools and won every time but once (Kit stepped on it). The half-model has sat under a rag on Dunstan's workbench for 2 months; Dunstan has not lifted the rag.
- Has never tried to look at Dunstan's race plans.

## Tegan (tegan)

- Age 41 (same age as Alwyn). Grew up in the sail loft at the edge of the shipyard (long high room, floor wide enough for a mainsail).
- Mother Kensa (invented, dead), the previous sailmaker, taught her from age 8; died 15 years ago; Tegan (then 26) has kept the loft since. Uses Kensa's sail palm. Father not named.
- Never married, no children.
- Brisa's room "above a sailmaker's loft" is the little room above Tegan's loft; Brisa came at 16. Tegan keeps sewing Brisa's coat buttons back on.
- Buys bolt rope from Wilmot directly.
- Made the Bright Morrow's tall new sails when Seraphine rebuilt the rig 9 years ago; tells Seraphine she carries too much sail.
- Has patched and recut Dunstan's race boats' sails for years; this summer is the first time sails were asked for in secret. Sews them after dark with the loft door barred; cover story: a new mainsail for a coaster.
- Has done sums on Dunstan's scrap of paper and formed her own (unstated) idea of the hull's shape; told nobody.
- Since the deep water turned strange (recent years, not the start of the Disturbance 50 years ago): captains want heavier storm canvas, doubled seams, spare sails. Some sails have come back from deep water with long straight tears.

## Tully (tully)

- Age 26. Came to the docks at 12 (14 years ago) asking for work; Garrow had been foreman 2 years and took him on.
- Father Treve (invented, dead), a dock porter, hurt his back slipping on a wet gangplank with a barrel when Tully was 11; died that winter (15 years ago).
- Mother Lamorna (invented) lives with her sister in a fishing village up the coast (far away, plays no part); writes twice a year.
- Unmarried, no children.
- Carries the heavy cargo (tea chests, iron, leaky barrels). Hobart never takes Tully's count. Carries Finch on his shoulders; shares lunch with him on the warehouse step.
- Does not know why Garrow stopped unloading at the far end of the outer pier after dark; has stopped asking.
- Boat savings: 6 years, tin box with a stone on the lid at the bottom of his sea chest; has about two thirds of the price of a small plain boat from Dunstan's yard (Dunstan has no time to build one this summer). Wants to fish the bay mornings, carry small coastal cargoes afternoons.
- Has looked at the Patience under its sailcloth on the beach; does not know whose it is; never asked.
- Has never moved Hobart's four iron-bound crates.

## Wilmot (wilmot)

- Age 72. A fisherman's son; came to the rope walk at 22 (50 years ago) under old Penrose (invented, dead), who made him spin yarn a full year first. Took over the rope walk when Penrose died (date not set).
- Rope walk: a long, low shed along the back of the Waterfront. A hired child turns the wheel for a copper; fewer every year.
- Wife Hedra (invented, dead) spun yarn beside him; died 18 years ago. No children.
- Only real apprentice ever: Tamar (Morwenna's husband), came at 14; died of lung fever ~7 years ago. No apprentice since.
- Taught Kit his last few knots; knows Kit wants to be a shipwright.
- Merriwether buys most of his rope; lately asks for heavier cables and hawsers. Tegan buys bolt rope directly. His rope also goes to Kaldemor in the harvest carts.
- Learned his old songs from Penrose, who learned them from the woman who taught him. Forgot the last verse of one when Lorcan asked for the words.
- Has never seen Ossie's knotted rope.

## Town-wide timing (set in review)

- The Disturbance began 50 years ago and the sea changed slowly at first (Annis). The deep water "turned strange" (creatures, torn nets, lights, adventurers hired) only in recent years: Ottilie's small-boat decline spans 10 summers; Ysolde's lights began 3 summers ago; Tristram still hunted 4 years ago; Barnacle stopped a little over a year ago. Never write "since the Disturbance" for a change someone under about 50 remembers beginning; say "since the deep water turned strange".

## Kenwyn (kenwyn)

- Age 69. Born in a fisherman's cottage on the Waterfront; only son; parents (unnamed) long dead. Never married (not stated otherwise; no family).
- Seasick through his first year at sea; at 22 (47 years ago) went to old Hendra (invented, dead), a woman salvager who worked coast wrecks and the Saltgrace. She died 38 years ago and left him her lamps, bars and place at the top of the salvagers' steps. Rule: never go into a wreck without knowing the way out.
- Last salvager of the harbour; the only one for about 20 years.
- Has worked the Saltgrace for over 40 years, for old iron and seasoned timber. Never reached the lowest hold (always flooded) or opened the purser's cabin (deep in the holds, under water at all but the lowest tides; stood at its door twice).
- Has seen the stamp burned into some Saltgrace timbers for 40 years and never placed it; does not (yet) know it matches Hobart's crates.
- Named the Holdfast; once, years ago, saw a length of grey body thick as a mast slide past a broken hatch. Always left it alone.
- This spring, first low tide of the year: wreck full (barnacled crabs in the holds, eels in the bilges, things behind the portholes). Came out unhurt; has not gone back. Sits at the top of the steps near the Patience.
- Has sold his iron and timber through Hobart's warehouses for 40 years; Hobart reweighs every piece and they argue to the middle.

## Town look (set 2026-10-15, Commerci direction)

- Bain Harbour is a rich, sunlit, self-governing merchant town of the sea, run by the harbour council. Houses of pale stone and sand-coloured plaster under terracotta roofs; canals cut in from the harbour through every quarter, humped stone bridges, water doors on the warehouses.
- The Harbour Hall is the grandest building on the coast: colonnade, bell tower, broad steps down to a square with market awnings. Old Bain's boat-hook still hangs above its door.
- Merchant Street runs along the town's main canal under arcades: trading houses, counting rooms, the spice and cloth shops, the bakery, the halls of the trades. Narrow houses and shops on it (Ambrose's, Maren's) are still narrow.
- The Fish Market stands where the old cove was, by the fishermen's steps: the oldest and plainest corner. Fishing is the humble origin, trade the pride. Small, plain homes (Kenwyn's birthplace, Garrow's narrow house, the Salt and Bread's old net loft) still exist on the Waterfront.
- Still true: Bain Harbour has no schoolhouse (Maren teaches in her front room); the harbour has one outer pier and Garrow wants a second.
- The Old Cut: a canal dug in the shipwrights' century across the headland's neck from the north quays to the open sea; disused for a generation (too narrow for today's ships). Its sea gates broke in a storm "a few winters ago"; since then the tide and the deep-water creatures come in along it. The Pinch is now its narrowest, deepest lock (gates rotted away), not a rock cleft.
- The Holm Wood (holm oak and sea pine) covers the headland's back behind the lighthouse; the shipwrights felled their timber there. At its heart, the Lantern Deep is a collapsed sea cave, a sea-water pool joined under the cliffs to the Drop. The Clatterstones, the Long Reef, the shelf and the Drop are unchanged as places.
- Kit was found on a lock gate in the Inner Cut (not a rock) in the crab hunt; Tristram goes to the rim of the Lantern Deep (not the rocks above the Drop) to see the Deep Lantern.
