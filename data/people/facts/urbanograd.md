# Established facts: urbanograd

Written by NPC lore writers so later writers stay consistent. Not shown on the wiki.

## Dina Finch (orla_finch)

- Age 52. Born in Urbanograd; her father (dead) carried her to her first Walkout Day play.
- Joined the troupe at 19 as a cart-pusher/stagehand; played Juna for 9 years.
- Director for 13 years, since the death of the previous director, Bellamy Stroud (dead, directed for decades).
- Office: the old fortress quartermaster's store in the Old Barracks.
- Dex was cast as the grandee 15 years ago, before Dina became director.
- Rue won the Juna part at an audition this spring (froze in the wings, then owned the stage).
- Quint's Charter reading opens Walkout Day; Dina times the first entrance to it.

## Perch (perch)

- Gender unset: no pronouns used for Perch.
- Age 71. Born in a stacked house below the Crooked Tower.
- Pigeon loft is on the Crooked Tower, the tallest and leanest of Old Grad's fortress towers (the highest roof in the city).
- Learned from the previous loft keeper, Isolde Spurr (dead); took over the loft at 20, 51 years ago.
- Pigeons carry messages to other towns; rooftop runners carry messages within the city.
- The refusals began "a few years ago" with an old pigeon named Greyfeather. Some routes are refused only at dusk; a few at all times.
- Perch and Starling compare Perch's route list with Starling's flicker dates; neither knows what they mean.
- Lux often stops at the loft for tea on the way down from the towers. Parsnip trades cabbage leaves for pigeon droppings.
- No apprentice yet.

## Brindle (brindle)

- Gender unset: no pronouns used for Brindle.
- Age 39. Born in Urbanograd to a family of rope-makers (parents dead; no living family).
- Joined the Gate Watch at 22 (17 years ago); captain for 7 years, chosen by the volunteers without a vote.
- Previous captain Garran Holt (alive) retired 7 years ago to keep bees near Kaldemor; plays no part.
- Knox has served 3 years longer than Brindle has been captain. Brindle keeps a weekly sightings slate by the gate.
- Brindle has noticed Dagny's courage and has not told her yet.

## Hatch (hatch)

- Age 63. Not born in Urbanograd; son of a carter (dead) who hauled goods between towns. Drove his own wagon from 16.
- At 33 (30 years ago) lost a wagon and horses to a monster in a ravine; became a hunter. Settled in Urbanograd "twenty-odd years ago".
- Has begun keeping written notes on what he sees; does not claim to know why monsters changed.
- Shade trained with him for four seasons on the roads, years ago.
- Ferro mended Hatch's spear-head when Ferro still made only sculptures; Hatch then talked Ferro into forging knives, before other adventurers asked. Hatch claims he started Ferro's weapon trade.
- Ferro's gender not set by this entry (no pronouns used).

## Ozzie Lantry (hob_lantry)

- Age 74. Born in Urbanograd. Grandfather (dead) was a Walker, a fiddler whose songs Selsior's theatres refused.
- Taught reading to children in his front room for most of his life.
- Has worked on the history in a corner of the Great Hall for 18 years.
- The Table's record books begin only after the first winter; no first-winter accounts are known. Why is unknown (his guesses: too cold to write, burned for warmth, hidden).
- Friends with Quint since boyhood (stole apples on the same rooftops).
- Has asked Dog-Ear to see the Juna script for 10 years; Dog-Ear only quotes a line or two.
- Has written down Nana Gristle's mother's story many times.

## Ivo Sable (ivo_sable)

- Age 44. Grew up on a farm in the orchard country outside Kaldemor, youngest child of apple growers (not Kaldemor's orchard keeper's family; family lives far away and plays no part).
- Came to Urbanograd at 19 to be a singer; was not good at it.
- Trained under street healer Henna Bright (dead), who kept the awning on Lantern Square; she died 15 years ago and left him the awning, cabinet and kettle.
- Clinic: a patched green awning on the edge of Lantern Square.
- Patch and Ivo send patients to each other and share supplies. Ivo has asked Crow to label bottles "perhaps a hundred times". Crow's gender not set here.

## Jinx (jinx)

- Gender unset: no pronouns used for Jinx. Drum's gender also not set here.
- Age 18. Born in Selsior to a family of clerks; parents alive in Selsior, write once a season, play no part.
- Came to Urbanograd at 14 (4 years ago) on a carter's wagon. Slept in a stairwell the first winter.
- Tinder befriended Jinx that first winter (shared her supper). Tinder refuses to teach Jinx torch juggling for now.
- Once juggled three of Margo's spare parts; one was a spring that went off mid-air.
- Pays Figgy's one-copper fee when a ball lands on her drawings.
- Dina has refused the torch act "over her dead body and the cart's".

## Lux (lux_lune)

- Gender unset: no pronouns used for Lux. Tinsel and Prism also have no pronouns here.
- Age 36. Grew up in Urbanograd. Began carrying oil for keeper Lionel Callow at 20; Keeper since Lionel Callow died 8 years ago.
- Lux is the 12th Keeper of the Lanterns; the 11 earlier names are scratched into the oldest beam of the lamp store at the foot of the first tower.
- The Walkers hung the first lanterns in their first year.
- Smashed lanterns: only on the outward side of the walls, never two nights in a row; frames bent, glass scattered. Lux watched three nights, saw nothing, found one broken after the fourth. Cause unknown.
- Perch's loft is on Lux's nightly round. Lux has promised to help with Prism's Walkout Day light show.

## Mags (mags_ferrow)

- Age 48. Cook of the Great Hall for 22 years.
- Grandmother Bettany Ferrow (dead) cooked for the Table nearly 40 years, handed over 22 years ago when her knees failed, advised from the fireside 4 more years, then died (18 years ago).
- Married Bastian Krell 17 years ago (after a winter of him praising her stew in unrelated debates). Mags kept her surname.
- Bastian tries out new speeches on Mags while she stirs.
- The Great Hall kitchen chimney is as old as the fortress and draws badly.
- More of the pot's ingredients now come from rooftop gardens as fewer farm carts arrive.

## Margo 'Sparks' Venn (margo_venn)

- Age 38. Born in Urbanograd. Childhood spent in the shop of her aunt Odile Venn (dead), a clockmender; Odile died when Margo was 23. Margo's parents not mentioned (treat as dead or absent).
- Opened her own workshop on the Row soon after (15 years ago); has blown its door off twice.
- Early inventions: a backwards music box (age 12), a kettle that whistles per temperature (15).
- Rivet's lift jammed last winter; Margo climbed up to help. Rivet's gender not set here.
- Margo does not know Bolt admires some of Rivet's ideas.

## Pip Marrow (pip_marrow)

- Age 19. Line: Juna Marrow -> daughter Hazel Marrow (born in Urbanograd's early years) -> son Corin Marrow -> Pip. Juna had only one child, Hazel.
- Pip's parents Corin and his wife (unnamed) both died of a winter fever within a week, 16 years ago, when Pip was 3.
- Raised by grandmother Hazel, a seamstress, in a crooked house near the Old Barracks. Hazel died 4 years ago aged 88.
- Has written three plays, burned two; the third is finished, in a drawer. New secret comedy in a notebook she always carries; title secret.
- Dina has mentored her since early childhood and closer since Hazel died.
- Nix asked Pip to sit as model for the new Juna mask because the old pictures of Juna disagree. Nix's pronouns not set here.

## Quint (quint_holloway)

- Age 77. Keeper of the Charter Scroll for 26 years. Formerly a bookbinder with a shop off the Night Market.
- Predecessor keeper Mercy Oakes chose him; she died the following winter (25 years ago).
- The hall's copy of the Open Charter is kept in a cedar case in a niche behind the Caller's chair; only Quint touches it.
- Reads the whole Charter aloud in the square every Walkout Day morning; has opened Dina's plays for 13 years; reads slowly on purpose.
- Friends with Ozzie since boyhood; suppers most weeks. Remembers Rook as a child under the Table's benches.
- No family named.

## Rivet (rivet)

- Gender unset: no pronouns used for Rivet. Vane also gets no pronouns here.
- Age 22. Born in Urbanograd, in a stacked house four floors above Workshops Row.
- Mother Hedda (dead), a porter who hauled crates up the rooftop stairs for 20 years until her back gave out; died when Rivet was 16 (6 years ago). Father not mentioned.
- Built the lift 3 years ago, at 19: ropes, a counterweight of old fortress stones, a hand-winch and a platform, bolted to the old granary at the end of Workshops Row. Porters now work the winch.
- Last winter's jam: a crate of lantern glass stuck halfway up. Rivet replaced the whole counterweight line afterwards. Good rope from outside is scarcer.
- Rehung Margo's workshop door the last time she blew it off.
- Wants the second lift near the Old Barracks (over a public stair, so it needs the Table). Has spoken at the Table twice, talked over both times by Bastian's bridge campaign.
- Scrap rides the lift platform when Rivet isn't looking. Edgar has filled a notebook with drawings of the lift; Rivet lets him hold the tools.
- Does not know Bolt admires some of Rivet's ideas.

## Rue (rue)

- Age 20. Grew up on a barley-and-geese farm in the farmlands around Kaldemor, third of five children. Parents and siblings alive there, write when carts run, play no part; her mother sends a cake every Walkout Day.
- Came to Urbanograd 3 years ago at 17 on a cider cart.
- Worked 2 years for the troupe as cart-pusher, Thimble's pin-fetcher and sweeper; never said a line, learned them all.
- This spring's audition: froze in the wings (Hambone fetched water), then owned the stage; Dina gave her the part the same day.
- Dex coaches her on timing. She will wear Nix's new Juna mask, modelled on Pip.
- Met Crumb at the Night Market in her first week (recognised his rolls from harvest fairs); he saves her burnt buns.
- Rehearses to Pounce on the Rooftops.

## Scrap (scrap)

- Age 9. Vane's daughter (Vane: no pronouns). Scrap's other parent is not mentioned.
- Never uses stairs; rides Rivet's lift when Rivet isn't looking.
- Collection of named lost things in a biscuit tin under a loose slate on Vane's roof: a brass button "Sir Wobble", a glove "Lefty", and a cracked lantern pane from an outer tower found in a gutter, named "Bad News".
- Fed by Ladle at the Night Market (not for want of supper; always hungry); repays with Rooftop gossip.
- Practises sneaking on everyone; has never caught Shade unawares. Shade has quietly taught her how to fall safely.

## Shade (shade)

- Gender unset: no pronouns used for Shade.
- Age 35. Grew up on Urbanograd's Rooftops; parents were two street musicians (unnamed, dead) who died within a year of each other when Shade was 12; rooftop folk fed Shade afterwards.
- Trained with Hatch at 20 (15 years ago), four seasons, spring to winter, on the roads; Hatch was already settled at the Gate Walk.
- Has never lost a traveler. Guides the last stretch into the city; many old routes were first walked by Hatch. Unsafe now: a ford (something waits at dusk), a wooded cutting gone silent, a hill path with oversized tracks.
- Carries throwing knives from Ferro; knows their names, has never told anyone.
- Taught Scrap how to fall from a low wall safely.

## Edgar Penhallow (alder_penhallow)

- Name is Edgar (id keeps an older name). Age 19. Born in Selsior near the Crown Market; second son of a Cordwainers' Guild family (shoe- and bootmakers). Parents and elder brother alive in Selsior, unnamed, play no part.
- Apprenticed at the guild hall at 12. Sent to Urbanograd this spring for a season to report on its bootmakers; due back at the guild hall when summer ends.
- Notebooks: 1 measurements; 2 drawings of Rivet's lift; 3 Dyna's colours, Jinx's juggling, Table notes "Who is in charge?"; 4 boots. Started a fifth last week.
- Asked Cobble three times to take him on; first refusal "wrong sort of hands"; sweeps Cobble's doorstep every morning.
- Rivet is his first friend in the city; lets him hold the tools.
- Letters home are only about boots; has not told his family he may not come back.

## Bastian Krell (bastian_krell)

- Age 50. Born in Urbanograd; father (unnamed, dead) was a Night Market crier.
- Nickname "Bellows" for his voice; trade came later: makes and mends bellows for forges, kilns and glass furnaces, shop on Workshops Row. Ferro and Tinsel use his bellows.
- First spoke at the Table at 30 (20 years ago), objecting to a proposed tax on lamp oil; has not missed a gathering since.
- No children. Married Mags 17 years ago (as set).
- Has privately promised to give up his own time at the next gathering so Rivet can speak about the second lift; has not told Rivet.
- Lately ends speeches with Brindle's call for volunteers.

## Cobble (cobble)

- Age 76. Born in Urbanograd on the Row. Shop on Workshops Row has no sign.
- Apprenticed at 14 to bootmaker Henrike Tull (dead), a Walker's daughter who never joined a guild; she died 52 years ago (Cobble 24) and left him the shop and lasts.
- Invented the soft-soled rooftop boot himself in his first years on his own.
- Made first pairs for Kite's grandmother (running when Cobble was young), Kite's mother, and Kite "a few years ago". Whether Kite's mother and grandmother are alive is NOT stated. Keeps Kite's run records on the back of his shop door.
- Also makes shoes for Old Barracks actors.
- Edgar asked three times: refused once aloud, twice with a grunt. Cobble now leaves a scrap of hide and an awl by the door to see what Edgar does.
- Good hides arrive less often; hoards the best for the runners. No family named.

## Dagny Roe (dagny_roe)

- Age 21. Grew up on Bain Harbour's Waterfront; family renders fish oil for lamps (3 generations). Parents and two older brothers alive there, unnamed, write twice a year, play no part. (Not a lighthouse family: Ysolde keeps that light.)
- Came to Urbanograd at 18 (3 years ago); hauled oil for Night Market lamp-sellers; joined the Gate Watch a year later (2 years ago) when Brindle asked the Table for volunteers; given the night watch.
- First month on the gate (late autumn): her lamp went out; the thing on the road was a lost goat. Two lamps ever since.
- Reports eye sightings to Brindle for the slate. Steps forward and talks at the dark.
- Knox gives her carved wooden owls and calls her the bravest of them (she thinks it a joke).
- Brings wounded travelers to Patch; Patch makes her a bitter hot drink; Dagny walks Patch home after late patients.

## Dex (dex)

- Age 41. Cast as the grandee 15 years ago, at 26, by Bellamy Stroud, after 3 seasons as the back end of a mule. Bellamy died 2 years later; Dina took over (13 years ago).
- The grandee: a puffed-up Selsior worthy who bans everything fun, falls off things and is chased off the cart by the market crowd. Hat: a tower of purple felt and feathers, kept locked by Thimble; Dex may never take it home.
- Coached Rue this spring on laughs and staging.
- Sprout is 8. Sprout's mother Lark (dead) sang in the troupe's chorus; died of a fever when Sprout was barely 1 (about 7 years ago). Dex raised him backstage. On her anniversary they watch the lanterns come on from the Rooftops and sing one of her songs.
- Recites tragic speeches to the empty theatre in the early mornings.

## Dimity Ash (dimity_ash)

- Age 40. Scribe 12 years (since 28). Born in Urbanograd; parents a sign-painter and a printer's devil (unnamed, both dead).
- Nickname "Inkpot": once spilled an inkpot on a visiting Selsior guild master and minuted it word for word.
- Desk: small sloping desk beside the Caller's chair.
- At 20 began copying speeches for scribe Mortimer Reed (dead), whose eyes were failing; he died 12 years ago and the Table gave her the desk without argument.
- Oldest surviving books (after the first winter) are on scavenged paper and old ledgers turned upside down; never copied. She has begun copying the oldest volume a page or two after each session.
- She and Ozzie agreed each tells the other first if anything turns up in an attic.
- Keeps a private tally of Bastian's mid-speech changes of mind.

## Drum (drum)

- Gender unset: no pronouns used for Drum.
- Age 31. Grew up in a cooper's yard above Goa Harbour's Inlet Quay; parents (unnamed) alive there, coopers, occasionally send a bucket inland with no note; play no part.
- Came to Urbanograd at 17 (14 years ago) with mallets and three nested buckets; started playing the first night.
- Kit: buckets, pans, a washtub and one cracked kettle; sits on an upturned crate at the edge of Lantern Square.
- Has a rhythm for each of the nine street tunes Saffi has finished.
- Works on the monster-rhythm late at night in a corner of the Old Barracks yard; has noticed (proves nothing) the outer lanterns seem to survive on nights the beat is loudest; tallies patterns that silence the stray dogs at the gate.

## Ferro (ferro)

- Gender unset: no pronouns used for Ferro.
- Age 46. Born in Urbanograd; parent (unnamed, dead) was a tinker who died when Ferro was 20 (26 years ago), leaving a handcart of scrap.
- Forge near the far end of Workshops Row: hinge heron at the door, copper fox on the roof, an unnamed spiral of chain and springs in the yard. Made the climbable fiddler sculpture on a corner of Lantern Square. Bastian made and mends Ferro's bellows.
- Hatch brought the bent spear-head about 16 years ago (Ferro 30); a month later talked Ferro into a pair of hunting knives.
- Blade names are scratched small on the tang (e.g. Small Errand, Last Lantern, Mind the Step). Ferro has never told Shade the names; Shade found them and once asked for a chipped knife to be mended rather than replaced.
- Last Walkout Day's sculpture: a small iron cat for Perch's loft; Pounce sleeps beside it.

## Figgy (figgy)

- Age 8. Ochre's daughter; Figgy's other parent is not mentioned.
- Fee: free to look, one copper to step on a drawing; never lets anyone off. Saves the coppers in a jar under her bed for a wall of her own.
- "Rain rule": watches the colours wash down the gutters, sometimes from under the edge of Ivo's awning.
- Says loudly that Splash's chimney paintings are the best in the city (everyone knows they are Splash's; nobody can prove it).
- Nana Gristle judges her drawings and keeps a honey cake in her pocket for good days.
- Ochre has told her the old walls around the square belong to the Walkers' pictures.

## Hambone (hambone)

- Age 72. Born in Urbanograd. Joined the troupe at 20 (52 years ago) as a cart-pusher, under Bellamy Stroud.
- Nickname from his second season: played the back half of a prize pig in a Bellamy farce; the crowd threw sausages. Real name not given.
- Previous cart keeper: Fenwick Corliss (dead), a wheelwright; died 40 years ago; Hambone has kept the cart since (age 32).
- Showed Dina how to push the cart when she joined (33 years ago).
- "Juna's planks": three dark worn planks in the middle of the stage. Story came via Fenwick; unproven; Ozzie has found no letter either way.
- Shares a backstage bench with Thimble (30 years). Fetched Rue's water at the audition. Sprout rides the tailboard.
- Cracked wheel: left rear, the oldest; found in early spring; bound with wire. Dina, Thimble and Sprout know. Needs seasoned wood from outside.

## Kite (kite)

- Gender unset: no pronouns used for Kite. Splash also gets no pronouns here.
- Age 19. Cobble made Kite's first boots 4 years ago, at 15.
- Grandmother (unnamed, dead) ran messages over the roofs when Cobble was young; died when Kite was small; taught Kite the old routes (the long jump over the Night Market, the gutter run below the Crooked Tower, the "singing" slates).
- Mother (unnamed, alive) ran until a fall on a wet roof ruined her knee; moved to the farmlands near Kaldemor; writes when carts get through; plays no part.
- Took the Gate Walk to Great Hall record last summer, bettered it twice since. Timed by a sand-glass at each end.
- Tick (runs messages by the streets) means to beat Kite one day.
- Carries Splash's paint pots at night and keeps watch; only Kite knows for certain which pictures are Splash's.
- Wants to run from the top of the Crooked Tower to the gate without touching a stair.

## Knox (knox)

- Age 44. Born in Urbanograd; formerly a joiner's labourer on Workshops Row (beams, rooftop bridges).
- Wife Jessamy (dead), a stall-keeper's daughter; died 8 years ago after a fall on an icy stair in winter.
- Joined the Gate Watch 10 years ago, when Tick was a baby, under captain Garran Holt; Brindle had already served 7 years then.
- Tick is 10. Raised at the Gate Walk.
- Carves foxes, hares, goats and owls; owls go to Dagny. Brindle still looking for words to tell her; Knox lets the owls say it.
- Patch pulls his carving splinters.

## Moth (moth_kessel)

- Gender unset: no pronouns used for Moth (registry calls Moth Glim's father; used only as a noun). Glim's other parent not mentioned.
- Age 49. Wakes at noon. Keeper of the stalls for 16 years.
- Predecessor: old Bramble (dead), a rope-seller who kept the hat most of a lifetime.
- The draw: begun by the first traders in the Walkers' early years after quarrels over corners; first name drawn, first pick. Moth's felt hat is the third hat anyone remembers.
- Never fixes the draw for Glim; Glim never asks.
- Shares the last cup of hot cider with Ladle most nights; pretends not to notice her feeding rooftop children.
- Missing outside traders: salt sellers, wool carters, spice peddlers. Some corners empty for nights. Moth now counts the names before each draw; has privately considered promising the best corners to outside traders (not done).

## Nana Gristle (nana_gristle)

- Age 79. Never had children; the square began calling her Nana about 40 years ago. "Gristle" because she is tough as gristle.
- Outlived a husband and two sweethearts (all unnamed, dead).
- Mother Posy (dead): a baby of a few months on the Long Walk, carried in a laundry basket; died 18 years ago, aged about 82. Nana was born when Posy was about 21.
- Grandmother Marguerite (dead): a Walker, lace-maker never let into a Selsior guild; packed her lace bobbins around the baby.
- Bench: sunny side of Lantern Square, mid-morning until the lanterns come on, every day.
- Knows which roofs fell in during her childhood and who patched them; Kite finds her warnings irritatingly right.
- Scolds Rook for skipping meals, speaking too softly and letting Bastian go on.
- Every evening crosses to Saucer's tea stall for spiced tea and a honey cake; Saucer reads her leaves.
- Ozzie suspects some details she adds are new.

## Ochre (ochre)

- Gender unset: no pronouns used for Ochre (registry: Figgy's mother; used only as a noun).
- Age 38. Born in Urbanograd. Ground colours for old Rosalba (dead), the square's muralist, from 12; brush at 16; Rosalba died 15 years ago and the walls passed to Ochre.
- Rosalba learned from someone taught by the Walkers. Rule: the oldest pictures are kept, never improved.
- Surviving Walker pictures around the square: a cart full of laughing people, a line of walkers with bundles, a lantern between two towers, a rude cartoon of a guild master. Repainted every spring, line for line.
- Has never spoken properly with Splash; pointed remarks pass through Figgy. Climbs up to look at each new Splash mural.
- Gate Walk history: a hundred sheets of drawings ready. Good red is made from a red earth dug outside the walls and carted in.
- Knows Splash also wants the Gate Walk; pretending not to. Secretly looking for a wall for Figgy.

## Old Parsnip (parsnip)

- Age 81. Garden spans three rooftops near the Crooked Tower (tubs, troughs, split barrels, an old bathtub); a rooftop shed holds his seed chest of drawers.
- Began as a boy carrying soil up the stairs for the old rooftop growers; tubs of his own by 20. All his teachers are dead (unnamed).
- Rooftop gardening dates from the spring after the Walkers' first winter (no details of that winter known).
- Pounce sleeps in his warmest trough on sunny afternoons; he planted it with catmint.
- Hears Scrap sneaking and never lets on. Jinx juggled one of his cabbages; he jokingly asks for it back.
- Saves seed every autumn in labelled paper twists. People now come weekly for seed. Some old varieties came from Kaldemor's farms long ago; he has no fresh seed of them.

## Patch (patch)

- Age 68. Born in Urbanograd near the Gate Walk. Mother (unnamed, dead) sold herbs; taught her in the meadows beyond the gate.
- Herb house: narrow, a few steps inside the fortress gate on the Gate Walk; kept for more than forty years. Treating travelers by age 20.
- Gives frightened or young patients a boiled sweet. Dagny's drink: mostly willow bark plus something Patch will not name.
- Brindle has asked her to stop going out to the fields alone.
- Treats Knox's carving splinters and Tick's skinned knees.
- Some herbs she needs most (for cleaning bites, stopping fevers) grow only where nobody walks now.

## Piper (piper)

- Gender unset: no pronouns used for Piper.
- Age 20. Grew up behind the Old Barracks; hung about the theatre from 12. Plays a tin whistle.
- Has written the play's songs for 4 years (since 16, when Dina kept an unasked-for tune for the grandee's entrance). The play's words have barely changed in 100 years; its songs have always drifted.
- Dina cuts half the songs and hums the survivors; Piper has heard her and never said so.
- Friendly war with Hambone, who hums the old versions; Sprout sings both.
- Ozzie once sang Piper the Walker songs his grandfather taught him; Piper keeps them written out, tied with a ribbon.
- Has written a whistle second part for each of Saffi's nine street tunes; has never asked to play with her.

## Pounce (pounce)

- A cat; he/him. Big grey, torn left ear, feather-duster tail. Age unknown; arrived in Perch's loft one wet autumn "years ago", origin unknown.
- Daily round: loft in the morning; Parsnip's catmint trough around midday; loft beside Ferro's iron cat in the afternoon; out over the roofs at dusk.
- Seen above the Night Market, on the Great Hall's ridge, and once riding Rivet's lift.
- Tick salutes him. Weather sign: comes in early before storms; sleeps on open slates before dry spells. Parsnip waters by him, Kite plans runs by him; Figgy asks Kite whether he has come in. Nobody keeps a list of his forecasts.
- Stays in on evenings when the wind blows from the middle of the island.

## Saffi (saffi_quell)

- Age 22. Born in Selsior to a family of respectable court-dance musicians (parents and grandparents alive there, unnamed, write once a year, play no part). Fiddle from age 4.
- Sped up the dances until dancers at a guild dinner "gave up and ran"; left for Urbanograd at 17 (5 years ago).
- Plays one of her family's slowest dances very fast whenever a letter arrives.
- Plays at the edge of Lantern Square, often sitting on the iron knee of Ferro's fiddler sculpture. Ties her hair with a red cloth strip.
- Ivo calls her the city's best fiddler.
- Nine finished street tunes include Workshops Row, the Gate Walk and the Night Market. List of the rest pinned inside her fiddle case.
- Does not know Piper has written second parts; has noticed a thin person with a tin whistle who never plays it.

## Splash (splash)

- Gender unset: no pronouns used for Splash.
- Age 20. Born in Urbanograd, in a stacked house whose front door opens onto a roof.
- Parents (unnamed, alive), sign-painters, moved to Selsior when Splash was 16 (4 years ago); write now and then; play no part.
- Self-taught; no master. Paints with cheap sign-shop dregs, brick dust and soot. Subjects include a heron in a rain barrel, a giant asleep with a town on its chest, the moon dropping a coin into a chimney.
- Knows (via Kite) that Ochre looks at every new mural; has never said so; is pleased by it.
- Has decided what the Gate Walk picture will be; has told no one, not even Kite. Knows of Ochre's Gate Walk plan and pretends not to.

## Starling (starling)

- Gender unset: no pronouns used for Starling.
- Age 34. Born in Urbanograd. Parents (unnamed, dead) were bookbinders; left a narrow flat-roofed house near the Crooked Tower and an old star chart from the city's early years with Walker constellation names (the Cart, the Fiddle, the Open Door).
- Sleeps afternoons in a hammock between two chimney stacks.
- Telescope: long brass tube, three eyepieces, finder made from a bottle bottom, built from Tinsel's flawed spare lenses.
- First noticed a flicker about 2 years ago: the horizon toward the middle of the island wavers for a few breaths like air over a stove. Irregular; not tied to moon or season as far as Starling can tell; never seen from below the Rooftops.
- First brought the dates to Perch's loft last autumn. Some dates seem to match Perch's refusals, some do not.
- Also wants to finish a full star chart of the sky over Urbanograd.

## Thimble (thimble)

- Age 66. Born in Urbanograd. Worked for a dressmaker off the Night Market before the troupe.
- Joined the troupe 30 years ago, aged 36, under Bellamy Stroud's direction: complained backstage about costumes; costume maker Wilhelmina Gage (dead) handed her a needle. Wilhelmina died a few winters later.
- Juna's costume is a green jacket; Thimble keeps a scrap of every Juna jacket she made sewn into her own coat's lining. Has never found the oldest Juna jacket.
- Hat key on the ribbon with her spectacles; Dex has asked every year for 15 years to take it home.
- Secretly keeps trimmed hat feathers in a packet in case a smaller hat is needed (for Sprout).
- Knows about the cracked wheel; has told no one. Now claims she always knew Rue could act (she did not).
- Dyna's prices have risen for real as outside cloth grows scarce.

## Tick (tick)

- Age 10 (as set). Mother Jessamy died when Tick was 2; he remembers only a laugh.
- Began running messages at 7. Means to beat Kite's Gate Walk to Great Hall record by the streets; writes down the names of everyone who says it is impossible.
- Notebook of travelers' strange stories is on its third volume; records teller's name and date. Entries include a talking crow on the Kaldemor road, a ford whose water ran backwards for an evening, a stag with antlers like a burnt tree, lights moving in an empty wood. Zip's broken arm appears in three versions.
- Brindle lets him read the sightings slate aloud; Dagny lets him hold one of her lamps until bedtime.

## Tinder (tinder)

- Age 33. Born into a traveling show, Brightwater's (painted wagons, acrobats, a dancing goat). Parents (unnamed, dead) were tumblers in the show.
- Taught from age 11 by the show's fire-eater Cassius (dead; died 12 years ago, the winter before the show reached Urbanograd). The bucket and the Burning superstition are his ("bad luck to play with fire while the fire is listening").
- Came to Urbanograd 11 years ago, at 22; stayed when the show left; the show never returned.
- Pitch: middle of Lantern Square away from the awnings; moves mid-show if a storyteller starts on the Burning.
- Gives Ivo the first coins of every good night.
- Privately will teach Jinx the torch trick the day Jinx checks the bucket unprompted; has not told Jinx.

## Tinsel (tinsel)

- Gender unset: no pronouns used for Tinsel.
- Age 42. Furnace on Workshops Row. Learned as a child from glassblower Ignatius Fenn (dead), who kept the same furnace; he died 17 years ago when Tinsel was 25.
- Makes nearly all the coloured lantern panes (amber, rose, sea-green, cornflower blue). Lux says amber carries furthest in fog; Tinsel says it depends on the fog.
- The crate of lantern glass stuck in Rivet's lift last winter was Tinsel's.
- Glowing-glass attempts so far: powdered shells, rare sands, river stones, apothecary powders, once Margo's spark-powder. Result: paperweights on the windowsill; none glows.
- Good sand comes from outside; Tinsel now reuses broken glass, reluctantly.

## Vane (vane)

- Gender unset: no pronouns used for Vane (registry calls Vane Scrap's mother; used only as a noun). Scrap's other parent still not mentioned.
- Age 37. Born in Urbanograd. Learned from bridge-builder Corbett (dead); Vane took over at 24 (13 years ago).
- More than 200 rooftop bridges. Named bridges: the Long Lean, Grandmother's Step, the Fiddler's Crossing (above Lantern Square), the Wobbler (does not wobble), the Kiss (short), the Gutter Queen, Old Copper.
- Has told Bastian the covered bridge over Lantern Square would need more timber than reaches the city in a year; quietly measures the gap anyway.
- Finds Splash's paintings under bridges; knows whose they are; says nothing.

## Zinnia 'Zip' Calloway (zinnia_calloway)

- Female. Age 18. Grew up in her grandmother's laundry at the bottom of the Gate Walk. Parents (unnamed) died when she was too small to remember.
- Grandmother Ottoline Calloway (dead), a washerwoman who had walked to every town as a girl; died 2 winters ago. Zip ran the laundry one season, gave the tubs to a neighbour, lives in the room above.
- Last spring: joined a caravan to Kaldemor; 2 days out chased a large tusked beast, fell off a stream bank, broke her arm in two places. Torvi Brask was the caravan guard and carried her back. Torvi has taught her since; they train most mornings on open ground inside the gate.
- Torvi's gender not set here (no pronouns used).
- Bolt tested the spark lamp in the workshop; Margo made Zip promise to bring the lamp back.

## Bolt (bolt)

- Age 16. Grew up in Bain Harbour, youngest son of a net-mender; parents and brothers (unnamed) alive there, write when carts get through, play no part.
- Came to Urbanograd at 14 (2 years ago); asked Margo if she needed a sweeper; apprentice since.
- Has tested the spark lamp on a broom, a barrel and once, by accident, himself.
- Keeps a hidden notebook of drawings of Rivet's lift counterweight under his sleeping mat. Neither Margo nor Rivet knows he admires Rivet's ideas.
- Has a half-drawn idea combining Margo's springs with Rivet's counterweights.

## Hex (hex_dunmore)

- Gender unset: no pronouns used for Hex.
- Age 52. Shop on Workshops Row with a door that has no visible handle.
- Swept for locksmith Esmond (dead) from age 12; Esmond died when Hex was 26 (26 years ago) and Hex kept the shop.
- The returned box: dark walnut cube, brass key-shaped inlay on each side, no keyhole; made about 28 years ago; sold at the Night Market to a traveler Hex cannot remember; came back this spring via a carter with a note. Something rattles inside. Hex now writes sequences in a book locked in another box.
- Velvet sells some of Hex's smaller boxes. Hex has not been told what Velvet thinks the warm stone is.

## Prism (prism)

- Gender unset: no pronouns used for Prism.
- Age 19. Grew up in Fairy Forest; family (unnamed) are basket-weavers, alive there, play no part. Small gift (only small coloured lights lasting a few breaths); a few years of lessons at the Grove School.
- Came to Urbanograd two springs ago with a caravan of glass-carters.
- Paints only inside the walls, never toward the road (agreed with the Gate Watch). Pictures: the Walkers' cart, the line with bundles, the first lanterns, herons.
- Walkout Day plan: after the play, the Long Walk in lights above Lantern Square; Lux will dim the lanterns. Best so far: forty lights, briefly.

## Sprout (sprout)

- Age 8 (as set). Mother Lark (as set). Thimble sews his urchin costumes and lets them out each spring.
- Knows about the cracked wheel and has kept it secret.
- Once did the grandee's fall off the Great Hall's table during a debate.
- Told Rue after her audition that he freezes too, "only when about to do something brilliant".
- Has touched the grandee's hat once, briefly.

## Torvi Brask (torvi_brask)

- Gender unset: no pronouns used for Torvi (as set by Zip's entry).
- Age 31. Born on the Brask homestead (stone shelter, goat pens) on the far slopes of the highlands. Whether the Brasks belong to any of Midbarium's four families: deliberately unset.
- Parents (unnamed) alive on the homestead, send word with wool carters, play no part. No siblings named.
- Grandmother Hulda Brask (dead) taught Torvi the axe; died when Torvi was 22 (9 years ago). Torvi carries her axe (handle replaced twice). Her lesson: a fighter who never runs gets only one bad day. Tavern story: she drove off a hill cat while holding a milk pail.
- Came to Urbanograd 6 years ago, at 25, meaning to stay a week; first night danced in Lantern Square to Drum's buckets and stayed. Likes Saffi's fiddle tunes.
- Attended the Open Table once (date unset) and laughed aloud when the evening ended in agreeing to talk more next week.
- Caravan guard on the roads to Kaldemor, Selsior and the harbours. Shade often brings Torvi's caravans in over the last stretch. Swaps stories with Hatch (Torvi after the first cup). Brindle buys Torvi drinks. Has carried bitten travelers to Patch.
- Zip's caravan (last spring): Torvi crossed the stream too late to stop her fall, laughed and spoke the "bravery wasted" line at the bank, carried her to the wagons, bound the arm. Torvi saw only tracks at the top of the bank, larger than any boar's; the beast was gone. What it was: open.
