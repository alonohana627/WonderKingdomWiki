# Established facts: cavelor

Written by NPC lore writers so later writers stay consistent. Not shown on the wiki.

## Shared Cavelor facts (set by the writer of the Doran-Varn batch)

- Wardens take their first turn on the Deep Watch at sixteen (as Marrun did).
- The damp fever: a sickness in Cavelor sixteen years ago. It killed Maelis (Gorran's wife) and Ilse and Tamm (Kell's parents; Ilse was Oswin's daughter).
- Monsters first reached Cavelor's outer tunnels eleven years ago.

## Doran (doran_sunward)

- Age 19. Younger child of Gorran; Thera is 3 years older (22).
- Mother Maelis died of the damp fever 16 years ago, when Doran was 3.
- First Deep Watch turn at 16. Best friend Kell since childhood.
- Went up with one of Marrun's messengers (unnamed) last summer; gone nearly two months; home last autumn. Saw rain, the grassland, and Selsior, where the messenger delivered Marrun's letters to the crown. Met no named surface person.
- Nickname "Sunward" given within a week of his return.
- Has told only Kell that he wants to go back to the surface.
- Doran thinks Varn is jealous he wasn't sent up; Varn has never said.

## Brask (warden_brask)

- Age 44. Captain of the Deep Watch 12 years (since 32). Predecessor Captain Dunmar, died 12 years ago; Marrun chose Brask after asking the Wardens.
- Parents were Wardens, both dead. First turn at 16.
- Married Halvara 14 years ago; son Ottel is 8.
- Keeps the roster in a book in the guardroom at the head of the Deep Watch.
- Doubled the Wardens on the lowest passages this spring. Writes what he hears in private shorthand in the roster margins, then crosses it out. What he hears is never stated.
- Oldest friend: Gorran.

## Ottel (ottel)

- Age 8. Only child of Brask and Halvara. Never been to the surface.
- Rhenna is one year older (9).
- Told Doran the sun was impossible in front of Annick and three Wardens. If the sun is real he'll give it his best stone (shown to no one).

## Thera (warden_thera)

- Age 22. Elder child of Gorran; was 6 when her mother Maelis died.
- First turn at 16, the day after her birthday; 6 years on the watch, never missed a turn.
- Only Wardens with ten years' service walk the very lowest stretch beside the prison; Thera is not yet allowed.
- Began her private notebook this spring; has shown no one.
- Oswin says hers are the only reports he never has to ask questions about.

## Annick (annick_songkeeper)

- Age 31. Chosen at 9 by Singer Aldra (dead 11 years ago), her teacher.
- At 16 (15 years ago) sang the Oath songs in the Oath Hall when Aveline and Marrun swore their alliance.
- The old songs are mostly unwritten; they come from all four peoples' guardians.
- Knows about Corvan's hidden honey jar (found it three times); not supposed to. Not engaged or married.
- Takes Deep Watch turns; sings under her breath on the lowest passages.
- Started the sun song after Doran's return last autumn; suspects she must see the sun herself.
- No Oath song about the sun exists: for centuries no one in Cavelor saw it, and none of the few who have climbed the Stair since the expedition (messengers, Doran, Marrun) was a singer.

## Gorran (gorran_anvil)

- Age 66. Inherited the forge from his father; a long line of smiths at that anvil.
- Married Maelis (a gardener; dead, damp fever 16 years ago) when past forty.
- Still walks one Deep Watch turn a week on the upper passages; Brask has offered to excuse him.
- Has been asked for more weapons every year since monsters reached the outer tunnels 11 years ago. Mends Varn's claw-scored armour.
- Would tell Doran to go back up if asked; Doran hasn't asked.

## Halvara (halvara_rootkeeper)

- Age 41. Head of the Root Gardens 10 years. Gardening about 30 years.
- Learned partly from Maelis (Gorran's late wife), who taught her to sing to seedlings.
- Root Gardens: caverns between the great roots, lit by the Tree's glow, watered by deep springs; mushrooms (food, drying, lamp-wicks) and root-fruit vines on the roots.
- Deepest beds (nearest the lower passages) strange for 3 years: too fast, too large, an unknown cap colour, warm soil. Takes Oswin a monthly note.
- Sends surplus to Corvan; lets him win about half their haggles.
- Hasn't asked Brask what he hears below.

## Kell (kell_inkhand)

- Age 19. Parents Ilse (Oswin's only daughter) and Tamm, both died in the damp fever 16 years ago, when Kell was 3. Raised by Oswin alone.
- Kell's grandmother (Oswin's wife, unnamed) died years before Kell was born.
- Copies the old ledgers into fresh books stored in a different hall from the originals.
- Keeps a private book of the four peoples' Balrog stories from the ledger margins.
- Asked Oswin ("each might hold a piece of it") and Marrun (said he did not know) which story is true.
- Oswin keeps Kell's childhood drawings in a drawer labelled "Miscellaneous Tremors".
- Keeps Doran's secret; wishes he could see surface libraries.

## Oswin (oswin_ledgerward)

- Age 83 (two years older than Marrun); friends since childhood in the Oath Hall.
- Taken into the Archive at 18 by Master of the Ledgers Edrith (dead). Master of the Ledgers 38 years (since Edrith died).
- 50 years ago, aged 33 and Edrith's under-archivist, Oswin wrote the ledger entry recording the change in the seal with his own hand.
- Still takes Warden turns; Brask gives him the shortest passages.
- Wife (unnamed) died 24 years ago. Only daughter Ilse.
- Marrun calls him by a childhood nickname (not stated).
- Suspects Thera is holding something back; enters Halvara's monthly notes in the ledgers.

## Rhenna (rhenna)

- Age 9. Ysra's daughter; grew up in the Oath Hall. Her father is not mentioned (left open).
- Has counted that more Wardens come to Ysra than used to; told no one.
- Learned the Oath from Annick; sings very quietly; practises alone on the Archive stairs.
- Afraid of the sky since Doran's stories last autumn; hasn't told her mother.

## Grisel (grisel_oldwarden)

- Age 84 (3 years older than Marrun). First turn at 16 (68 years ago); last turn at 76 (8 years ago) after 60 years.
- Lives in a small room by the Upper Stair; her chair always ends up facing the deep passages.
- Marrun's partner for nearly 30 years; it ended 33 years ago when she broke her leg in a fall on the lower stair and the roster paired them elsewhere. Marrun still visits; she knows his footsteps.
- Never married, no children.
- Night of the expedition (50 years ago): she had swapped turns with a sick Warden; heard strangers' footsteps in the upper passages, raised her lantern, saw the explorers, and sent for Marrun.
- Privately has never forgiven herself for letting the explorers in with joy; knows it was not her fault.
- Has noticed Kell counting where she stops.

## Varn (varn_tunnelscout)

- Age 37. Mapping the outer tunnels for 15 years (began 4 years before monsters reached them 11 years ago).
- His maps hang in the guardroom at the head of the Deep Watch; Marrun's messengers check them before climbing.
- Found the tracks this spring, in passages empty for centuries by the oldest Archive maps (dead ends, old rockfalls). More than one creature; source unknown.
- Races Doran up the Upper Stair; usually wins and never mentions it.
- Parents (both Wardens) dead "some years ago"; no family in Cavelor.
- Whether Varn is jealous Doran was sent up is deliberately unanswered.

## Ysra (ysra_mender)

- Age 38. Healer of the Halls 16 years (since the damp fever, aged 22). Rhenna's father still not mentioned (left open).
- Chosen at 14 by old Healer Maudry (dead), who kept the alcove before her. First Deep Watch turn at 16.
- Maudry caught the damp fever while nursing and died of it 16 years ago; Ysra became Cavelor's only healer. No apprentice now.
- Nursed Maelis, Ilse and Tamm in the fever; could not save them.
- Her workplace: a healer's alcove off the Oath Hall (cots, dried roots, a spring-water basin).
- Keeps a patient book (as Maudry did) for 16 years: numbers rising since monsters reached the outer tunnels 11 years ago, faster since Brask doubled the lowest passages this spring. Shows it to Brask monthly; he changes nothing.
- Scolds Brask about his extra shifts, lately in front of Halvara.
- Asks Corvan for surface honey for burns; he gives it reluctantly.
- Does not know Rhenna counts the Wardens or fears the sky; has noticed her standing straight.
