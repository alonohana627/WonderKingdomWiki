# Established facts: selsior

Written by NPC lore writers so later writers stay consistent. Not shown on the wiki.

## Elinor (elinor)

- Age 17. Elder child of Aveline and Leofric.
- Leaves the palace at dawn by the little door in the kitchen garden wall; back before Agnes makes her bed.
- Trains with Rowena since last autumn; asked with hood up and name unspoken. Elinor believes the disguise works (Rowena knows).
- Cloak is Sybil's loan. Hands blistered; hides them at breakfast.
- Has visited the Guard stables (Wystan's) since she was small; met Aylwin there. Aylwin recognised her under the cloak the first morning and has told no one.
- Someone does know about Elinor and Aylwin, but who is unset.
- Has not been told the queen suspects.

## Alaric (alaric)

- Age 9. Younger child of Aveline and Leofric.
- Beetles in labelled jars on his windowsill; keys from drawers and flowerbeds (one Cuthbert says opened a lost larder); has found the locks for very few of them.
- Godwin taught him helpful vs harmful beetles; they watch the mean wild bees together.
- Races the clock tower bells with Peterkin; Peterkin is the only one he has told about the passage.
- Passage evidence: a draught under one throne-hall tapestry, a hollow-sounding stretch of wall, a key that fits nothing. Osbert neither confirms nor denies. Whether the passage exists is unset.
- Has noticed Elinor gone some mornings; she told him she goes riding.

## Crispin (crispin)

- Age 42. House playwright of the Gilded Stage for 15 years, under Lavinia.
- Father (unnamed, dead) painted scenery for the Theatre Row theatres; Crispin keeps one of his brushes.
- His best-known comedy: The Baker's Wager, much revived; bakers quote it.
- The new Balrog War play: four acts done, no fifth act. Five endings drafted (one where the Balrog speaks; one where it never appears, only its light on the actors' faces, which he prefers and Lavinia would hate).
- Lavinia reminds him about once a day that she has bet everything on it.
- Marigold has hinted she knows where he keeps his discarded endings.
- Married to Rosamund; no wedding date set.

## Edric (edric)

- Age 46. Joined the Guard at 20 (26 years ago); first patrol at 23. Already a seasoned rider when Rhys arrived as a recruit (22 years ago).
- Lieutenant of the Road Patrol 7 years, appointed by Rhys in Rhys's first year as Captain.
- Tristan is his younger brother. Father (unnamed, dead) was a carter between the capital and the other towns; mother (unnamed, dead) kept the house in Riverside. No other family.
- Writes a report after every patrol; it goes to Leofric's map and to Gervase's monster map.
- Patrols leave the Knights' Court before dawn, aim to be back before the gates close.
- Tells the same terrible jokes on every ride.

## Hodge (hodge)

- Age 10. Lives in a house off the courtyard where Gytha runs the washing yard, in the old quarters.
- Hodge's father: gone; Gytha does not talk about him (nothing else set: not stated whether dead or away). No grandfather mentioned.
- Most dawns crosses the rooftops to a ridge overlooking the Knights' Court practice yard and copies the drills with a broom handle; mouths Bertrand's corrections. Has never held a real sword.
- Has seen the cloaked stranger (Elinor) training at dawn from his ridge; does not know who it is and wants to find out.
- Gytha has never caught him on the roofs.
- Favourite of Winifred's stories: the Last Stand at the Tree.

## Leofric (leofric)

- Age 47. Grew up in the Scholars' Quarter; father (unnamed, dead) a copyist of maps; mother (unnamed) dead.
- From age 20 (27 years ago) surveyed the royal roads with Gervase for about 7 years.
- Met Aveline 20 years ago and married her before that year was out (consistent with "met four years before coronation; married 20 years ago").
- The great map is in the room beside the queen's study; red mark per monster encounter, dated. Reports come weekly from Edric. Gervase's monster map agrees with his.
- Senses Elinor is hiding something; has not worked out what.

## Osbert (osbert)

- Age 69. Came to the palace at 14 as a boot boy (55 years ago, early in Bertram's reign); has lived nowhere else since.
- Royal Steward 34 years (since age 35), appointed by Bertram after Steward Hamlyn (dead) died.
- Never married.
- Keeps in a desk drawer: the coronation feast menu, a beetle in a jar from Alaric, a list of everyone who served in the household in his time.
- Has trained under-stewards (unnamed), none trusted with all the keys.
- Knows Elinor draws the bolt of the kitchen garden door at dawn and whose boots are muddy; has told no one.
- Knows whether the throne-hall passage exists; the answer is unset.

## Pim (pim)

- Age 16. The Guard takes recruits from age 16 (set here). Pim enlisted at dawn on his 16th birthday, this spring.
- Parents (unnamed) died of a winter fever when he was a baby. Raised in the old quarters by his grandmother Gisela (dead two winters ago), who mended pots and kettles at a stall.
- In his first week told Captain Rhys to stand at ease; Rhys did. Once saluted a statue.
- Pinned "guard full of holes" above his bunk.
- Shares a supper bench with Tobe.

## Robin (robin)

- Age 9. Son of Agatha and Silas; grandson of Osmund. Family lives in Riverside.
- Rules: never alone at the deep end of the bank; never after dark (newer, since Osmund stopped crossing after dark).
- Jasper pays him a little over value; Robin wants to see inside Jasper's unopened box.
- The badge: found last spring at the year's lowest water, in the mud below the ferry landing. Coin-sized, heavy, stamped with a seal. Jasper did not recognise it (not the crown's, any town's he knows, or any guild's); Osmund nodded "put it somewhere safe". Jasper has made two offers; Robin refused. What the seal is: unset.

## Agatha (agatha)

- Age 37. Daughter of Osmund. Mother Edda (dead), died when Agatha was 15. No siblings mentioned.
- Osmund fished as well as ferrying when younger; gave her his nets when his hands grew too stiff. She mends them every evening on the house step.
- Met Silas across his market stall (she brought eels and called his prices a disgrace). Married 11 years.
- Torn nets: long ragged rents through several rows of mesh, empty around the hole. Cause unset. She has moved her fishing to shallower water near the bank.
- Osmund will not tell her why he no longer crosses after dark.

## Aylwin (aylwin)

- Age 19. Parents (unnamed) drowned when a river barge overturned in a spring flood when he was 4; raised by Wystan in rooms above the Guard stables. Not stated which parent was Wystan's child.
- Joined the Guard at 17; on the road patrol since last year, under Edric.
- The boars: earlier this spring, on the road to Kaldemor; his horse held while others shied; he and it held the road while the patrol turned. Says a boar ate his hat.
- Recognised Elinor by her walk the first morning she trained with Rowena; told no one.
- Poems wrapped in oilcloth under a loose board in the Guard stables' hayloft; mostly about Elinor. Has one he may give her.

## Brann (brann)

- Age 48. Joined the Guard at 19 (29 years ago); at the gate 20 years; Gate Sergeant 12 years.
- Biscuit turned up at the gate four winters ago. Brann secretly feeds him supper crusts.
- Taught Hollis to start greetings stern.
- Sends hurt travellers to an apothecary first; passes what he hears to the Knights' Court the same day.
- No family stated.

## Constance (constance)

- Age 23. Grew up on an orchard farm along the Royal Road toward Kaldemor; parents (unnamed, alive) still keep it, write twice a year, play no part.
- Came to the Royal Academy at 15; studied 5 years under Morwen; left at 20 (3 years ago) before her final year. Still borrows books from Morwen.
- Has lodged at the Crooked Lantern for 3 years.
- Partnered with Thurstan 2 years ago: met in the Lantern's common room the evening he came back from his first bounty.
- Keeps bound notebooks, one per season on the road, on the shelf above her bed. Has shown them only to Thurstan and Joan. Suspects the monster changes come in waves; unproven.

## Gytha (gytha)

- Age 35 (Hodge born when she was 25). Only child of Simkin and Winifred; grew up in Simkin's lane.
- Never speaks of Hodge's father to anyone, including Winifred, who has never asked or told that story. Whether he is dead or away: still unset.
- Has heard many reports of Hodge on the roofs from neighbours; never caught him. Does not know about the dawn broom-handle drills. The broom handle Hodge uses is an old one that used to lean in a corner of her washing yard.
- Would accept Simkin's trade or any other for Hodge, not the Guard.

## Joan (joan)

- Age 44. Born upstairs at the Crooked Lantern (room over the kitchen). Never married.
- The Crooked Lantern: old quarters tavern, lopsided sign with a crooked lantern, low beamed common room, nine rooms upstairs rented cheap to adventurers.
- Mother Beatrix (invented, dead) kept the Lantern for more than 30 years; married Roland, who lodged there between journeys; died 12 years ago. Joan has kept it alone since.
- Roland was away adventuring through much of Joan's childhood; gave up the road when his knees failed (year unset); sits in a corner of the common room.
- Joan keeps a slate behind the bar of lodgers out on the road and their expected return; has never yet lost one.
- Hook: Roland has started telling a story he never told when Joan was a girl (content unset).

## Mariota (mariota)

- Age 19. Tobe's elder sister (two years older); they have no other living family.
- Grew up in the Scholars' Quarter in two rooms over their father's bindery.
- Father Ranulf (invented, dead), bookbinder for the Academy and libraries; died two winters ago of a bad chest; the bindery was sold to pay what was owed.
- Mother Sabine (invented, dead) died of a fever 12 years ago (Mariota 7, Tobe 5).
- Won her Academy place before Ranulf died (by 17), with an essay that argued with its examiners; Bartholomew made sure she kept it.
- Studies under Theobald; is combing his 60 years of charts and the Academy's histories for a cause of the monster change. Has three things she is fairly sure of and a fourth she fears and has told no one (all contents unset).
- Visits the Court weekly to check on Tobe's third cut.

## Maud (maud)

- Not the same person as Agatha's dead mother Edda. This Maud is the housekeeper, age 52.
- Born a few streets from the Crown Market; daughter of a fruit seller (unnamed, dead) with a barrow at the market's edge; father also dead. Never married.
- Housekeeper to Dame Orsa 22 years; lives in the rooms above and behind the general store.
- Has never learned what Dame Orsa's title was for; has searched the house and found nothing; tells Margery a new (often pirate) theory about once a month.
- Margery is her oldest friend in the market and first stop each dawn. Alys lets her taste cheese now (took about ten years).
- Keeps kitchen rinds and scraps in her apron pocket for Biscuit.

## Morwen (morwen)

- Age 54. Began teaching at the Academy at 26 (28 years ago).
- Family claims to have been in Selsior since the Academy's founding (unproven). For generations they collected fragments of Balrog War seal-craft; kept in a chest, now in Morwen's rooms.
- Raised by her grandmother Eluned (invented, dead), who died 28 years ago when Morwen was 26. Parents not mentioned. No husband or children.
- Does not know how the seal was finished or how to mend it; says so. Has one fragment she has never shown anyone (content unset).
- Bartholomew has known her since she was a young teacher; they argue over supper about once a week.

## Rosamund (rosamund)

- Age 40. Grew up in the old quarters; parents (unnamed, dead) a glover and a seamstress.
- Joined the Gilded Stage at 17 (23 years ago): first part a line and a half as a maid bringing a letter; Lavinia was still playing the great parts then.
- Met Crispin when he became house playwright (15 years ago); married him 12 years ago.
- Marigold is 10. Carried on stage as a baby in a basket in the second act of The Baker's Wager (so that comedy is at least 10 years old).
- Quarrel with Percival began 10 years ago when Lavinia first cast them opposite each other. Privately thinks he is a very good actor.
- Reads all Crispin's drafts aloud.

## Thurstan (thurstan)

- Age 21. Youngest of four and only boy on a hill farm a few days west of Selsior. Father (unnamed) died when he was small. Mother and three older sisters (unnamed, alive) still run the farm; they write; play no part.
- Came to Selsior at 18 (3 years ago); has lodged at the Crooked Lantern since his first night. Joan doubles his supper.
- Roland trained him for most of his first year: sacks in the Crown Market by day, drills in the Lantern's back yard by evening; no bounty until he could hold a guard for a count of a hundred. Calls Roland "the old man".
- First bounty 2 years ago; Constance pointed out four mistakes and became his partner. They have not yet lost a fight. He carries spare ink for her.
- Has never tried the Guard's trials.

## Tobe (tobe)

- Age 17. Mariota's younger brother (see Mariota for parents Ranulf and Sabine, the bindery, and dates).
- After the bindery was sold, worked fetching and shelving in a Scholars' Quarter library; read its sword books there.
- Joined the Guard this spring at 17 (same intake as Pim).
- Has read six accounts of the Sword Saint, all disagreeing.
- Drills alone in the evenings. Avice once corrected his third cut in five words; he has not thanked her.

## Wat (wat)

- Age 11. Kenric is 7 years older (18); Kenric was already Selene's apprentice at 15.
- Father (unnamed, dead) died in a fall from a builder's scaffold when Wat was a baby.
- Mother Cristin (invented, dead) washed at Gytha's yard; died of a fever 3 winters ago (Wat 8). Wat met Hodge as a toddler in Gytha's yard. Gytha was kind to Cristin.
- Picked pockets in the Crown Market for most of a year; told Kenric he was running errands. Reynold caught him two summers ago when Wat tried for Reynold's own purse; timed his run across the market and hired him.
- Wat and Kenric sleep in the loft above Selene's forge.
- Now tells owners when their purses are badly tied.
- Knows about Hodge's dawn broom-handle training; has not told Gytha.

## Winifred (winifred)

- Age 74. Born in the old quarters. At 12 went to work for a seamstress (unnamed) sewing for envoys' households near the plaza; heard all four peoples' Balrog tales there.
- Tells stories from a stool on her own doorstep, in the lane where Simkin's shop is.
- Married Simkin 49 years ago, the year after he took the shop in the lane (he has been there 50 years). Simkin's age: not set here.
- Gytha, their only child, born when Winifred was 39.
- Has never told or asked about Hodge's father.
- Sleeps little; from her window at dawn has seen Hodge cross the roof ridge opposite most mornings with something long over his shoulder. Has not told Gytha. Does not necessarily know what he does at the Court.

## Agnes (agnes)

- Age 19. Mother Juliana (invented, dead) kept the palace stillroom; died of a winter fever when Agnes was 6. Raised in the palace kitchens by Cuthbert and the kitchen staff.
- Entered household service at 14 (5 years ago) under Osbert; lower corridors 3 years; upper halls for the last 2 years.
- Friends with Peterkin (trades news for kitchen biscuits). Can get no stories out of Sybil.
- Noticed Elinor's muddy boots last autumn; knows the mud is from a much-trodden yard, not the garden; knows Elinor goes out before dawn and is back before breakfast. Theories: a sweetheart, a secret, or a horse. Does not know about the sword training. Has told no one. Suspects Osbert already knows.

## Aldith (aldith)

- Age 72. Born in her cottage on the Riverside bank; garden runs down to the river, river-mint bed at the bottom.
- Mother Elfrida (invented, dead about 30 years) was the Riverside herb wife before her; also Ansel's mother. Father not mentioned.
- Never married, no children. Has not found anyone to hand the garden on to.
- Ansel left to study healing in the Scholars' Quarter when young. Relative ages of Aldith and Ansel: unset.
- Recipe swap: Ansel's folded notes turn up in her basket; her unlabelled salve goes into the palace medicine chest.
- Rubs mint and comfrey salve into Osmund's stiff hands; doses Robin's coughs.
- Edwina: friends for 50 years; meet on Aldith's bench.
- Has seen river wounds no hook or net made (a bargehand bruised as if gripped). Keeps her own notes in a cheap book; has not told Ansel.

## Avice (avice)

- Age 18. From a herding family in the Midbarium highlands (which of the four families: unset). Family unnamed, alive, far away, play no part.
- Walked the whole way to Selsior this spring, many days; joined in the same intake as Pim and Tobe.
- Her five words to Tobe on the third cut: "Your back foot is lying."
- Arrives in the practice yard earlier than any recruit; that is how she has seen the cloaked stranger with Rowena. Does not know who it is; has told no one.
- Bertrand bellows at her least; she resents it.

## Baldwin (baldwin)

- Age 68. Lamplighter 50 years, since 18; learned under an old lamplighter Odo (invented, dead, no relation).
- The palace-steps lamp burns blue; nobody knows why.
- Lights the plaza lamps around the Great Bell at dusk, then old-quarters lamps on the long way home; lives in a narrow house near the gate end of the city. Winifred's evening stories start when his lamps are lit.
- Never married. Ida passes him most nights. Buys oil and wicks from Gilbert; extra gate lamps paid from his own pocket.
- Has noticed Hugh goes quiet on still evenings; has never asked why. Takes no side in the Bell argument.

## Bartholomew (bartholomew)

- Age 77. Shepherd's son from the downs north of the capital; came to the Academy at 14, studied 10 years, began teaching at 24.
- Master of the Academy 21 years, chosen by the other masters. Still teaches one class a season.
- Known Morwen since she began teaching, 28 years ago. Thomasin is his protegee.

## Benedict (benedict)

- Age 73. Came to the library as a boy to fetch and shelve; Head Librarian 30 years. Teaches palace copyists and scribes in a north-lit back room; Petronella his best pupil.
- Ermengarde's shop is beside the library, through the wall. Relative ages unset.
- Knew Ranulf (Mariota and Tobe's father), who did the Academy's larger binding work. After the bindery sale, Benedict gave Tobe the fetching-and-shelving job: the "Scholars' Quarter library" in Tobe's lore is the Academy's great library.
- Missing volume of the Aristocrat war chronicles: Benedict read its first pages 18 years ago at a desk by the window, was called away, and it was gone. Searches every shelf yearly. What it says and where it went: unset. Thomasin asks about it more often lately.

## Bertrand (bertrand)

- Age 51. Joined the Guard at 18 (33 years ago); grew up in the Crown Market back streets; father (unnamed, dead) unloaded carts. Drill sergeant 15 years.
- Married Helewise 19 years ago (met arguing over porridge rations). No children. Both say "a few hundred recruits" (in total, not per year) is family enough.
- The list: three books, 600+ names, shelf above his bed; date in, rank earned, posting. Marks a name in the margin when it appears among the hurt in patrol reports. Deaths among his recruits: not stated.
- Has noticed a small figure (Hodge) on a roof ridge copying drills at dawn; has told no one; bellows louder.

## Biscuit (biscuit)

- Dog. Arrived four winters ago, thin, in snow, off the Royal Road; origin unknown.
- Middling size, short coat, colour of a well-baked crust. Tail a short stub (born so or lost: unknown). Who named him: disputed; Hollis claims it loudest.
- Sleeps on an old sack by the gatehouse door. Morning: greets Hollis, checks Tilda's taxi stand, then to the Crown Market (Margery's stale loaves, Maud's apron scraps), back by afternoon. Evening: Brann's supper crusts; sleeps on Brann's boots.
- Goes a little way out on the Royal Road most days; lately comes back sooner and stares into the dark past Baldwin's gate lamps.
- Afraid of thunder and of the gate's great bar slamming.

## Cuthbert (cuthbert)

- Age 48. Came to the palace at 14 as a scullion (34 years ago, mid-Bertram); royal cook 18 years (since 30). Cooked Aveline's coronation feast 16 years ago (the menu Osbert keeps).
- Wife Juliana (see Agnes) died 13 years ago. Makes her plum preserve every autumn from her book.
- Keeps a stained recipe book of every town's dishes and who taught them; the Cavelor page is blank (has never tasted Cavelor food).
- Harvest feast (one dish from every town) not yet held.
- Remembers the lost larder from his scullion days; cannot remember where it was.

## Edith (edith)

- Age 66. Costumer on Theatre Row 48 years (since 18). Works in a long attic halfway down the Row, in a building that has housed three theatres in her lifetime.
- Sybil is her younger sister. They sup together once a week.
- Dressed Lavinia in the great parts, Rosamund since her first part; sewed the basket lining for baby Marigold in The Baker's Wager and kept a scrap.
- Haggles weekly with Isabeau.
- Has noticed one subject Sybil goes quiet on (unspecified; implied the princess/cloak); does not know the secret.

## Everard (everard)

- Age 18. From one of the harbour towns (which: unset). Family unnamed, alive, far away, play no part.
- Came to the Academy at 15 (3 years ago); hid his first magic for a year before that.
- Sits in the Gilded Stage's cheap gallery; can quote The Baker's Wager; suspects the Balrog War play lacks an ending. Interested in stage lighting (whether he secretly does it: unset).
- Mariota has nearly caught him being clever twice.

## Fulke (fulke)

- Age 37. Born a street off the plaza. Lute second-hand from an old plaza singer (unnamed, dead).
- Tell: when he lies, his voice rises half a tone and his eyebrows go up.
- Plays for Mirabel each evening after Baldwin lights the Bell lamps. Wrote a verse about the Bell humming; sings it without the tell. Takes no side.
- Sings all four peoples' Balrog War versions; claims no answer.
- Asks travellers at the gate for songs most mornings. Thinks Cecily will be better than him; has not told her.

## Gervase (gervase)

- Age 49. Grew up in the Scholars' Quarter. Two years older than Leofric.
- Surveyed with Leofric from age 22 (27 years ago) for about 7 years; the survey ended 20 years ago, the year Leofric met Aveline. Claims the disputed road was one they walked together.
- Works in a narrow house in the Scholars' Quarter, maps on walls, ceiling and floor. No family stated.
- Edric sends him a copy of every patrol report and visits in person. Has started a second sheet for roads near the towns; measuring the spread's speed.

## Gideon (gideon)

- Age 78. Aged 20 in the crowd at Bertram's coronation (58 years ago); heard the Great Bell ring then and at Aveline's coronation (twice in his life).
- Royal Herald for 40 years: took the post 56 years ago (2 years into Bertram's reign) and retired 16 years ago. His last two proclamations: Bertram's death and Aveline's coronation. Clement took the post that year.
- 50 years ago (aged 28) cried the expedition's setting out from the palace steps, and later the news of what it found (Children of the Tree, Cavelor).
- Perch: a room under the eaves above the plaza, up a long ladder. Hears the clock tower bells, the Court's drill bell, the market's opening bell, a bell on the gatehouse. Has said nothing about hearing the Great Bell since the coronation (open).
- Clement climbs up weekly; Gideon has heard him rehearse to the pigeons. Aldous doesn't climb ladders: once a month Gideon climbs down and sits with Aldous at the palace when the vault closes. How long they have been friends: unset.
- No family mentioned.

## Hugh (hugh)

- Age 71. Baldwin's older brother (by 3 years). Parents not mentioned. Never married.
- Served in the Royal Guard (walls, palace) when young. Warden of the Great Bell 30 years (since 41), given the post in Bertram's reign when the old Warden (unnamed, dead) died.
- The Warden rings the Bell: Hugh rang it himself at Aveline's coronation 16 years ago, the only time in his wardenship. Aged 13 at Bertram's coronation.
- Waits each evening until Baldwin has lit the lamps around the dais before going home.
- Hears the hum on still, windless evenings (unlike Mirabel's windy dusk); has told no one, not Mirabel or Baldwin. Since when: unset. Cause: open.
- Tells Mab off every afternoon for cartwheels by the dais; has never actually stopped her.

## Hollis (hollis)

- Age 24. Grew up in a narrow house in the city's inner streets. Parents (unnamed, alive), a bricklayer and his wife, moved to farming country past the Greenwood when he was 19; write twice a year; play no part.
- Joined the Guard at 19 (5 years ago); posted to the gate a year later (4 years at the gate).
- A worn, hollowed step under the gate arch trips travellers: "Mind the step, and the Sergeant."
- Trades road news with Tilda on slow afternoons. Claims loudest to have named Biscuit; his story changes.
- Wants to walk the Royal Road past the rise to the east.

## Ida (ida)

- Age 38. Grew up in the old quarters in a lane with steps. Father (unnamed, dead) cleared and mended the old drains; as a girl she carried his lamp; she still keeps his old map of the drains. Mother (unnamed, dead) kept a mending stall. No siblings.
- Joined the Guard at 20 (18 years ago); night watch 16 years. Reports to the Knights' Court each morning.
- Married Dickon 12 years ago; no children. She works nights, he days; they meet at dawn and dusk.
- Lettice and her terrier sometimes walk a lane with her.
- The scratching: for some months; slower and heavier than rats, moves along the channels grating to grating, keeping pace with her and stopping when she stops. Has seen nothing. Told Rhys; he did not laugh. Ida thinks the rats are leaving. Cause: unset.

## Helewise (helewise)

- Age 49. Grew up behind her mother's dry-goods stall (beans, lentils, peas) in the Crown Market; mother (unnamed) dead. Father not mentioned.
- Came to the Knights' Court at 20 as a stores clerk; Quartermaster 14 years. Storeroom under the Knights' Court, ledger chained to the counter.
- Met Bertrand 19 years ago over porridge and married him the same year.
- Plays Vessa and Selene (sisters) off against each other; Vessa calls her a bandit.
- After a bad report evening, sends new boots to the recruit Bertrand will shout at hardest.
- Short of boots, saddle leather, shield straps, cloaks; fears the Guard cannot equip the next intake.

## Jocelyn (jocelyn)

- Age 34. Came to the vault at 19 (15 years ago) from a clerk's stool in the palace counting room; Aldous fetched her himself. Her answer: she counts "until it comes out the same twice".
- Married Wulfric 4 years ago; she asked him first whether he would say anything. No children. She labels his lunch and his cloak hook.
- The box: found last winter on the back shelves of the oldest records room. Plain, heavy, iron-bound, the vault's old mark on the lid, a lock no vault key opens, label faded almost to nothing. In no ledger. Aldous says it predates him and he does not know what is in it; he did not seem surprised. Contents: unset.
- She has started looking at keys in other people's hands (any link to Alaric's keys: unset).

## Kenric (kenric)

- Age 18. Grew up in two rooms near Gytha's washing yard. Remembers his father (Kenric was 7 at the scaffold fall).
- Apprenticed to Selene at 15 (3 years ago); had hung round her forge since small. Cristin died that same winter; Selene let both boys sleep in the forge loft.
- Two summers ago Reynold walked Wat to the forge and told Kenric about the pickpocketing and the job. Kenric blames himself.
- Has made knives, hinges, nails, a poker. First sword this winter if Selene judges him ready; plans to balance it for a short arm.

## Lambert (lambert)

- Age 22. Born in Selsior, rooms over a shop at the bottom of Theatre Row. Parents (unnamed, alive), a shopkeeper and his wife, moved to farming country when he was 16; play no part.
- Rafe's first lesson at about age 10: juggled three spare puppet heads and handed them over. By 15 could keep five things up.
- Pitch on the plaza steps beside Emmeline's flower stall; courting about a year.
- Taught Mab to juggle two balls; she passes his hat.
- Has asked Clement about juggling for the queen; Clement promised nothing. Routine ends with a prop crown borrowed from the Gilded Stage.

## Lavinia (lavinia)

- Age 72. Came to the Gilded Stage at 15 (57 years ago); swept the stage a season, then went on for a sick actress.
- The old manager (unnamed, no heirs) died 35 years ago and left her the theatre.
- Played her last great part 18 years ago; since then only small chosen parts. Never married.
- The Gilded Stage has a palace box no one from the palace has sat in for many years.
- The bet: the theatre's savings, a loan against the building, and her name. Has not said whom she will hand the theatre to.

## Lucan (lucan)

- Age 46. Father (unnamed, a bargehand) died when Lucan was 20; mother (unnamed, kept a tavern kitchen at a river landing) died soon after. No siblings. No spouse mentioned.
- Bought his first barge at 25; now runs three. Bennet built two of them.
- Carries grain (to Hereward's mill and bakers), stone from quarries upstream, timber.

## Wystan (wystan)

- Age 72. Came to the Knights' Court stables at 14 (58 years ago); stablemaster 40 years (since 32), through three captains.
- Wife (unnamed) died young. Only child: daughter Elswyth (invented, dead), raised above the stables; married a riverman (unnamed) and lived in Riverside. Both drowned 15 years ago when their barge overturned in a spring flood. So Aylwin's mother was Wystan's daughter.
- Told Pim not to salute near the horses.
- Text implies (does not state) that Wystan has noticed Aylwin and Elinor; "who knows" remains formally unset.
