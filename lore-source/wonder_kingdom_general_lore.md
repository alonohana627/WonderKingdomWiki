# Wonder Kingdom — Complete General Lore

**Worldbuilding Reference · Veligor · General Lore · Version 1.0**

## 1. Overview of Wonder Kingdom

Wonder Kingdom is a prosperous, peaceful, colorful, and genuinely united fantasy kingdom. Its inhabitants come from different cultural backgrounds, follow different traditions, and live in settlements with distinct identities, yet they share a strong sense of belonging and mutual respect.

The game initially takes place on **Veligor**, a large island containing the kingdom's capital, several distinctive towns, vast wilderness regions, ancient ruins, enchanted forests, mountains, coastal settlements, and the enormous World Tree.

The kingdom was established following a devastating war against an ancient being known as **the Balrog**. Before this war, Veligor was divided between four peoples who had fought one another for generations. Their eventual cooperation against the Balrog led to the establishment of Wonder Kingdom and the beginning of a lasting era of peace.

Today, the kingdom's unity is genuine. Its people are happy, its settlements are prosperous, and its cultural differences are celebrated rather than feared.

However, the wilderness surrounding these settlements has become increasingly dangerous.

Fifty years ago, an expedition into the World Tree accidentally damaged the ancient magical seal imprisoning the Balrog beneath its roots.

Since then, the Balrog's influence has gradually spread throughout Veligor, making wild creatures increasingly aggressive and transforming once relatively peaceful environments into dangerous territories populated by monsters.

The people of Wonder Kingdom remain united, but the land surrounding them is becoming increasingly hostile.

This contrast forms the foundation of the game's world and overarching narrative.

---

## 2. The Age of Division

Long before Wonder Kingdom existed, Veligor was inhabited by four major peoples.

Each developed its own culture, traditions, values, and understanding of how society should function.

### The Savages

The Savages inhabited the rugged wilderness and mountainous regions of Veligor.

They valued physical strength, resilience, personal independence, ancestral traditions, and their connection to the natural world.

Their communities developed around survival, cooperation, and respect for those who demonstrated courage and perseverance.

They resisted attempts by outside authorities to control their territories or impose unfamiliar social structures.

### The Magicians

The Magicians dedicated themselves to understanding the supernatural forces of Veligor.

They were particularly interested in the World Tree, ancient magical environments, and the relationship between magic and nature.

Their societies valued knowledge, magical ability, preservation, and the study of forces that ordinary people did not fully understand.

They opposed attempts to exploit or control locations they considered magically significant.

### The Commons

The Commons consisted primarily of farmers, merchants, craftsmen, settlers, and other ordinary inhabitants of Veligor.

Their communities emphasized cooperation, practical skills, agriculture, commerce, stability, and prosperity.

They established villages, farms, workshops, trade routes, and settlements that supported everyday life.

They sought greater security and stability across the island.

### The Aristocrats

The Aristocrats established fortified settlements, political institutions, systems of law, and organized leadership structures.

They valued governance, order, administration, and the development of large, structured communities.

They believed that centralized organization could create a more stable and prosperous Veligor.

### The Ancient Conflict

For generations, the peoples of Veligor were divided into two broad alliances:

- The Savages and Magicians.
- The Commons and Aristocrats.

Their disagreements involved territory, political authority, cultural traditions, and access to important natural and magical locations.

These conflicts escalated into prolonged struggles that prevented Veligor from becoming a united civilization.

Although each group possessed its own strengths, none could establish lasting peace.

The conflict continued until the appearance of a threat greater than anything the island had previously encountered.

---

## 3. The Coming of the Balrog

The ancient conflict between Veligor's peoples was interrupted by the appearance of **the Balrog**, an immensely powerful and destructive being.

The Balrog threatened every civilization on the island.

Settlements were devastated, armies were destroyed, and even the most powerful warriors and magicians struggled to resist it.

For the first time, the four peoples faced an enemy that none could defeat independently.

Their old disagreements became insignificant compared with the danger threatening their collective survival.

Recognizing this, the four peoples agreed to unite their forces.

Each contributed essential abilities and resources.

**The Savages** provided their strongest warriors, whose courage and physical prowess proved invaluable in confronting the Balrog directly.

**The Magicians** provided their knowledge of ancient magic and supernatural forces, ultimately making the Balrog's imprisonment possible.

**The Commons** organized supplies, built equipment, maintained logistical networks, and supported the united armies.

**The Aristocrats** contributed military organization, leadership, administration, and coordination between the different forces.

Together, the four peoples fought the Balrog.

Their cooperation ultimately allowed them to defeat the creature.

However, the Balrog could not be permanently destroyed.

Instead, the united forces developed a powerful magical seal capable of imprisoning it beneath the enormous World Tree at the center of Veligor.

The Balrog was sealed deep within the tree's ancient roots.

This victory marked the end of the ancient conflict and the beginning of a new era.

---

## 4. The Founding of Wonder Kingdom

Following the defeat and imprisonment of the Balrog, the leaders of the four peoples gathered to establish a lasting peace.

They signed a historic treaty that formally ended their old conflicts and established a united kingdom.

This kingdom became known as **Wonder Kingdom**.

The founding treaty established the principle that all peoples of Veligor could coexist peacefully, regardless of their ancestry, cultural background, social position, or magical abilities.

The kingdom was founded upon the cooperation that had made victory against the Balrog possible.

Over the following generations, the four peoples gradually developed a shared identity.

Their original cultures were not erased. Instead, they continued evolving within the new kingdom.

Communities traded with one another, established relationships, exchanged knowledge, and participated in the development of a common civilization.

The ancient hostilities gradually disappeared.

Eventually, Wonder Kingdom became a genuinely peaceful and prosperous society.

Its inhabitants no longer regarded other communities as historical enemies.

The unity created by the founding treaty became a natural part of everyday life.

### The Meaning of Unity

Wonder Kingdom is not a civilization held together by fear, coercion, or temporary political compromise.

Its unity is genuine.

Its people understand and appreciate one another despite their cultural differences.

Different towns maintain their own traditions and lifestyles, but these differences do not create widespread hostility.

The kingdom's inhabitants are proud of their shared history and the civilization their ancestors established.

The old conflicts remain part of historical knowledge, but they no longer define relationships between communities.

This applies to every settlement on Veligor, including Urbanograd, whose unconventional culture is accepted throughout the kingdom.

## 5. Veligor — The Island of Wonder Kingdom

Veligor is a large island containing the principal settlements and environments of Wonder Kingdom.

Its geography is diverse, with fertile plains, mountainous highlands, magical forests, coastal regions, rural settlements, ancient ruins, underground caverns, and extensive wilderness areas.

The enormous World Tree stands near the center of the island and serves as one of its most recognizable landmarks.

The island's settlements are connected through roads, pathways, and regional trade networks.

Although the surrounding wilderness has become increasingly dangerous, the inhabited settlements remain lively, functional, and prosperous.

Each town possesses a distinct cultural and environmental identity.

### 5.1 Selsior — The City of Everything

Selsior is the capital of Wonder Kingdom and the largest city on Veligor.

It is a bustling fantasy metropolis, characterized by its enormous population, extensive commercial activity, lively public spaces, and remarkable cultural diversity.

Selsior is known as **The City of Everything**.

Its residents engage in commerce, craftsmanship, education, entertainment, administration, research, and countless other occupations.

The city contains busy marketplaces, shops, workshops, residential neighborhoods, educational institutions, cultural venues, restaurants, and public gathering places.

#### Architecture

Selsior follows a classic medieval fantasy architectural style.

Its buildings feature timber-framed structures, stone foundations, colorful rooftops, towers, and traditional urban construction.

Cobblestone streets connect its neighborhoods.

The city's central areas contain broad avenues, large public squares, and important communal spaces.

Surrounding these central areas are dense, organically developed neighborhoods containing winding streets, narrow passages, courtyards, residential buildings, and local marketplaces.

#### Culture and Atmosphere

Selsior is particularly cheerful, sociable, and energetic.

Street performances, celebrations, entertainment, public gatherings, and everyday social activity contribute to its joyful atmosphere.

The city is busy not only because people work there, but because its inhabitants enjoy participating in urban life.

People from every part of Wonder Kingdom live and interact within Selsior.

Its cultural diversity is natural and widely accepted.

Selsior represents the prosperity, cooperation, and shared identity that emerged from the founding of Wonder Kingdom.

It is a city where many different lifestyles and ambitions coexist.

### 5.2 Kaldemor — The Town of Joyful Craftsmanship

Kaldemor is a cheerful pastoral settlement surrounded by fertile land, rolling hills, green fields, and pleasant rural landscapes.

Its general atmosphere is inspired by the bright, welcoming character of MapleStory's Henesys.

The town's cultural identity centers on **joyful craftsmanship, creativity, agriculture, and community life**.

Its inhabitants take pride in producing things with their own hands and maintaining a comfortable, fulfilling lifestyle.

#### Architecture and Environment

Kaldemor features colorful wooden houses, charming cottages, workshops, gardens, agricultural buildings, and welcoming communal areas.

Its surrounding landscape includes meadows, farmland, orchards, gentle hills, and patches of woodland.

The environment is colorful, sunny, and inviting.

#### Culture and Atmosphere

Kaldemor's inhabitants value creativity, practical knowledge, friendliness, cooperation, and the satisfaction of meaningful work.

Traditional crafts are an important part of everyday life.

Woodworking, carpentry, pottery, farming, and other artisanal activities contribute to the town's cultural identity.

Kaldemor is not an impoverished or undeveloped farming settlement.

It is a prosperous and happy town whose inhabitants appreciate their lifestyle.

Where Selsior represents the excitement and diversity of metropolitan civilization, Kaldemor represents the joy of creating, growing, and living within a close-knit community.

### 5.3 Midbarium — The Highland Settlement

Midbarium is a rugged mountainous settlement associated with the ancestral culture of the Savages.

Its geography and general visual atmosphere are inspired by MapleStory's Perion.

The settlement is surrounded by rocky mountains, cliffs, valleys, highland pathways, and difficult terrain.

Its inhabitants historically developed a culture shaped by the challenges of living in this environment.

#### Architecture and Environment

Midbarium's buildings are adapted to the mountainous landscape.

Stone foundations, heavy timber structures, terraces, carved pathways, stairways, and wooden bridges are characteristic of its proposed architectural style.

Its surrounding wilderness contains rocky valleys, caves, highland trails, and mountain regions.

#### Culture and Atmosphere

Midbarium is associated with strength, resilience, independence, and ancestral traditions.

Its inhabitants value the ability to overcome difficulties and preserve the knowledge of earlier generations.

However, Midbarium is not a hostile, primitive, or miserable settlement.

Its rugged appearance does not reflect aggression toward outsiders.

Modern Midbarium is a peaceful and welcoming community whose inhabitants are proud of both their ancestral traditions and their membership in Wonder Kingdom.

The exact balance between warrior traditions, ancestral culture, and wilderness-oriented lifestyles remains open for further development.

### 5.4 Fairy Forest — The Magical Woodland

Fairy Forest is a magical woodland settlement surrounded by ancient trees, enchanted vegetation, supernatural creatures, and environments rich in magical energy.

It is historically associated with the Magicians, one of the four founding peoples.

Its general atmosphere is inspired by the magical woodland identity of MapleStory's Ellinia.

#### Environment and Culture

The inhabitants of Fairy Forest maintain a close relationship with magic and nature.

Magical knowledge, preservation of natural environments, and understanding supernatural phenomena are important aspects of the region's cultural identity.

The settlement exists harmoniously within its forest environment.

Its architecture and inhabited spaces are closely integrated with the surrounding woodland.

#### The Children of the Tree

Fifty years ago, explorers discovered a mysterious group of magical beings living inside the upper regions of the World Tree.

These beings became known as the **Children of the Tree**.

Following contact with the expedition, the Children gradually migrated into Fairy Forest.

They became part of its community and developed relationships with the kingdom's other inhabitants.

Their presence contributes to Fairy Forest's distinctive magical identity.

The precise nature, appearance, abilities, and origins of the Children of the Tree remain to be developed.

### 5.5 Urbanograd — The Town of Societal Rebels

Urbanograd is a distinctive urban settlement founded **100 years before the game's present**.

Unlike the original four peoples, Urbanograd's identity was not established during the ancient conflicts preceding Wonder Kingdom.

Instead, it emerged within the already united kingdom.

Its founders were people who preferred unconventional lifestyles, greater personal expression, and independence from traditional social expectations.

Over the following century, Urbanograd developed a distinctive counterculture.

Its general atmosphere is inspired by MapleStory's Kerning City.

#### Cultural Identity

Urbanograd is known as the home of **societal rebels**.

Its inhabitants value individuality, creativity, unconventional thinking, freedom of expression, and the ability to live differently from established social norms.

The town attracts people who enjoy alternative lifestyles and unusual forms of art, culture, and personal expression.

However, its inhabitants are not political insurgents.

They do not seek to destabilize Wonder Kingdom, overthrow its institutions, or create conflict with neighboring settlements.

Their rebellious identity is primarily cultural and personal.

#### Relationship with Wonder Kingdom

Urbanograd is fully integrated into Wonder Kingdom.

Its residents respect the kingdom and maintain positive relationships with other communities.

The rest of Veligor accepts Urbanograd's unconventional character.

Its people are understood rather than distrusted.

Urbanograd demonstrates that the kingdom's founding principles extend beyond coexistence between the original four peoples.

Wonder Kingdom also accepts communities that develop entirely new cultural identities.

### 5.6 Bain Harbour — The Coastal Town

Bain Harbour is a prosperous harbour settlement located on one side of Veligor.

Its cultural atmosphere resembles aspects of both Selsior and Kaldemor.

It combines elements of conventional urban life, cheerful craftsmanship, commercial activity, and coastal living.

#### Environment and Identity

Bain Harbour contains docks, coastal buildings, markets, maritime workshops, and facilities supporting travel and trade.

Its architectural and cultural characteristics reflect a combination of metropolitan and pastoral influences.

The town is welcoming, prosperous, and socially lively.

#### Independence

Bain Harbour is **not the designated harbour of Selsior or Kaldemor**.

It is a separate settlement with its own history, community, and identity.

Its similarities to those towns are cultural and atmospheric rather than administrative.

Bain Harbour belongs to Wonder Kingdom on equal terms with its other settlements.

### 5.7 Goa Harbour — The Wilderness Harbour

Goa Harbour is a coastal settlement located on the opposite side of Veligor from Bain Harbour.

Its cultural identity resembles aspects of Midbarium and Fairy Forest.

It combines rugged highland influences with magical woodland traditions and maritime life.

#### Environment and Identity

Goa Harbour has a more rustic and nature-oriented atmosphere than Bain Harbour.

Its architecture and surrounding environment reflect the influence of mountain landscapes, forests, and coastal terrain.

Its inhabitants maintain maritime traditions while also reflecting the independent and magical characteristics associated with nearby regional cultures.

The settlement is adventurous, welcoming, and distinct from the island's other towns.

#### Independence

Goa Harbour is **not the designated harbour of Midbarium or Fairy Forest**.

It has its own identity, inhabitants, traditions, and history.

Its resemblance to those settlements is cultural rather than political or administrative.

Bain Harbour and Goa Harbour are not rivals.

Both are peaceful and prosperous parts of Wonder Kingdom.

### 5.8 Cavelor — The Underground City of Guardians

Cavelor is a small, ancient underground city located deep beneath the World Tree.

It was established following the original battle against the Balrog.

After the creature was imprisoned, some of the strongest warriors and guardians of that era remained beneath the tree to protect the magical seal.

These individuals became the founders of Cavelor.

#### Origins

The original guardians understood that defeating the Balrog did not eliminate the threat permanently.

They chose to remain near its prison, maintaining vigilance over the ancient seal.

Over generations, their descendants established a permanent underground society.

They developed homes, infrastructure, traditions, and institutions suited to life beneath Veligor.

#### Cultural Identity

Cavelor's cultural identity centers on responsibility, vigilance, discipline, endurance, and the preservation of historical knowledge.

Its inhabitants maintain traditions inherited from the original guardians.

They preserve records and knowledge concerning the Balrog, the ancient battle, and the magical protections surrounding its prison.

Despite its serious historical responsibility, Cavelor is not an inherently unhappy or oppressive settlement.

Its inhabitants have developed their own functioning community and way of life.

#### Rediscovery

For generations, Cavelor remained largely isolated from the rest of Wonder Kingdom.

Fifty years ago, the Great World Tree Expedition reached its underground chambers and established contact with its inhabitants.

This rediscovery reconnected Cavelor with the surface civilization.

The settlement remains culturally distinctive while belonging to the same united kingdom.

## 6. The World Tree

At the center of Veligor stands the enormous World Tree.

The tree is one of the island's most important geographical, historical, and magical landmarks.

Its extraordinary size and internal structure distinguish it from ordinary vegetation.

The World Tree contains vast internal environments that can be explored both upward and downward.

### The Upper World Tree

The upper regions contain expansive chambers, ancient branches, magical vegetation, and unusual ecosystems.

These regions are home to extraordinary magical creatures and environments.

Historically, the Children of the Tree inhabited this part of the tree.

Their civilization remained largely unknown to the surface population until the expedition fifty years ago.

Following their discovery, many Children migrated to Fairy Forest.

### The Lower World Tree

Beneath the surface, the World Tree's roots extend deep underground.

These roots form passages through ancient caverns and subterranean environments.

The underground settlement of Cavelor exists within this region.

Deeper still lies the Balrog's prison.

### The Ancient Seal

The Balrog was imprisoned beneath the World Tree following its defeat by the four united peoples.

The seal was established using powerful magic and the combined efforts of the original alliance.

For generations, it successfully prevented the creature from escaping.

The World Tree therefore serves two contrasting purposes.

It is a source of natural beauty, magical wonder, and extraordinary life.

At the same time, its deepest roots contain the greatest ancient threat to Wonder Kingdom.

---

## 7. The Great World Tree Expedition

Fifty years before the game's present, an expedition was organized to explore the interior of the World Tree.

Although the tree had been a significant landmark for centuries, much of its internal structure remained unexplored by the wider kingdom.

The expedition became one of the most important exploratory undertakings in Wonder Kingdom's history.

### Discovery of the Upper Chambers

The explorers initially ascended through the tree's enormous internal passages.

They discovered remarkable magical environments containing unusual vegetation, extraordinary creatures, and complex natural structures.

Within these regions, they encountered the Children of the Tree.

This discovery established contact between Wonder Kingdom and a previously isolated magical community.

Following the expedition, the Children gradually migrated to Fairy Forest, where they became part of the kingdom's wider society.

### Discovery of Cavelor

After exploring the upper chambers, the expedition descended into the underground regions beneath the tree.

There, the explorers discovered Cavelor.

They learned that descendants of ancient guardians had inhabited the underground city for generations.

These guardians had preserved knowledge of the Balrog and maintained responsibility for protecting its prison.

The discovery revealed that the legendary battle against the Balrog had left behind a living institution dedicated to preventing its return.

### The Accident

During the expedition's exploration of the deeper underground passages, the explorers accidentally opened a rift.

This rift damaged the ancient magical protections imprisoning the Balrog.

The seal was not immediately destroyed.

Instead, its strength began deteriorating gradually.

At first, the full consequences of the accident were not apparent.

Over the following decades, however, the weakening seal began affecting the world above.

---

## 8. The Age of Disturbance

The fifty years following the expedition are provisionally known as **the Age of Disturbance**.

During this period, the Balrog's influence began spreading beyond its prison through the damaged magical seal.

The effects became increasingly noticeable throughout Veligor.

The most significant consequence was the transformation of the island's wilderness into a considerably more dangerous environment.

### The Growing Aggression of Wildlife

Wild creatures became increasingly aggressive.

Animals and other creatures that previously avoided travelers or behaved relatively peacefully began attacking people who entered their territories.

Monsters became common throughout forests, plains, mountains, caves, and other wilderness regions.

The exact effects varied by location.

Some areas experienced relatively minor disturbances, while others became home to extremely dangerous creatures.

The kingdom's inhabited settlements remained peaceful, but journeys through the surrounding wilderness became more hazardous.

### The Nature of Monsters

The monsters encountered throughout the game are connected to the weakening Balrog seal.

The spread of the Balrog's influence explains why Veligor contains so many aggressive creatures despite the peaceful nature of its civilization.

However, not every monster must be directly controlled by the Balrog.

Some may be ordinary creatures whose behavior has been altered by its influence.

Others may be magical beings affected by the changing environment.

The possibility of entirely new creatures appearing as a result of the disturbance remains open.

The precise mechanism is not yet established.

### The Effect on the Kingdom

The increasing danger of the wilderness has affected travel, exploration, agriculture, and activity outside established settlements.

Nevertheless, Wonder Kingdom continues to function successfully.

Its settlements remain prosperous and welcoming.

Its people cooperate in responding to the growing danger.

The kingdom does not collapse into fear, hostility, or political division.

Instead, the monster threat becomes a common challenge faced by an already united civilization.

---

## 9. Wonder Kingdom in the Present Day

Fifty years have passed since the Great World Tree Expedition.

Wonder Kingdom remains peaceful, prosperous, and culturally diverse.

Selsior continues to flourish as the bustling capital.

Kaldemor maintains its cheerful rural traditions and craftsmanship.

Midbarium preserves its resilient highland culture.

Fairy Forest remains a center of magical life and natural harmony.

Urbanograd, now a century old, continues to express its distinctive countercultural identity.

Bain Harbour and Goa Harbour thrive as independent maritime communities with different regional influences.

Cavelor continues its ancient responsibility beneath the World Tree.

### Everyday Life

The kingdom's inhabitants enjoy normal lives despite the dangers outside their settlements.

They engage in commerce, agriculture, craftsmanship, education, recreation, exploration, and cultural activities.

Different communities maintain close relationships.

Travel, trade, friendships, and cultural exchange connect the island.

People are proud to belong to Wonder Kingdom.

Their happiness and cooperation are genuine rather than an illusion concealing widespread societal problems.

### The Dangerous Wilderness

Beyond the settlements, the situation is different.

Monsters inhabit much of Veligor's wilderness.

Forests, mountains, plains, caves, and ancient ruins have become increasingly dangerous.

Travelers and adventurers must contend with aggressive creatures and hazardous environments.

The contrast between peaceful civilization and dangerous wilderness is a defining characteristic of the game's setting.

### The Forgotten Threat

Most ordinary inhabitants regard the Balrog as a legendary or historical figure.

The creature was defeated and imprisoned so long ago that its return is not considered an immediate concern by much of the population.

The people of Cavelor, however, preserve a deeper understanding of the original battle and the ancient seal.

They recognize that the prison's deterioration remains a serious danger.

Deep beneath the World Tree, the Balrog's influence continues to expand.

## 10. The Player's Place in the World

The player character is a native inhabitant of Wonder Kingdom.

They were born and raised within its civilization and naturally belong to its society.

They are not an outsider arriving from an unknown land.

They are not someone who must earn acceptance into the kingdom.

Wonder Kingdom is their home.

The player begins their journey as a member of the peaceful generation that inherited the achievements of the kingdom's founders.

### Adventure and Exploration

Throughout their journey, the player explores Veligor's settlements, wilderness regions, caves, forests, mountains, and ancient locations.

They encounter monsters affected by the Balrog's weakening seal.

They meet inhabitants of different communities, learn about their traditions, and become familiar with the kingdom's history.

The player can participate in local stories and activities that have no direct connection to the Balrog.

Wonder Kingdom is a functioning world with its own everyday concerns, interests, achievements, and adventures.

The central threat exists within this larger setting rather than defining every aspect of it.

### Discovering the Past

As the player progresses, they gradually encounter deeper historical knowledge.

The founding of Wonder Kingdom, the ancient battle, the World Tree, the Great Expedition, and the guardians of Cavelor provide the foundation for the larger narrative.

The player eventually discovers the significance of the damaged seal and the growing influence of the Balrog.

The exact story progression, quest structure, and player's ultimate role in confronting the ancient threat remain to be defined.

---

## 11. The Central Narrative

The history of Wonder Kingdom is fundamentally a story about unity, peace, and the responsibility of later generations to preserve what their ancestors achieved.

The first generation faced the Balrog while divided by generations of conflict.

Only by overcoming their differences could they defeat the creature.

Their victory resulted in the founding of Wonder Kingdom.

Over the centuries, the kingdom became the peaceful society its founders had envisioned.

The current generation does not face the same social divisions.

Its inhabitants already understand one another.

Their communities are genuinely united.

They have inherited a civilization in which different traditions coexist naturally and peacefully.

However, they also inherited the ancient responsibility of keeping the Balrog imprisoned.

The accidental damage caused by the Great World Tree Expedition has gradually weakened the protections established by the founders.

The monster threat spreading through Veligor's wilderness is an early consequence of the Balrog's growing influence.

The central conflict is therefore not about rebuilding a divided society.

It is about **protecting an already successful and peaceful civilization from an ancient danger that has begun returning**.

Wonder Kingdom is a world worth protecting because it is genuinely good, not because its inhabitants must first repair a broken society.

## 12. Established Worldbuilding Principles

These principles govern future lore development.

1. **Wonder Kingdom is genuinely united.** Its different communities are not secretly hostile toward one another.
2. **The four original peoples were historically divided.** Their conflict ended through cooperation against the Balrog and the subsequent founding treaty.
3. **Cultural differences remain important.** Unification did not erase the distinct traditions or identities of Veligor's communities.
4. **Urbanograd was founded 100 years ago.** Its inhabitants are societal rebels in a cultural sense, not political insurgents.
5. **Bain Harbour and Goa Harbour are independent towns.** Their resemblance to other settlements is cultural and atmospheric, not an indication of administrative dependence.
6. **Bain Harbour and Goa Harbour are on opposite sides of Veligor.** They have different identities without being rivals.
7. **Selsior is the City of Everything.** It is a joyful, bustling, cosmopolitan metropolis with classic medieval fantasy architecture.
8. **Kaldemor is the Town of Joyful Craftsmanship.** It is a cheerful, prosperous, pastoral community inspired by Henesys.
9. **Midbarium is a rugged highland settlement.** Its identity is associated with strength, resilience, independence, and ancestral traditions.
10. **Fairy Forest is a magical woodland settlement.** It is associated with the Magicians and became the new home of the Children of the Tree.
11. **Cavelor was established by ancient guardians.** Its inhabitants preserve the responsibility of watching over the Balrog's prison.
12. **The Great World Tree Expedition occurred 50 years ago.** It discovered the Children of the Tree and Cavelor but accidentally damaged the Balrog's seal.
13. **The Balrog's weakening seal makes the wilderness increasingly dangerous.** This is the central lore explanation for the aggressive monsters encountered throughout Veligor.
14. **The kingdom remains peaceful despite the monster threat.** The danger affects the land and wilderness rather than causing widespread hostility between its peoples.
15. **The player belongs to Wonder Kingdom from the beginning.** The player character was born and raised within its society.
16. **The Balrog is the overarching ancient threat.** Its exact nature, intentions, and eventual fate have not yet been fully established.
17. **The world is predominantly bright, colorful, prosperous, and adventurous.** Dark environments and dangerous creatures exist, but they do not define the everyday atmosphere of the entire kingdom.
18. **Not every local story must concern the Balrog.** The world contains ordinary lives, personal adventures, regional traditions, and independent mysteries.
19. **Veligor is the initial setting, not necessarily the entire world.** Other islands and kingdoms may be introduced in the future.
20. **Undecided details must remain undecided.** Future lore development should distinguish established facts from proposals rather than silently introducing new canon.
